## 9. Quora (en)

Questions people ask (common phrasings in search results):
- "If I had invested $100 a month in Nvidia 10 years ago, how much would I have now?"
- "Is it too late to invest in Nvidia?"

**Answer (≈170 words):**
I ran this with real monthly closing prices (split-adjusted, in USD, no dividends or fees), using $10 a day — about $300 a month.

- 10 years: $36,680 invested → about $620,000 today (+1,590%)
- 5 years: $18,420 invested → about $67,600 (+267%)
- Last 12 months: $3,810 invested → about $3,190 (−16%)

For context, the same daily amount over 10 years in AMD returned +1,286%, Microsoft +214% and the S&P 500 index +120%.

Two caveats matter more than the headline number. First, the ride was brutal: on monthly prices Nvidia fell about 63% in 2021–22, 52% in 2018 and 79% in 2008. Many people sell at exactly those moments. Second, the last year shows that even a great decade does not protect a new buyer — a year of steady buying is currently down 16%.

So "is it too late?" has no honest answer; what you control is how much of your money depends on one company.
