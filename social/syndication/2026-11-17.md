## 6. Quora (en)

Questions people ask (common phrasings, matching the "If you'd invested in Ethereum 5 years ago…" articles ranking in search):
- "If I had invested in Ethereum every month for the last 5 years, how much would I have now?"
- "Is it still worth dollar-cost averaging into Ethereum?"

**Answer (≈175 words):**
I ran this with real monthly closing prices (USD, no fees), using $10 a day — about $300 a month — starting five years ago, in November 2021.

- $10 a day for 5 years: $18,260 invested → about $15,100 today (−17%)
- $1,000 at once five years ago: about $390 today (−61%)
- $10 a day for 8 years (from late 2018): $29,220 → about $88,900 (+204%)
- $10 a day for the last year: −17%

For context, the same five-year habit in Bitcoin and the S&P 500 is up about 49%, and gold about 59%.

So the honest answer: buying regularly did not make money in ether over five years — it only made the loss smaller than a lump sum at the top. The regular buyer's average price is about $2,190, so ETH (around $1,810 now) would need roughly +21% to break even. Whether it gets there is unknown; on monthly closes ether has fallen 90% and 74% before.
