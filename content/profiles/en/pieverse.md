**Pieverse** is a young crypto project on BNB Chain that builds payment rails for AI agents: programs that can pay for services by themselves, with every payment stamped and stored as an audit-ready receipt. **PIEVERSE** is its token, launched in November 2025. ## How it works

The internet has an old status code, "402 Payment Required", that was never really used. Coinbase's x402 standard revives it so that an app or AI agent can pay for a page, an API call or data automatically in stablecoins. Pieverse's version, x402b, adds two things for BNB Chain: a relayer network that pays the blockchain fee for the user and is reimbursed from their stablecoin balance, so no separate gas token is needed, and a compliance receipt for each payment, stored on BNB Greenfield. Its pieUSD is a wrapped form of USDT made to work with this gas-free method. The team also builds consumer apps such as Purr-Fect Claw, an AI assistant inside messengers like Line, Kakao and WhatsApp that needs no wallet or seed phrase.

## What the token is used for

According to the project, PIEVERSE is used for governance votes on upgrades and fees, for staking with rewards, for paying fees at a discount and for early access to new products. The payments themselves mostly run in stablecoins, so the token's value depends on how much the Pieverse ecosystem grows and how much of that activity actually needs PIEVERSE.

## Its story so far

Pieverse was founded in 2024 around the idea of "timestamping everything of value" and took part in Binance Labs' Most Valuable Builder programme. Co-founder and CEO Colin Ho previously worked as an engineer at Google and Uber. In October 2025 it raised $7 million in a round led by Animoca Brands and UOB Venture Management, bringing total funding to about $10 million. The token started trading in mid-November 2025 and was worth about $0.12 in its first days. In 2026 interest in AI-agent payments grew, and in November 2026 PIEVERSE more than doubled within a week to new records as the project promoted new agent products.

## Alternatives

Pieverse competes with other projects that combine crypto and AI agents, such as [Virtuals Protocol]({lang}/virtual/), [Kite]({lang}/kite/) (which uses Pieverse technology) and the [Artificial Superintelligence Alliance]({lang}/fet/). For simply paying with stablecoins, agents can also use Coinbase's original x402 on other networks without any project token.

## Main risks

- **Token unlocks:** most of the supply is not yet circulating; team and investor tokens unlock over time and can add heavy selling.
- **Concentration:** in early November 2026 a few dozen wallets were reported to hold almost all the supply, so a handful of holders can move the price.
- **Young project:** the products are new, some only in early access, and real usage is hard to measure.
- **Weak link to the token:** payments run in stablecoins, so growth of the network does not automatically mean demand for PIEVERSE.
- **Regulation and hype:** AI and payment tokens are popular themes; rules for payments and a change in fashion can both hit the price.

## What it means if you buy regularly

A token that has risen many times in one year carries the risk of a deep fall, and regular buying spreads that risk over time but does not remove it. With so little history, nobody can know how PIEVERSE behaves in a long bear market. If you buy at all, many people treat a token like this as a small, speculative part of their crypto holdings that they can afford to lose. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
