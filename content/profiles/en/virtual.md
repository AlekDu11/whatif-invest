**Virtuals Protocol** is a platform where anyone can create an AI agent — a chatbot character, a trading bot or a market-research assistant — and launch a token for it that people can buy and sell. Every agent token is traded against VIRTUAL, the platform's own token, so activity around agents creates demand for it. Most of it happens on Coinbase's Base network. ## How it works

A creator gives an agent a personality and skills and launches its token on Virtuals. The new token is paired with VIRTUAL, and every trade pays a small fee that is shared between the protocol and the agent's creator and treasury. Well-known agents such as Luna, a virtual pop-star character, and AIXBT, an account that posts crypto market analysis, helped make the platform famous. The Agent Commerce Protocol (ACP) lets agents find each other, agree on a task and pay for it, with the money held in escrow until the work is delivered.

## What VIRTUAL is used for

VIRTUAL is the base currency of the whole system: you need it to buy agent tokens on the platform, it is used for governance votes and staking, and it is the reserve asset in Virtuals' newer products. So the token mainly rises and falls with interest in on-chain AI agents. Because much of the fee income has come from trading agent tokens, the team said in February 2026 that revenue tied to trading is "cyclical and vulnerable" and that it wants agents to earn more from paid services.

## Its story so far

The team behind Virtuals started in 2021 as PathDAO, a blockchain gaming guild, and later moved to AI characters for games and apps. The agent launchpad on Base took off in late 2024, during a wave of excitement about AI agents in crypto, and VIRTUAL set its record at the start of January 2025. When the AI-agent hype cooled during 2025, the price fell most of the way back. Since then the platform has added more networks, including Solana, a revenue network for agent-to-agent commerce (12 February 2026), the Occupy launchpad (September 2026) and the Butler app for automated trading.

## Alternatives

Virtuals competes for the "AI crypto" theme with platforms where agents are built or traded, such as the ElizaOS ecosystem, and with AI networks like [Bittensor]({lang}/tao/) and [Artificial Superintelligence Alliance]({lang}/fet/) that focus on AI computing rather than agent tokens. On Solana, memecoin launchpads such as pump.fun compete for the same traders.

## Main risks

- **Hype cycles:** demand for agent tokens depends on a trend that can fade quickly, as it did in 2025.
- **Revenue quality:** much of the activity comes from token trading and incentives; the team itself said profitability was delayed.
- **Agent tokens:** most agent tokens are tiny and very risky; many lose almost all their value.
- **Technology and bridges:** smart contracts and cross-chain bridges can be hacked.
- **Regulation:** rules on AI and on tokens tied to products or people are still being written.

## What it means if you buy regularly

VIRTUAL is a young token tied to one fashionable theme, and its price history so far has been dominated by one big boom and a long fall. Regular buying spreads your entry points over time, but if interest in AI agents stays low, it cannot prevent losses. Many people keep such a token to a small part of their crypto holdings. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
