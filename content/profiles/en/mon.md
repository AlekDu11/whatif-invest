**Monad** is a layer-1 blockchain that runs the same apps and smart contracts as Ethereum but aims to be far faster and cheaper, handling thousands of transactions per second. It is built by Monad Labs, founded in 2022 by former Jump Trading engineers Keone Hon and James Hunsaker together with Eunice Giarta, and its coin MON pays fees and secures the network. ## How it works

Most blockchains process transactions one after another. Monad runs many of them at the same time ("parallel execution") and only re-checks the ones that touch the same data, which lets it target about 10,000 transactions per second with blocks under a second. It uses its own consensus system, MonadBFT, and a custom database built for speed. Because it is fully compatible with the Ethereum Virtual Machine, developers can move their Ethereum apps and wallets to Monad with little or no change.

## What the token is used for

MON is the network's fuel: every transaction pays a fee in MON. Validators stake MON to produce blocks and earn rewards, and holders can delegate their coins to validators. The bet on MON is that a fast, cheap chain that "speaks Ethereum" attracts enough trading, games and payments apps to create real demand for block space.

## Its story so far

Monad Labs was founded in 2022 and in April 2024 raised $225 million in a round led by the venture firm Paradigm, one of the largest crypto raises of that year. After a long test period, the team held the first token sale on Coinbase's new token sale platform in November 2025, raising about $269 million. The mainnet went live on 24 November 2025, MON started trading on major exchanges the same day, and users of the test network received an airdrop. The token set its record two days later, in November 2025.

## Alternatives

Monad competes with other fast smart-contract chains. [Solana]({lang}/sol/) is the biggest high-speed network but does not run Ethereum apps directly. [Sei]({lang}/sei/) also offers a parallel, Ethereum-compatible chain, and [Sui]({lang}/sui/) and [Aptos]({lang}/apt/) promise similar speed with a different programming language. Many apps stay on [Ethereum]({lang}/eth/) itself or on its cheaper layer-2 networks such as [Arbitrum]({lang}/arb/).

## Main risks

- **Big swings:** MON is a young token with a short price history; large drops are common for new coins.
- **Unlocks:** about half of the supply was locked at launch; as team, investor and ecosystem tokens unlock, selling pressure can grow.
- **Competition:** many chains fight for the same developers and users, and speed alone may not be enough.
- **Young technology:** a new network can face bugs, outages or security problems.
- **Regulation:** rules for tokens differ by country and can change quickly.

## What it means if you buy regularly

Monad has traded only since late 2025, so its price history is short and says little about the long run. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads your entry price over time and can soften sharp swings, but it does not remove the risk that a young project fails to win users. Many people keep such tokens to a small share of their savings they can afford to see fall sharply. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
