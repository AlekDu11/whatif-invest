**Aster** is a decentralised exchange for perpetual futures — leveraged bets on a price with no expiry date — that started on BNB Chain and is backed by YZi Labs, the former Binance Labs. Its token became one of 2025's most talked-about launches after Binance co-founder Changpeng "CZ" Zhao praised it. ASTER is worth {{mcap:aster}} today ({{rank:aster}} among cryptocurrencies).

> - ASTER launched on 17 September 2025; 53.5% of the 8 billion maximum supply is set aside for airdrops, and 8.8% went to users on day one.
> - Its record high is {{ath:aster}} ({{athd:aster}}); today the price is {{fromath:aster}} from it.
> - Since 17 June 2026, 99% of daily trading fees buy ASTER back, and the same amount is burned from reserves until the total supply falls to 3 billion.

## How it works

Aster was formed when the yield project Astherus merged with APX Finance, an older perpetuals exchange, and rebranded on 31 March 2025. Traders connect a wallet, deposit collateral and open leveraged long or short positions: **Simple Mode** offers one-click trades, **Pro Mode** a full order book, across BNB Chain, Ethereum, Solana and Arbitrum. Besides crypto, it lists contracts that track US stocks. On 17 March 2026 it launched its own blockchain, **Aster Chain**, where orders are encrypted so others cannot see your positions.

## What the token is used for

ASTER is mainly used for staking: holders lock it as veASTER to earn rewards, and the roadmap also ties governance votes to it. The main argument for its value is the link to trading revenue: from December 2025 up to 80% of daily fees went to buybacks, and since June 2026 99%, paid out to stakers. Each buyback is matched by a burn from reserves, starting with the team's share.

## Its story so far

ASTER began trading on 17 September 2025 at a valuation of about $560 million for the whole supply. After CZ's posts it rose more than tenfold within days, and in late September Aster briefly led all perpetual exchanges tracked by DefiLlama in daily fees and volume. On 5 October 2025 DefiLlama's founder said Aster's volumes moved almost exactly with Binance's and delisted them; the token fell about 10% that day. The data returned in mid-October, still unverified. In March 2026 the team replaced monthly unlocks of about 78 million tokens with staking rewards of roughly 2 million a month, and pushed the team's unlock back to September 2027.

## Alternatives

The main on-chain rival is [Hyperliquid]({lang}/hype/); others include [Lighter]({lang}/lit/), [dYdX]({lang}/dydx/), [GMX]({lang}/gmx/) and [ApeX]({lang}/apex/). For many traders the real alternative is still a centralised exchange such as Binance, whose coin is [BNB]({lang}/bnb/).

## Main risks

- **Unlocks and concentration:** most of the supply still sits in airdrop, ecosystem, treasury and team pools controlled by the project.
- **Leverage and smart contracts:** leveraged positions can be liquidated in seconds, and a bug in the contracts or the new chain could cost users money.
- **Volume doubts:** the 2025 DefiLlama delisting showed that reported volumes are not independently verified.
- **Reliance on Binance figures:** much of the interest came from CZ and YZi Labs; if that support fades, demand may too.
- **Regulation:** many countries restrict leveraged crypto futures, and rules for decentralised perps are still being written.
