**Official Trump** is a memecoin on the Solana blockchain that Donald Trump announced on his social networks on 17 January 2025, three days before his second inauguration. ## How it works

TRUMP is a standard token on Solana, created by CIC Digital LLC, an affiliate of the Trump Organization, and Fight Fight Fight LLC, the company that runs the project. Like other Solana tokens it can be sent in seconds and traded on decentralised exchanges and on big exchanges such as Binance. The issuers earn money from trading fees in the main liquidity pools and from the tokens they hold. Their tokens are locked and released on a schedule: the first unlock came on 18 April 2025, and after that new tokens are released every day, which adds steady selling pressure.

## What the token is used for

TRUMP has no technical role in a network. It is held as a political collectible, traded for short-term price moves and used in promotions run by the issuers — the best known was a private dinner in May 2025 at Trump's golf club in Virginia for the 220 biggest holders. Some exchanges and wallets let people stake or lend it, but those rewards come from those platforms, not from the token itself. Its value comes entirely from what the next buyer is willing to pay.

## Its story so far

The coin went on sale late on 17 January 2025, and within two days its price jumped from a few dollars to above $70, putting it briefly among the largest cryptocurrencies. On 19 January Melania Trump launched her own MELANIA coin, and TRUMP began a long fall as early buyers took profits. In April 2025 the issuers announced the holders' dinner, which lifted the price for a while, just before the first locked tokens were released. Since then the price has mostly followed the wider memecoin market and the regular unlocks, staying far below its January 2025 peak.

## Alternatives

TRUMP competes for the same money as other large memecoins: [Dogecoin]({lang}/doge/), [Shiba Inu]({lang}/shib/), [Pepe]({lang}/pepe/) and the Solana memecoins [Bonk]({lang}/bonk/) and [dogwifhat]({lang}/wif/). People who want exposure to the network it runs on often buy [Solana]({lang}/sol/) instead.

## Main risks

- **Unlocks:** most of the supply belongs to the issuers and is released over time, which can push the price down.
- **No fundamentals:** the price rests on attention and political news, which can change quickly.
- **Concentration:** a few large wallets hold most of the tokens, and their sales move the market.
- **Huge swings:** falls of 80–90% from a peak are common for memecoins, and TRUMP has already had one.
- **Regulation and politics:** investigations, new laws or a change in political mood can hit the coin directly.

## What it means if you buy regularly

TRUMP's history is short and dominated by a launch spike, so a regular buyer who started after January 2025 bought at much lower prices than the first buyers — but that helps only if the price recovers. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads timing risk but does not remove the risk of large losses. Many people treat memecoins as a very small, speculative part of their savings — money they can afford to lose. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
