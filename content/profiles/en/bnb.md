**BNB** is the coin of Binance, the world's largest crypto exchange, and of BNB Chain, the blockchain built around it. People use it to pay lower trading fees on Binance and to pay for transactions on BNB Chain. BNB is worth {{mcap:bnb}} today ({{rank:bnb}} among cryptocurrencies).

## How it works

BNB lives on BNB Smart Chain, a network similar to Ethereum that runs the same kind of smart contracts but with cheaper, faster transactions. Only a small group of validators — chosen by how much BNB is staked with them — confirm blocks, which keeps fees low but makes the network less decentralised than Ethereum. Every quarter the BNB Foundation burns coins using an "Auto-Burn" formula based on BNB's price and the number of blocks produced; part of every gas fee is burned too. Burning will continue until the total supply falls to 100 million.

## Why people think it has value

BNB is useful in three ways. On Binance it cuts trading fees and gives access to token sales and "Launchpool" rewards for new coins. On BNB Chain it pays the gas fee for every transaction, from trading memecoins to using lending apps. And holders can stake it. Because coins are burned every quarter, the supply shrinks over time — as long as Binance keeps the programme going.

## Its story so far

Binance sold BNB in a July 2017 coin offering for about $0.15. It first ran on Ethereum, moved to Binance's own chain in 2019, and BNB Smart Chain launched in September 2020. During the 2021 boom BNB rose to about $690 as BNB Chain filled with cheap DeFi apps. In October 2022 a hacker exploited a bridge and created about 2 million BNB, and the chain was paused for hours. In November 2023 Binance agreed to pay $4.3 billion to settle US charges and CZ pleaded guilty to anti-money-laundering violations and stepped down; he served four months in prison in 2024 and was pardoned by the US president in October 2025. That same month BNB set its record above $1,369 before falling back in 2026.

## Alternatives

Exchanges have their own coins too — [OKB]({lang}/okb/) of OKX, [Cronos]({lang}/cro/) of Crypto.com and [KuCoin's KCS]({lang}/kcs/). As a smart-contract platform, BNB Chain competes with [Ethereum]({lang}/eth/), [Solana]({lang}/sol/) and [TRON]({lang}/trx/).

## Main risks

- **One company:** BNB depends on Binance's business, reputation and decisions; trouble at the exchange would hit the coin directly.
- **Regulation:** Binance has paid billions in fines and is restricted in several countries; new actions are possible.
- **Centralisation:** a small number of validators run BNB Chain, which makes it easier to pause or control.
- **Hacks:** bridges and apps on BNB Chain have been hacked, including the 2022 exploit.
- **Big swings:** BNB fell about 70% between 2021 and 2022 and lost more than half its value again from the 2025 record.

## What it means if you buy regularly

Buying a fixed amount of BNB regularly smooths out the price you pay, but the bet stays concentrated on one company and its legal situation. Many people keep crypto to a small share of savings they can afford to see fall sharply. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
