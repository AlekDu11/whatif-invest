**Shiba Inu (SHIB)** is a meme token named after the Japanese dog breed that also inspired Dogecoin. It was created in August 2020 by an anonymous founder called "Ryoshi" as a joke "Dogecoin killer", grew into one of the most traded coins of the 2021 boom and now has its own small ecosystem of apps and networks. In November 2026 all SHIB in circulation was worth about $2.1 billion, around number 39 among cryptocurrencies.

> **Key takeaways**
> - SHIB started with 1 quadrillion (1,000 trillion) tokens; about 589 trillion are in circulation.
> - In May 2021 Ethereum co-founder Vitalik Buterin burned about 410 trillion SHIB he had been sent — roughly 41% of all tokens.
> - The record high came in October 2021; in November 2026 SHIB traded about 96% below it.
> - Since 2023 SHIB has its own layer-2 network, Shibarium, where fees are paid in the BONE token.

## How it works

SHIB is an ERC-20 token on the [Ethereum]({lang}/eth/) blockchain, so it is stored in the same wallets and moved with the same technology as other Ethereum tokens. Because the supply is so large, one SHIB costs a tiny fraction of a cent. Around it the community built ShibaSwap, a decentralized exchange (2021), and Shibarium, a faster and cheaper layer-2 network linked to Ethereum (2023). On Shibarium, transaction fees are paid in [BONE]({lang}/bone/), and part of the fees is used to burn SHIB — send it to addresses nobody can spend from — which slowly reduces the supply.

## What the token is used for

SHIB has little use beyond its ecosystem: it can be traded, staked or provided as liquidity on ShibaSwap and used in Shibarium apps. Its value comes mainly from its community, its brand and its listing on almost every big exchange, which makes it easy to buy. Price moves are driven far more by crypto sentiment and hype around meme coins than by network usage.

## Its story so far

Ryoshi sent half of all SHIB to Vitalik Buterin's wallet. In May 2021 he burned about 90% of what he received and gave the rest to charity, including a COVID-19 relief fund in India. Listings on big exchanges followed, and in October 2021 the price jumped to its record — early buyers turned tiny sums into fortunes, which made SHIB famous. It then lost most of its value in the 2022 crash. Shibarium launched in August 2023 after a rocky start, and in September 2025 attackers took about $2.4 million from the Shibarium bridge.

## Alternatives

SHIB's closest rival is [Dogecoin]({lang}/doge/), the original dog coin, which is several times larger. Other big meme tokens include [Pepe]({lang}/pepe/) on Ethereum, [Bonk]({lang}/bonk/) on Solana and [FLOKI]({lang}/floki/). Unlike SHIB, most of them have no network of their own.

## Main risks

- **Big swings:** SHIB has fallen about 96% from its record; meme coins can halve in weeks.
- **Hype-driven demand:** the price depends on attention and trends, not on profits or cash flow.
- **Concentration:** a few large wallets, including exchange wallets, hold a large share of the supply.
- **Huge supply:** burns are small compared with hundreds of trillions of tokens, so they barely change scarcity.
- **Technology and security:** bridges and new networks such as Shibarium can be hacked; regulation of crypto can also change.

## What it means if you buy regularly

A fixed regular amount buys more SHIB when it is cheap and less when it is expensive, so you do not have to guess the top or bottom. But regular buying cannot turn a speculative meme token into a safe one: a long fall would still mean losses. Many people keep meme coins to a very small share of their savings. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
