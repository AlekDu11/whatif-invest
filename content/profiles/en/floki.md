**FLOKI** is a memecoin that appeared in June 2021, named after the Shiba Inu puppy Elon Musk said he was getting. Unlike many dog coins, its community tries to build real products around it — a blockchain game, a crypto education site and tools for other token projects. ## How it works

FLOKI is not its own blockchain but a token on two existing networks, Ethereum and BNB Chain, with a bridge to move it between them. There are trillions of tokens, so one FLOKI costs a tiny fraction of a cent. Decisions about the treasury and projects are put to votes of the Floki DAO, where token holders take part. The core team stays largely anonymous and works with a large volunteer community.

## Why people think it has value

Most of FLOKI's value comes from attention: a recognisable mascot, an active community and the hope of the next memecoin rally. Its backers argue that the token is also useful — it is used inside the Valhalla game and its NFT world, for staking, for governance votes and in tools such as FlokiFi Locker, which lets projects lock their tokens to show they will not dump them. A sister project, TokenFi, launched in 2023 for creating tokens. Whether these products bring lasting demand is still unclear.

## Its story so far

FLOKI launched in June 2021, at the height of the dog-coin craze that followed Dogecoin and Shiba Inu. In late 2021 it ran big advertising campaigns, including on the London Underground; a UK advertising watchdog later ruled those ads irresponsible because they did not warn about the risks. Like most memecoins it fell sharply in 2022. Binance listed FLOKI in May 2023, which brought it many new buyers, and in the 2024 memecoin wave it set its record in June 2024. In 2025 the Floki DAO approved using treasury tokens to support the new European ETP from Valour.

## Alternatives

The best-known dog coins are [Dogecoin]({lang}/doge/), the original from 2013, and [Shiba Inu]({lang}/shib/), which, like FLOKI, built its own ecosystem. Newer memecoins such as [Pepe]({lang}/pepe/) and [Bonk]({lang}/bonk/) compete for the same buyers. People who want blockchain games for their own sake may look at gaming tokens instead.

## Main risks

- **Big swings:** memecoins can lose 80–90% in months; FLOKI has done so before.
- **Hype-driven price:** its value depends on attention and social media, which can disappear quickly.
- **Concentration:** large wallets and the project's treasury hold big shares; their moves can shake the price.
- **Products and team:** an anonymous team and products with modest use mean there is little to fall back on if interest fades.
- **Regulation and technology:** marketing rules for crypto are tightening, and bridges between chains have been hacked in the past.

## What it means if you buy regularly

FLOKI's short history consists of a few sharp spikes and long falls. Buying a fixed amount regularly avoids putting everything in at a peak, but for a memecoin it cannot remove the risk of losing most of the money. Many people treat such coins as a small, speculative part of their crypto at most. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
