**Beldex (BDX)** is a privacy-focused cryptocurrency: payments on its blockchain hide the sender, the receiver and the amount. It launched in March 2018 on code forked from Monero and has since grown into a set of privacy apps — a messenger, a decentralised VPN and a browser — all running on the same network. ## How it works

Beldex started as a fork of the Monero codebase, so it inherits Monero-style privacy: ring signatures mix your payment with others, one-time "stealth" addresses hide the receiver, and the amount is concealed. It began with proof-of-work mining. With the "Bucephalus" hard fork in December 2021 it moved to proof of stake: the network is now secured by masternodes, servers whose owners lock 10,000 BDX as collateral. In return they earn block rewards, and groups of masternodes take turns producing and checking blocks. The same upgrade cut the block time from 120 seconds to 30 seconds. Part of every transaction fee is burned, which removes coins from supply.

## What BDX is used for

BDX pays for private transfers and is the stake that masternodes must lock. The masternodes also carry the project's apps: **BChat**, an end-to-end encrypted messenger relayed by masternodes; **BelNet**, a decentralised VPN that routes traffic through masternodes; the **Beldex Browser** with a built-in VPN and tracker blocking; and the **Beldex Name Service (BNS)**, which turns long addresses into readable names. A bridge lets BDX move to other networks as a wrapped token, for example on BNB Chain.

## Its story so far

Beldex launched in March 2018, after a token sale (ICO) announced that spring. The company behind it, Beldex International, gives the Seychelles as its base and Afanddy Bin Hushni as founder and chairman. In its early years BDX was a small mined Monero fork; in November 2019 it traded for a fraction of a cent. The December 2021 switch to proof of stake turned it into a masternode network, and in the years that followed the project shifted its focus from "private money" to a whole privacy ecosystem around BChat, BelNet and the browser. BDX also completed a 1:1 swap of its wrapped token on BNB Chain to a new contract. In September 2026 an analysis by Phemex counted about 9.94 billion BDX on the chain, of which about 11.8 million had been burned.

## Alternatives

The best-known privacy coin is [Monero]({lang}/xmr/), the project Beldex was forked from; it is much bigger and is still mined. [Zcash]({lang}/zec/) offers optional privacy using zero-knowledge proofs, and [Dash]({lang}/dash-token/) also uses masternodes but with weaker, optional mixing. Outside crypto, Beldex's apps compete with ordinary VPN services and with encrypted messengers that many people already use for free.

## Main risks

- **Delisting:** regulators push exchanges to drop privacy coins; several large exchanges have already removed coins like Monero, and BDX trades mainly on mid-sized exchanges such as Gate, MEXC and KuCoin.
- **Supply transparency:** total supply is about 9.9 billion BDX, but around 2 billion are not counted as circulating, and the project has not clearly explained who holds them.
- **Concentration:** the token allocation set aside large shares for ecosystem development (40%), seed investors and the team; decisions by a small group can move the price.
- **Small network:** a Coin Bureau review in February 2025 found only a small share of BDX staked in masternodes, which makes the network easier to attack than bigger chains.
- **Thin trading and lookalikes:** daily volume is small compared with market value, and similar tickers (such as BDXN) or unofficial wrapped tokens can confuse buyers.

## What it means if you buy regularly

BDX is a small, thinly traded coin whose price can swing sharply and whose access depends on a few exchanges. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads your purchases over time instead of trying to guess the bottom, but it does not remove the risk of losses — or of an exchange delisting the coin. Many people keep such small privacy coins to a tiny part of their savings that they can afford to lose. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
