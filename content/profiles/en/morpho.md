**Morpho** is a lending network on Ethereum and other blockchains: people deposit crypto or stablecoins to earn interest, and others borrow against their own crypto, with smart contracts instead of a bank in the middle. ## How it works

Morpho has two layers. **Markets** are small, fixed lending pools: each has one asset to lend, one asset as collateral, a price feed and a liquidation level, and once created its rules cannot be changed. If a borrower's collateral falls too far in value, it is sold to repay the loan. **Vaults** sit on top: a deposit goes to a professional "curator" who spreads it across several markets to earn interest within set risk limits. Apps like Coinbase, Crypto.com, Gemini and Bitpanda show these vaults and loans to their users without people touching the blockchain directly. In 2026 Morpho added a fixed-rate, fixed-term product called Midnight, where the market rather than a formula sets the rate.

## What the token is used for

MORPHO is a governance token: holders vote in the Morpho DAO on things like which chains are supported and how incentives are spent. It became transferable in November 2024. In 2026 the protocol itself did not take a share of the interest, so holders had no direct claim on fees; switching on such a fee would need a governance vote. Demand for the token therefore depends mostly on expectations about Morpho's growth and future decisions.

## Its story so far

Paul Frambot and three co-founders started Morpho in August 2021 and raised $18 million from a16z and Variant in June 2022. Its first product, Morpho Optimizer (June 2022), matched lenders and borrowers on top of Aave and Compound for better rates. In January 2024 it launched its own markets and vaults, and in August 2024 it raised $50 million led by Ribbit Capital. Coinbase began offering crypto-backed loans through Morpho in January 2025, the Ethereum Foundation put 2,400 ETH into Morpho vaults in October 2025, and Société Générale's crypto arm and Bitwise used it in early 2026. In April 2026 a French minister called Morpho a "unicorn", based on its token's market value of more than $1.5 billion.

## Alternatives

The largest rival is [Aave]({lang}/aave/), the biggest lending protocol, which uses large shared pools instead of isolated markets. Others include Compound, Spark from the [Sky]({lang}/sky/) ecosystem and Kamino on Solana. Centralized platforms such as Nexo also offer crypto-backed loans, but there a company holds the funds.

## Main risks

- **Big swings:** MORPHO is a young token; by November 2026 it had fallen by more than half from its January 2025 peak.
- **Vault and curator risk:** a curator can choose risky collateral or a faulty price feed, and depositors in that vault can lose money.
- **Smart-contract risk:** a bug or an attack on a market or an integration could cause losses, despite audits.
- **Token supply:** about 30% of the 1 billion MORPHO was not yet in circulation in November 2026, and early backers hold a large share.
- **No fee claim:** if the DAO never switches on fees, usage growth may not reach token holders; lending rules may also tighten.

## What it means if you buy regularly

MORPHO has traded for only a short time, so there is little history to judge regular buying by. A fixed amount each week or month spreads out the entry price, but with a young governance token the risk of a large loss stays high. Many people keep such tokens to a small part of their crypto. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
