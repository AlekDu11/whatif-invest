**Bonk** is a memecoin with a Shiba Inu dog logo on the Solana blockchain. It appeared on Christmas Day 2022, when Solana was at a low after the collapse of the FTX exchange, and half of all tokens were given away free to Solana users, artists and developers. ## How it works

BONK is a standard token on Solana, so it can be sent in seconds for a fraction of a cent and traded on any Solana exchange. There is no company that issues profits to holders. Instead, the community and the developers around the project build tools that use BONK: Telegram trading bots, NFT marketplaces, games and, since 2025, **BONKfun**, a site where anyone can launch their own memecoin in a few clicks. These tools charge fees, and part of them is used to buy BONK on the market and burn it, or is sent to holders and partners. The project is run by a loose group of contributors, not by a single founder.

## What the token is used for

BONK is mainly a community and trading token. People use it to tip, to pay inside Solana apps and games, and to trade. Some apps let users stake BONK for rewards or voting rights, and token burns approved by the community have removed trillions of tokens from circulation. Outside crypto, a Nasdaq-listed company, Bonk Inc. (BNKK), holds BONK as a treasury asset, and exchange-traded products in Europe give investors exposure through a regular broker. None of this changes the basic fact: BONK's value comes from demand, not from earnings.

## Its story so far

Bonk was created by anonymous Solana developers and released on 25 December 2022 as "a dog coin for the people". The free airdrop and the joke spirit made it popular at a time when many had given up on Solana, and its price jumped many times over in the first weeks. It was listed on Binance in December 2023 and rose with Solana to its peak in November 2024. In 2025 the BONKfun launchpad briefly overtook pump.fun as the busiest memecoin launch site on Solana, and in 2025–2026 BONK gained a listed treasury company and European ETPs. Like all memecoins, it also went through falls of more than 80%.

## Alternatives

Among dog-themed memecoins, [Dogecoin]({lang}/doge/) and [Shiba Inu]({lang}/shib/) are older and larger, while [dogwifhat]({lang}/wif/) is the other big dog coin on Solana. [Pepe]({lang}/pepe/) and [FLOKI]({lang}/floki/) are frequent rivals for the same buyers. People who want exposure to the Solana network itself often buy [Solana]({lang}/sol/) instead.

## Main risks

- **No fundamentals:** the price rests on hype and attention, which can disappear fast.
- **Huge swings:** memecoins often rise or fall by tens of percent in a few days.
- **Dependence on Solana:** an outage, a hack or a fall in Solana's popularity hits BONK too.
- **Many copies:** thousands of new memecoins launch every day and compete for the same money.
- **Regulation:** rules for memecoins and for the products built on them differ by country and can change.

## What it means if you buy regularly

BONK's history is short and its price is extremely volatile, so the result of regular buying depends heavily on when you start and stop. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads timing risk but does not remove the risk of large losses. Many people treat memecoins as a very small, speculative part of their savings — money they can afford to lose. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
