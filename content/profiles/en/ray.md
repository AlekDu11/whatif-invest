**Raydium** is a decentralised exchange (DEX) on the [Solana]({lang}/sol/) blockchain: people swap tokens there directly from their own wallets, without a company holding their money. Launched in February 2021, it became one of the main places where new Solana tokens, including many memecoins, start trading. ## How it works

Raydium is an automated market maker (AMM). Instead of matching buyers with sellers, it keeps pools of two tokens, for example SOL and USDC, and a formula sets the price from the ratio between them. Anyone can add tokens to a pool as a "liquidity provider" and earn a share of the trading fees. Raydium offers simple pools, "concentrated liquidity" pools where providers pick a price range, and LaunchLab — a tool launched in April 2025 where anyone can create a new token that starts trading on Raydium automatically.

## What the token is used for

RAY's main link to the business is buybacks: according to Raydium's documentation, 12% of the trading fees on most pools go to buying RAY on the open market. In the July–September 2026 quarter that was $5.23 million, and the buyback wallet held about 32% of all RAY in circulation, according to a Blockworks report paid for by Raydium's developer. Holders can also stake RAY for rewards. RAY does not give holders a vote or a share of profits by law — its value depends on trading activity and on those buybacks.

## Its story so far

Raydium started in February 2021, at first sharing its liquidity with Serum, an order-book exchange backed by FTX and Alameda Research. In the 2021 boom RAY rose to about $16.8 in September 2021. The FTX collapse in November 2022 hit the whole Solana world, and in December 2022 a hacker stole an admin key and drained about $4.4 million from Raydium pools; RAY fell to about $0.13 that month. In 2024 the memecoin wave brought record volume, as tokens from pump.fun "graduated" to Raydium. In March 2025 pump.fun opened its own exchange, PumpSwap, and Raydium answered with LaunchLab. In the third quarter of 2026 tokenized stocks made up 20% of Raydium's volume and 45% of its swap fees.

## Alternatives

On Solana, Raydium competes with [Orca]({lang}/orca/), [Meteora]({lang}/met-token/) and pump.fun's PumpSwap ([Pump.fun]({lang}/pump/)), while [Jupiter]({lang}/jup/) routes trades across all of them. On other blockchains the closest equivalents are [Uniswap]({lang}/uni/) and [PancakeSwap]({lang}/cake/).

## Main risks

- **Competition:** Solana exchanges fight hard for volume, and one big partner leaving — as pump.fun did in 2025 — can cut income sharply.
- **Fashion-driven income:** revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was about 72% lower than a year earlier.
- **Hacks:** smart-contract or key failures, like the December 2022 attack, can cost users money.
- **Concentration:** a large share of RAY sits with the team, the treasury and the buyback wallet.
- **Regulation:** rules for DEXs, memecoin launchpads and tokenized stocks are still being written.

## What it means if you buy regularly

RAY has gone through a boom, a crash of more than 99% from its peak and several sharp rebounds. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads the entry price over time, but it cannot make up for a fall in trading on Solana. Many people keep tokens like this to a small part of their savings. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
