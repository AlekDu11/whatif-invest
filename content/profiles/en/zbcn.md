**Zebec Network** is a crypto payments project best known for "streaming" payroll: instead of waiting for payday, employees receive their salary second by second as they work. It started on the Solana blockchain in 2022 under the name Zebec Protocol and has added crypto debit cards and payout tools for businesses. ## How it works

A company deposits stablecoins or other crypto into a Zebec smart contract and sets a salary. The contract releases the money continuously, so an employee can withdraw what they have earned at any moment, for example after a few hours of work. The same "streaming" works for subscriptions, grants and payments to contractors. Zebec cards connect a crypto balance to ordinary card networks, so the holder can pay in a shop while the crypto is converted in the background.

## What the token is used for

ZBCN is used for governance — holders vote on proposals for the network — and for staking, which earns rewards and can give benefits inside Zebec's products. Salaries and payments themselves usually move in stablecoins such as USDC, not in ZBCN, so using the payroll service does not automatically create demand for the token. The bet on ZBCN is that more companies adopt on-chain payroll and cards and that the ecosystem directs more value to token holders.

## Its story so far

Zebec, founded by Sam Thapaliya, launched Zebec Protocol on Solana in March 2022 with its ZBC token. In 2023 it introduced Zebec Pay for real-time salaries and the Zebec Card, and in January 2024 it announced its entry into the Japanese market. In April 2024 holders approved a proposal to migrate ZBC to a new token, ZBCN, in a token split, and the project rebranded as Zebec Network. ZBCN set its record in May 2025 and then lost most of that value as the market for smaller tokens cooled.

## Alternatives

For crypto payments and invoices, [Request Network]({lang}/req/) offers similar tools for businesses, while [Stellar]({lang}/xlm/) and [XRP]({lang}/xrp/) focus on cheap cross-border transfers. Zebec runs mainly on [Solana]({lang}/sol/), whose fast and cheap transactions make payment streaming practical. Outside crypto, payroll companies such as ADP, Gusto and Deel, plus banks' own instant-payment systems, serve almost all salary payments today.

## Main risks

- **Big swings:** ZBCN has lost most of its value since its 2025 peak; deep drops can happen again.
- **Weak link to usage:** payments mostly move in stablecoins, so growth in payroll may not lift the token's price.
- **Competition:** traditional payroll firms and instant bank transfers are far larger and more trusted.
- **Smart-contract and card-partner risk:** a bug, hack or the loss of a card-issuing partner could interrupt the service.
- **Regulation:** payroll, cards and tokens are tightly regulated, and rules differ by country.

## What it means if you buy regularly

ZBCN's history is short and has been very volatile. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads your entry price over time, but it does not protect you if real use of the network stays small or the token keeps falling. Many people keep smaller crypto projects to a tiny share of their savings that they can afford to see fall sharply. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
