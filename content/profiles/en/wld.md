**Worldcoin**, renamed simply **World** in October 2024, is a project co-founded by OpenAI chief Sam Altman and Alex Blania that wants to give every person a digital "proof of human" — a way to show online that you are a real, unique person and not a bot. Its token **WLD** is given to people who verify themselves and is used across its app and network. WLD is worth {{mcap:wld}} today ({{rank:wld}} among cryptocurrencies).

## How it works

You visit an **Orb**, a camera device run by local operators. It photographs your iris and face, turns them into an encrypted code and checks that this code has never been registered before. You then receive a **World ID** in the World App on your phone. With it you can prove "I am a unique human" to a website or app without telling it your name. The project says the images are deleted and the code is split across several parties so no one holds it whole. Since October 2024 the project also runs **World Chain**, its own Ethereum layer 2 network built on the OP Stack, where verified humans get priority.

## What the token is used for

WLD has three main jobs. It is handed out as grants to verified people, which is how the project attracts users. It is used for payments and fees inside the World App and on World Chain. And holders can take part in decisions about the protocol. The bet behind it is simple: if AI makes bots indistinguishable from people, a reliable proof of human — used for dating apps, ticket sales, video calls and online votes — could become valuable, and WLD is the token of that network.

## Its story so far

The token launched on 24 July 2023 after a two-year test phase. It reached a record of about $11.7 in March 2024 during the AI hype, then fell for most of the next two years as large amounts of new tokens were unlocked. In July 2024 the team and investors agreed to stretch their unlocks from three to five years. Kenya suspended the project in 2023, and Spain and Hong Kong ordered it to stop scanning in 2024. In 2025 World launched in six US cities and raised $135 million from a16z and Bain Capital Crypto by selling WLD. In April 2026 it added Tinder and Zoom integrations, and in July 2026, at its third birthday, it reported more than 39 million users and a further $52.5 million token sale led by Pantera.

## Alternatives

Other "proof of personhood" projects, such as Human Passport (formerly Gitcoin Passport) and Humanity Protocol, try to solve the same problem without an iris scan. World Chain competes with other Ethereum layer 2 networks such as [Optimism]({lang}/op/) and [Arbitrum]({lang}/arb/), and in the end all of them rely on [Ethereum]({lang}/eth/).

## Main risks

- **Unlocks:** well under half of all WLD was in circulation in 2026, so new supply keeps coming to the market for years.
- **Privacy and regulation:** iris data is sensitive; regulators can fine the project or stop the Orbs in a whole country.
- **Concentration:** Tools for Humanity, its investors and the team hold a large share and control development.
- **Adoption:** the token only gains if apps and people really use World ID; free grants can attract users who simply sell.
- **Big swings:** WLD has already lost most of its value from its 2024 peak — drops of 80–90% are possible.

## What it means if you buy regularly

WLD is a young, volatile token whose supply is still growing. Buying a fixed amount regularly smooths out the entry price, but new unlocks can push the price down for long periods. Many people keep such tokens to a very small part of their crypto. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
