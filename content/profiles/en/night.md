**Midnight** is a blockchain built for privacy: apps on it can prove that something is true — that you are over 18, that you have enough money — without showing the data behind it. It was created by Input Output, the company of Cardano founder Charles Hoskinson, as Cardano's first "partner chain", and its token NIGHT is worth {{mcap:night}} today ({{rank:night}} among cryptocurrencies). People watch it as one of the few big bets on privacy that is designed to work with regulators rather than against them.

## How it works

Midnight uses zero-knowledge proofs — a kind of cryptography that lets you prove a statement without revealing the details. Each app keeps two kinds of data: public data written on the blockchain and private data that stays on the user's device. Developers decide what is shown and what is hidden, and they can let auditors or regulators see specific facts. The project calls this "rational privacy". Developers write apps in Compact, a language based on TypeScript, so they do not need to be cryptography experts. At launch the network was run by a group of trusted operators, including a node run by Google Cloud; the roadmap moves block production to Cardano stake pool operators and later to full decentralization.

## What the token is for

NIGHT is public and can be traded on exchanges. Holding it does two things: it gives a say in future governance and it continuously generates DUST. DUST pays for transactions and smart contracts, cannot be sent to anyone else and slowly decays if it is not used. The idea is that businesses can run apps with predictable costs and without spending their tokens on every transaction, while the private fee resource cannot be used to hide payments. Validators receive NIGHT as block rewards. NIGHT exists as a native token on Cardano and is planned to work across both chains as one asset.

## Its story so far

Input Output announced Midnight in November 2022 as a data-protection chain linked to Cardano. In 2025 it ran the Glacier Drop: self-custody holders of ADA, BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB, AVAX and BAT could claim NIGHT for free, and more than 3.5 billion tokens were claimed across about 170,000 addresses. A second phase, the Scavenger Mine, let anyone earn tokens by solving puzzles in a browser; more than 1 billion NIGHT went to millions of addresses. NIGHT launched on Cardano on 4 December 2025, redemptions opened on 10 December 2025, and the mainnet went live in March 2026.

## Alternatives

Older privacy coins such as [Zcash]({lang}/zec/) and [Monero]({lang}/xmr/) focus on private payments, while Midnight focuses on private apps and data with optional disclosure. [Oasis]({lang}/rose/) and [Dusk]({lang}/dusk/) also offer confidential smart contracts, and Ethereum projects add privacy with zero-knowledge rollups. Its closest relative is [Cardano]({lang}/ada/), which shares the same founder and whose stake pools are meant to help secure Midnight.

## Main risks

- **Token unlocks:** airdropped tokens thaw in four installments over 360 days, and the reserve and treasury unlock later, which can add selling pressure.
- **Early-stage network:** the chain started in 2026 with trusted operators; full decentralization is still a plan, not a fact.
- **Regulation:** privacy technology is a target for regulators, and some exchanges have delisted privacy coins in certain countries.
- **Adoption:** the value of DUST and NIGHT depends on real apps using the network, and that is still unproven.
- **Big swings:** new tokens often fall hard after launch, and the price can be driven by Cardano news and large holders.

## What it means if you buy regularly

Midnight has traded only since late 2025, so its price history is short and says little about the long run. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads your entry points over time, which can soften the impact of the unlock schedule and sharp swings — but it does not remove the risk that a young project fails. Many people keep such tokens to a small part of their savings. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
