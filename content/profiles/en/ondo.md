**Ondo** (ONDO) is the token of Ondo Finance, a New York company that puts traditional investments — US Treasury bills, money-market funds and US stocks — on blockchains as tokens. It was founded in 2021 by former Goldman Sachs banker Nathan Allman and became one of the biggest names in "real-world assets" (RWA), a corner of crypto that big banks and asset managers now watch closely. ## How it works

Ondo buys real assets through regulated partners and issues tokens that track them. OUSG is a tokenized fund for qualified investors that holds mostly BlackRock's tokenized Treasury fund BUIDL. USDY is a dollar token for people outside the US that is backed by short-term Treasuries and bank deposits and pays their interest. Ondo Global Markets, launched in September 2025, offers more than 400 tokenized US stocks and ETFs to non-US investors. The tokens live on Ethereum, Solana and other networks, so they can move around the clock and be used in DeFi apps.

## What the ONDO token is used for

ONDO is used to vote in the Ondo DAO, which governs parts of the system such as the Flux Finance lending market and treasury spending. Holders can delegate their votes. The token does not give a share of Ondo Finance's profits: the yield from Treasuries goes to USDY and OUSG holders. Buyers of ONDO are therefore mostly betting that tokenized finance keeps growing and that the token will matter more over time — for example, if holders one day vote to share revenue, which had not happened by late 2026.

## Its story so far

Ondo started in 2021 with investors such as Founders Fund and Pantera Capital and launched OUSG in January 2023. The ONDO token became tradable in January 2024 and climbed to about $2.14 in December 2024, during the RWA hype. In 2025 Ondo announced its own network, bought the regulated broker Oasis Pro (October 2025) and launched tokenized stocks; an SEC investigation opened in 2023 was reported closed in December 2025 without charges. In 2026 it added Franklin Templeton ETFs, perpetual futures on tokenized stocks and a FINRA approval through Oasis Pro (July 2026), while the ONDO price fell more than 80% from its December 2024 peak.

## Alternatives

Ondo competes with other tokenizers: BlackRock's BUIDL itself, Franklin Templeton's BENJI and firms such as Securitize, Backed and Dinari. Among crypto tokens tied to the same theme are [Chainlink]({lang}/link/), which supplies price data to many RWA products, and [Sky]({lang}/sky/), the former MakerDAO, which holds Treasuries behind its stablecoin.

## Main risks

- **Token unlocks:** about 1.94 billion ONDO were unlocked in January 2026 and a similar batch is due in January 2027; new supply can push the price down.
- **No cash flow:** ONDO holders do not receive fees; value depends on governance and expectations.
- **Regulation:** rules for tokenized stocks and funds are still being written and differ by country; USDY and Ondo Global Markets are not available to US persons.
- **Falling rates:** lower interest rates make tokenized Treasuries less attractive.
- **Technology and partners:** smart-contract, bridge and custody failures at Ondo or its partners could hurt users.

## What it means if you buy regularly

ONDO has a short history: it rose fast and then lost most of its value, and big yearly unlocks are part of the plan. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads your entry price over time, but it does not protect you from a long decline. Remember that you are buying a governance token, not a share of the Treasuries Ondo holds. Many people keep such tokens to a small part of their savings. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
