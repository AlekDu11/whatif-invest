**Rain** is a prediction-market app on the Arbitrum network: anyone can open a market on almost any question, and others bet on the outcome. Its token, RAIN, is needed to trade, and part of every fee is used to buy RAIN back and burn it. Rain was worth about $2.3 billion in November 2026, after a steep rise and fall in its first year.

> **Key takeaways**
> - RAIN started trading in September 2025 on Arbitrum; there will never be more than 1.15 trillion RAIN, and about 714 billion circulate.
> - Every trade pays a 5% fee: 2.5% rewards market creators, liquidity providers and resolvers, 2.5% buys back and burns RAIN.
> - In November 2025 the Nasdaq-listed biotech Enlivex raised $212 million to hold RAIN as its treasury reserve.
> - In July 2026 RAIN was valued at about $9.5 billion, while Rain's own dashboard showed only about $53.6 million of trading in total.

## How it works

On Rain, a market is a multiple-choice question — who wins a match, whether something happens by a date. Its creator adds the first money (liquidity), and an automated market maker prices each answer: a share's price reflects how likely the crowd thinks that answer is, and a winning share pays out in full. Markets can be public or private, in any language. Public markets are settled by an AI oracle or the creator; anyone who disagrees can put up collateral to challenge the result, first before an AI judge, then a panel of human resolvers.

## Why people think it has value

To trade on Rain you must hold RAIN: it gives "trading power" that sets how much of your deposit you can use in markets. Holders also vote on fees, oracles and treasury spending through the Rain DAO. Because half of the 5% fee buys RAIN and destroys it, supporters argue that more betting means more tokens burned. Prediction markets were one of crypto's biggest themes in 2025–2026, and Rain pitches itself as the fully decentralised option.

## Its story so far

RAIN is issued by the Rain Foundation, registered in Panama in July 2025; the app's developers are not publicly named. The token launched on 9 September 2025. In November 2025 Enlivex announced a $212 million private placement to buy RAIN, with former Italian prime minister Matteo Renzi joining its board; RAIN was then worth about $862 million. An airdrop for beta users opened in February 2026, and version 2 shipped around the 2026 football World Cup. In July 2026 the analytics site L2Beat stopped counting about $7 billion of team-held RAIN in Arbitrum's statistics and questioned its trading, and the DAO approved a $23 million buyback and burn by the Foundation. RAIN set its record in late August 2026, then fell sharply; in September 2026 Enlivex cancelled a further financing deal with the Foundation.

## Alternatives

The biggest prediction markets are Polymarket and the US-regulated Kalshi. On-chain, [UMA]({lang}/uma/) settles Polymarket's markets and [Gnosis]({lang}/gno/) built early prediction-market tools. Rain runs on [Arbitrum]({lang}/arb/), a layer 2 network of [Ethereum]({lang}/eth/).

## Main risks

- **Token unlocks:** about 430 billion RAIN were still locked in October 2026 and are being released over months.
- **Concentration:** team and Foundation wallets hold a big share of supply; Enlivex held about 11% of circulating RAIN in July 2026.
- **Usage vs. price:** trading and deposits in the app have been small next to the token's market value.
- **Regulation and transparency:** prediction markets face scrutiny in many countries, and Rain's team is anonymous.
- **Big swings:** RAIN has already lost most of its value from its record and can fall further.

## What it means if you buy regularly

Our price history for RAIN only starts in June 2026, so there is very little track record. Buying a fixed amount regularly averages the price you pay, but it does not protect you from a long decline, dilution by unlocks or a failed project. Many people keep very young, volatile tokens to a tiny share of savings they can afford to lose. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
