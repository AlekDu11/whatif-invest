**LayerZero** is a messaging protocol that lets blockchains talk to each other: an app on one chain can send data, instructions or tokens to an app on another. Its token ZRO launched on 20 June 2024 with an airdrop to early users, and today it is worth {{mcap:zro}} ({{rank:zro}} among cryptocurrencies). People buy ZRO mainly as a bet that LayerZero stays the main "plumbing" between blockchains and that its own new network, Zero, gets used.

> **Key takeaways**
> - There will only ever be 1 billion ZRO; 8.5% (85 million) went to users in the first airdrop on 20 June 2024.
> - Its record high is {{ath:zro}} ({{athd:zro}}); today the price is {{fromath:zro}} from it.
> - Tokens for investors and the team (57.7% of supply) unlock monthly for two years after a one-year lock, so new ZRO keeps reaching the market until about mid-2027.
> - In April 2026 a bridge built on LayerZero, Kelp DAO's rsETH, lost about $292 million in an attack.

## How it works

Blockchains such as Ethereum, Solana or BNB Chain cannot read each other directly. LayerZero puts a small, unchangeable contract called an endpoint on each chain. When an app sends a message, independent checkers called DVNs (decentralized verifier networks) confirm on the other side that it really happened, and an executor delivers it. Each app chooses which DVNs it trusts and how many must agree — so its security depends on that choice. On top of this sits the OFT (Omnichain Fungible Token) standard: a token is burned or locked on one chain and minted or released on another, so the total supply stays the same. LayerZero says OFT has carried more than $290 billion of transfers across 160+ blockchains. Stargate, a bridge for moving stablecoins and ETH, was the first big app built on it.

## Why people think it has value

ZRO is the governance token: holders vote on how the protocol works. The most watched question is the "fee switch" — a small fee on every LayerZero message, which would be converted into ZRO and burned, reducing the supply. Holders have voted on it twice a year since December 2024, and every vote so far, the latest in June 2026, kept the fee switched off. In 2026 ZRO also got a bigger role: LayerZero announced its own blockchain, Zero, where ZRO is meant to be the gas fee, the staking token that secures the network, and the asset that 75% of trading fees from its ATLAS exchange engine (after rebates to venues) are used to buy back and burn.

## Its story so far

LayerZero Labs was founded by Bryan Pellegrino (CEO), Ryan Zarick (CTO) and Caleb Banister. Stargate went live in March 2022, and that month the company raised $135 million at a $1 billion valuation from investors including Sequoia, a16z and FTX Ventures; in April 2023 another $120 million round valued it at $3 billion. Before the token launched, the team ran a public "sybil hunt" against farmers who used many wallets to collect airdrops, and about 1.28 million wallets qualified. Claims opened on 20 June 2024 with a twist called "proof of donation": to claim, users had to donate $0.10 per ZRO to Protocol Guild, a fund for Ethereum developers. Many users were angry and the price fell in the first days, from about $4.40 at launch. ZRO set its record in December 2024. In August 2025 the Stargate DAO voted, with about 95% in favour, to be taken over by LayerZero for roughly $120 million in ZRO (0.08634 ZRO per STG), despite a rival offer from Wormhole. In February 2026 LayerZero announced Zero, a blockchain for financial markets backed by Citadel Securities, DTCC, ICE and ARK Invest, and in October 2026 it set 30 November 2026 as the planned launch date of the Zero main network and ATLAS.

## Alternatives

In cross-chain messaging, LayerZero competes with [Wormhole]({lang}/w/), [Chainlink]({lang}/link/) with its CCIP service, and [Axelar]({lang}/axl/). For simply moving tokens between chains, users also choose bridges such as [Across]({lang}/acx/). [Stargate]({lang}/stg/) is LayerZero's own bridge app, now owned by the protocol.

## Main risks

- **Big swings:** ZRO has spent long periods far below its record, and smaller coins can fall much faster than bitcoin.
- **Unlocks:** investors and the team receive new tokens every month until about mid-2027; if they sell, that can weigh on the price.
- **Hacks:** cross-chain bridges are among the most attacked parts of crypto. In April 2026 the Kelp DAO rsETH bridge, set up with a single DVN, lost about $292 million; LayerZero blamed North Korea's Lazarus group and the one-checker setup, and some projects moved to Chainlink afterwards.
- **Competition:** Chainlink, Wormhole and Axelar fight for the same apps, and switching is possible.
- **Value capture:** the fee switch is still off, and Zero and ATLAS are new — if they are not used, demand for ZRO stays limited.

## What it means if you buy regularly

ZRO's short history is a hyped airdrop, a sharp early drop, a record in late 2024 and long slides with sharp rebounds. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads your entry price over time instead of betting on one moment — but with a young coin and monthly unlocks it can still mean losing a large part of the money. Many people keep coins like this to a very small part of their crypto, which itself is a small part of their savings. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
