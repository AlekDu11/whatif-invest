**Grass** is a network that lets ordinary people earn rewards for sharing the internet connection they are not using. Their devices help collect public web pages, which Grass turns into cleaned-up datasets for companies that train artificial intelligence. It was built by the start-up Wynd Labs, its GRASS token launched on Solana in October 2024, and it is worth {{mcap:grass}} today ({{rank:grass}} among cryptocurrencies).

## How it works

Each user's computer or phone becomes a "node". When the network needs a public web page, it routes the request through one of these home connections, so the traffic looks like normal browsing rather than a single data centre. Validators and routers check and relay the work, and the collected pages are structured into datasets that AI labs can buy. Grass says the app only touches public data, not users' personal files or passwords. It describes its blockchain side as a data layer that settles on Solana, recording where each dataset came from.

## What the token is used for

GRASS rewards the people who share bandwidth. Holders can stake it to support routers and validators, earning extra rewards, and use it to vote on how the protocol develops. It is also meant to pay for network services such as bandwidth and fees. In practice, the token's value depends on two things: whether AI companies keep paying for Grass data, and how much new supply reaches the market.

## Its story so far

Wynd Labs, led by chief executive Andrej Radonjic, raised a $3.5 million seed round in December 2023 led by Polychain Capital and Tribe Capital. For more than a year users ran the Grass extension to collect points. On 28 October 2024 the GRASS token launched with an airdrop of 100 million tokens; within two days nearly 1.5 million addresses had claimed, at the time the most for any Solana airdrop, and the rush briefly overloaded the Phantom wallet. The price rose to about $3.89 in November 2024, then fell steeply through 2025, to about $0.17 in February 2026.

## Alternatives

Grass belongs to the "DePIN" group of crypto projects that reward people for sharing real-world resources. [Helium]({lang}/hnt/) does this with wireless coverage, [Filecoin]({lang}/fil-crypto/) with storage, and [Render]({lang}/render/), [io.net]({lang}/io/) and [Akash Network]({lang}/akt/) with computing power. For AI data itself, the real competition is traditional data and proxy companies that sell web data without a token.

## Main risks

- **Token unlocks:** a large part of the 1 billion GRASS is still being released to investors, the team and the community, which can weigh on the price.
- **Depends on AI demand:** if AI labs pay less for web data, the network's income and the token's appeal shrink.
- **Legal questions:** web scraping and data reuse face lawsuits and new rules in several countries.
- **Trust and privacy:** critics compared bandwidth-sharing apps to malware, and airdrop farming inflated user numbers.
- **Big swings:** GRASS lost most of its value after its 2024 peak; young tokens can fall sharply.

## What it means if you buy regularly

Our price history for GRASS starts in October 2024, right at launch, so it covers only one boom and one long fall. Buying a fixed amount regularly averages your entry price, but it cannot protect you from unlocks or a drop in demand for AI data. Many people keep a young token like GRASS to a small share of savings they can afford to lose. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
