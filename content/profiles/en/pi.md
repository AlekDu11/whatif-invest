**Pi Network** is a cryptocurrency you "mine" by tapping a button in a phone app once a day. It was started in 2019 by two Stanford PhD graduates, Nicolas Kokkalis and Chengdiao Fan, and gathered tens of millions of users before its coin, PI, could be traded at all. Trading opened in February 2025; today PI is worth {{mcap:pi}} ({{rank:pi}} among cryptocurrencies).

> **Key takeaways**
> - The whitepaper caps the supply at 100 billion PI; most of it is still not in circulation and is being released over years.
> - PI only became tradable with the "open mainnet" on 20 February 2025, after six years of in-app mining.
> - Its record high is {{ath:pi}} ({{athd:pi}}), set within a week of trading; today the price is {{fromath:pi}} from it.
> - Binance, Coinbase and Bybit did not list PI at launch; it trades mainly on OKX, Bitget, Gate and MEXC.

## How it works

Pi is not mined with powerful computers like [Bitcoin]({lang}/btc/). Users ("Pioneers") open the app and tap once every 24 hours to keep earning; you get a higher rate by adding up to five trusted people to a "security circle" and by inviting friends. The network itself runs on a version of the Stellar Consensus Protocol: a group of approved computers ("nodes") vote on which transactions are valid, which uses little energy. Before coins can move on the real blockchain, every user must pass identity checks (KYC) and migrate their balance from the app to a Pi wallet on the mainnet.

## Why people think it has value

Supporters see Pi as the easiest way into crypto: no money needed to start, and a huge community. Pi's team says PI should become a currency for everyday use inside its own ecosystem — apps built for Pi Browser, local shops that accept PI, games. In 2025 it announced a $100 million Pi Network Ventures fund to back projects that use PI, and launched Pi App Studio, a tool for building simple apps without code. So far, though, most demand comes from trading on exchanges rather than from real payments.

## Its story so far

The beta app launched on 14 March 2019 (Pi Day) and the test network a year later. In December 2021 Pi started an "enclosed mainnet": a real blockchain, but closed off from the outside world, so PI could not be sold. The open mainnet finally went live on 20 February 2025, and OKX, Bitget, Gate and MEXC listed PI the same day. The price hit about $3 on 26 February 2025, then fell more than 60% in March 2025 alone. By February 2026 PI had dropped about 95% from that peak, as millions of migrated balances and regular unlocks reached the market. By late 2025 the team said about 17.5 million users had passed KYC and about 15.7 million had moved to the mainnet — far fewer than the 60 million+ members it had claimed.

## Alternatives

[Stellar]({lang}/xlm/) uses the consensus design Pi is built on and is already used for payments. [Notcoin]({lang}/not/) also started as a free tap-to-earn game, inside Telegram. For people who want a scarce, widely traded coin, [Bitcoin]({lang}/btc/) remains the benchmark.

## Main risks

- **Supply:** billions of PI are still to be unlocked or migrated; new coins reaching exchanges can keep pushing the price down.
- **Centralisation:** the Pi Core Team and the Pi Foundation control the nodes and hold large wallets, and outside validators are few.
- **Limited listings:** the biggest exchanges have not listed PI, so trading is thinner and depends on a few platforms.
- **Doubts and regulation:** critics compare its referral model to a pyramid scheme; Bybit's CEO publicly called it a scam, and seven Chinese financial associations warned against it in 2025.
- **Privacy:** mandatory KYC means handing your ID to the project, and coins of users who do not complete it may never be released.

## What it means if you buy regularly

PI has traded only since February 2025, and almost all of that time the price fell, so its record says little about the long run. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads out your entry price, but it cannot help if the coin keeps losing value while new supply arrives. If you hold mined PI already, remember it was free to get but is not free of risk. Many people keep tokens like this to a very small part of their savings. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
