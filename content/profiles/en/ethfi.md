**Ether.fi** started as a way to earn staking rewards on ether without giving up control of it, and grew into one of the biggest apps on Ethereum. Since 2025 it has also been turning into a crypto "neobank", with a Visa card, savings vaults and loans. Its token **ETHFI** is worth {{mcap:ethfi}} today ({{rank:ethfi}} among cryptocurrencies).

## How it works

When you deposit ETH into ether.fi, it is used to run Ethereum validators, and you get **eETH** in return: a token that tracks your ETH plus the staking rewards it earns. Its wrapped version, **weETH**, is widely used as collateral in other DeFi apps such as Aave. Unlike many rivals, ether.fi lets depositors keep a say over the keys of their validators. For a long time the deposits were also "restaked" through EigenLayer for extra rewards; in August 2026 ether.fi took restaking out of weETH and moved it into a separate opt-in product. Around this core it built the **Cash** card: a Visa card that spends stablecoins or borrows against your crypto, plus in-app trading of tokenised stocks and fiat deposits and withdrawals.

## Why people think it has value

ETHFI is the governance token: holders vote on fees, upgrades and how the treasury is spent. Those who stake it get **sETHFI**, which receives ETHFI bought back with protocol revenue. Since 2025 part of the fees from staking withdrawals, the card, trading and lending is used for regular buybacks. The bet for holders is simple: the more people use ether.fi for staking, spending and borrowing, the more revenue there is to buy ETHFI. Media reports put card spending at over $100 million a month in September 2026.

## Its story so far

Ether.fi launched in 2023, when Ethereum had just allowed stakers to withdraw their ETH and the restaking trend was taking off. Deposits grew quickly with points programmes and airdrops. ETHFI was given out in an airdrop in March 2024 and listed on Binance, and it set its record the same month. As restaking rewards turned out much smaller than expected, the token fell more than 80% over the following year and a half. The team then shifted towards everyday finance: the Cash card, which moved to OP Mainnet in 2026, tokenised stocks and global fiat rails in August 2026, and a dedicated Aave lending market on Optimism. In October 2025 the DAO voted on a buyback of up to $50 million, after two earlier buyback proposals.

## Alternatives

The biggest rival in Ethereum staking is [Lido]({lang}/ldo/), followed by [Rocket Pool]({lang}/rpl/) and exchanges such as Coinbase and Binance that stake for their users. In restaking it competed with [EigenCloud (EigenLayer)]({lang}/eigen/) itself and other apps; in yield and "crypto bank" products with [Ethena]({lang}/ena/), [Pendle]({lang}/pendle/) and [Aave]({lang}/aave/). ETHFI's fortunes are also closely tied to [Ethereum]({lang}/eth/).

## Main risks

- **Big swings:** ETHFI is far below its 2024 record and can fall sharply again.
- **Smart contracts and partners:** a bug or hack in ether.fi, Aave or another linked protocol could cost users money and hurt the token.
- **Changing business:** the move from staking to cards and banking is new; it may not bring enough revenue to support the token.
- **Concentration:** investors and the core team received about 55% of the supply, and a few large wallets can move the price.
- **Regulation:** cards, stablecoins, tokenised stocks and staking all face rules that differ by country and can change quickly.

## What it means if you buy regularly

ETHFI's short history is one fast spike in March 2024 and a long fall after it, so a single purchase made at the wrong moment can hurt a lot. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads your purchases over highs and lows without guessing the bottom, but it does not remove the risk of losses. Buybacks help only if the business keeps growing. Many people keep a single-project token like this to a small part of their savings. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
