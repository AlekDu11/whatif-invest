**Kaia** is a blockchain created in 2024 when two Asian networks merged: Klaytn, started by South Korea's internet giant Kakao, and Finschia, started by the messaging app LINE. Its aim is to bring crypto to ordinary users of LINE and Kakao through simple apps inside the messenger. Its token, KAIA, is worth {{mcap:kaia}} today ({{rank:kaia}} among cryptocurrencies).

> - Kaia was formed by merging Klaytn (KLAY) and Finschia (FNSA); holders swapped their old tokens for KAIA in 2024.
> - LINE Mini Dapps on Kaia launched in January 2025 inside a messenger with about 200 million monthly users.

## How it works

Kaia is a "layer 1" blockchain that runs the same smart contracts as Ethereum, so developers can reuse their code and wallets. A new block is added every second and transactions are final at once. A group of validators, chosen in turn by a random draw, confirms the blocks — a system based on Istanbul BFT. Fees are low, about a tenth of Ethereum's according to the Kaia Foundation. The main way people meet Kaia is the Dapp Portal in LINE: small games and apps that open in the chat, with a wallet created in a few taps.

## What the token is used for

KAIA pays transaction fees on the network, and validators and holders stake it to secure the chain and earn rewards. New KAIA is created as rewards and part of it funds the ecosystem. Kaia is also betting on stablecoins: Tether's USDT has been issued directly on Kaia since May 2025, and in September 2025 Kaia and LINE NEXT announced Project Unify, a payments app for dollar and Asian-currency stablecoins.

## Its story so far

Klaytn's mainnet went live in June 2019 and its KLAY token reached about $4.30 in March 2021, before losing most of its value in the 2022 crash. LINE's LINK token, later renamed Finschia, had a similar path. In early 2024 both communities voted to merge, and Kaia's mainnet started on 29 August 2024. KAIA rose to about $0.41 in December 2024, then fell through 2025 and 2026 even as Mini Dapps signed up more than 130 million users, and in August 2026 it set a new low near $0.02.

## Alternatives

Kaia's closest rival is [Gram]({lang}/gram/) (called Toncoin until June 2026) on Telegram, which uses the same "crypto inside a messenger" idea. For apps and payments it also competes with [Ethereum]({lang}/eth/), [Solana]({lang}/sol/), [Sui]({lang}/sui/) and [Aptos]({lang}/apt/), and for stablecoin transfers with [TRON]({lang}/trx/).

## Main risks

- **Big swings:** KAIA lost more than 90% from its December 2024 peak; such falls can continue or happen again.
- **Dependence on partners:** much of its appeal rests on LINE and Kakao; if they change course, usage could shrink.
- **Users vs value:** many sign-ups come from games and rewards and do not automatically mean demand for KAIA.
- **New supply:** without a hard cap, new tokens keep entering circulation.
- **Regulation and concentration:** rules for crypto and stablecoins in Japan, Korea and Southeast Asia are strict and changing, and a small set of validators and the foundation hold a lot of influence.

## What it means if you buy regularly

Kaia's short history shows a quick rise and a long fall, even while its user numbers grew. Buying a fixed amount regularly means you buy more when the price is low and less when it is high, without trying to guess the bottom — but it does not remove the risk of losses. Many people keep crypto to a small part of their savings that they can afford to see fall sharply. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
