**Theta Network** is a blockchain built for video streaming and, more recently, AI computing: people run "edge nodes" that share spare bandwidth and graphics-card power and get paid in tokens. It was created by Theta Labs in San Jose, California, and uses two coins — THETA for security and voting, TFUEL for paying the network. ## How it works

Theta has three kinds of nodes. A small group of validator nodes, run by Theta Labs and enterprise partners, proposes and confirms blocks. Thousands of guardian nodes, run by ordinary holders who stake THETA, check and finalize those blocks. Edge nodes do the useful work: they relay video, render images or run AI jobs, and receive TFUEL for it. Smart contracts and fees on the network are paid in TFUEL, which works like the network's fuel.

## What the token is used for

THETA is the staking and governance coin: holders lock it in validator or guardian nodes to secure the chain and earn TFUEL rewards. Because no new THETA is ever created, its scarcity is fixed. TFUEL is the working currency — customers spend it on EdgeCloud computing and transactions, and part of it is burned. The bet on THETA is that real demand for cheap, decentralized video and AI computing grows and makes staking more valuable.

## Its story so far

Theta Labs, led by co-founder Mitch Liu, sold THETA tokens to investors in early 2018 and launched its own mainnet on 15 March 2019, together with TFUEL. In 2020 Google Cloud joined as an enterprise validator. In the spring 2021 boom THETA briefly traded above $15 (April 2021), then lost more than 95% over the next years. In May 2024 the team launched EdgeCloud, shifting the focus from video to AI computing. In December 2025 two former Theta Labs executives sued the company and its CEO in Los Angeles, accusing them of misleading partnership claims and token price manipulation; the claims had not been decided in court.

## Alternatives

In decentralized computing the main rivals are [Render]({lang}/render/), which pays for GPU rendering, and [Akash Network]({lang}/akt/), a marketplace for cloud servers. In video, [Livepeer]({lang}/lpt/) runs a decentralized network for video processing. Inside the Theta ecosystem, [Theta Fuel]({lang}/tfuel/) is the token that pays for the network's work. Outside crypto, Amazon, Google and Microsoft dominate cloud computing.

## Main risks

- **Big swings:** THETA lost more than 95% from its 2021 peak; deep drops can happen again.
- **Central role of one company:** Theta Labs and a few enterprise partners run the validator nodes and steer development.
- **Lawsuits:** the 2025 claims by former executives could hurt trust or lead to further legal problems.
- **Real demand:** EdgeCloud must win paying customers against big cloud providers and other crypto networks.
- **Regulation:** rules for tokens and staking differ by country and can change quickly.

## What it means if you buy regularly

Theta's history shows a sharp boom and a long fall. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads your entry price over time, but it does not protect you if the project fails to find real users. Many people keep smaller crypto projects to a tiny share of their savings that they can afford to see fall sharply. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
