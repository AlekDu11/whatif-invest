**Unibase** is a crypto project that wants to give AI agents — programs that act on their own, such as trading bots or assistants — a shared, long-term memory and a common way to talk to each other. Its pieces are Membase (memory storage), the AIP protocol (communication between agents) and Unibase DA (a data layer). The UB token went live on BNB Chain and Ethereum in September 2025 and is worth {{mcap:ub}} today ({{rank:ub}} among cryptocurrencies).

## How it works

Most AI agents forget everything when a session ends, and agents from different builders cannot easily share what they know. Unibase stores agents' memories on its Membase layer, where an agent's history, settings and learned knowledge can be saved, checked and reused. AIP, which the project renamed from "Agent Interoperability Protocol" to "Agent Internet Protocol" in July 2026, lets agents find and call each other and works with Model Context Protocol (MCP), the standard many AI tools use to connect to data. Unibase DA, the data-availability layer, is meant to keep all this data accessible and verifiable. Apps such as BitAgent, a platform for launching and staking agents, run on top of it on BNB Chain and Base.

## What the UB token is for

UB pays the network's fees: deploying agents, storing memory and sending messages through AIP. Builders stake UB to launch or promote agents, and people who contribute useful data, prompts or knowledge can earn UB as "knowledge mining" rewards. Holders who lock UB receive veUB, which gives voting power over the project's decisions. According to the published plan, 40% of the supply goes to the community, 18% to the team and advisers, 15% to the treasury, 10% each to the ecosystem and marketing, 5% to liquidity and 2% to Binance Alpha users.

## Its story so far

The project first presented a data-availability design in 2024 and partnered with IoTeX, a network for connected devices. Membase went live on a BNB Chain test network in February 2025, MCP support followed in March and AIP in April. The UB token was announced on 1 September 2025 and started trading around 12 September 2025, at a time when "AI agent" tokens were one of crypto's most popular themes. Its record came in May 2026; like many young AI tokens it then gave back a large part of the rise. The developer is linked to OpenOS Labs; the public sources we checked do not name the founders.

## Alternatives

Unibase competes with other projects at the meeting point of AI and crypto. [Virtuals Protocol]({lang}/virtual/) is a platform for launching and owning AI agents, [Artificial Superintelligence Alliance]({lang}/fet/) (formerly Fetch.ai) builds agent networks and tools, and [Bittensor]({lang}/tao/) pays for machine-learning work across many subnets. Outside crypto, big AI companies build memory directly into their own assistants, which is the strongest competition of all.

## Main risks

- **Young and unproven:** the token has little more than a year of history, and real demand for on-chain agent memory is not yet proven.
- **Unlocks:** about three quarters of the 10 billion tokens were not yet circulating in late 2026; new supply can push the price down.
- **Little transparency:** the public sources we checked do not name the team or its investors.
- **Hype cycles:** AI-agent tokens rise and fall together with the theme, often by more than 50%.
- **Competition and technology:** large AI firms and other crypto projects target the same problem, and the technical claims are the project's own.

## What it means if you buy regularly

UB has only a short and very volatile history, so there is no long record to judge regular buying by. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads your purchases over high and low prices without guessing the bottom, but it does not protect you from losses if the token keeps falling. Many people keep young, theme-driven tokens like this to a very small part of their savings. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
