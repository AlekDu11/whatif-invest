**Curve Finance** is a decentralized exchange built for swapping coins that should be worth about the same — mainly dollar stablecoins such as USDT, USDC and DAI, but also different versions of ETH or bitcoin. It launched in 2020, was created by Michael Egorov and runs on Ethereum and many other chains. **CRV** is its governance token, worth {{mcap:crv}} in total ({{rank:crv}} among cryptocurrencies).

> **Key takeaways**
> - The total CRV supply is capped at about 3.03 billion; new tokens are paid to liquidity providers, and the yearly issuance falls step by step.
> - Its record high is {{ath:crv}} ({{athd:crv}}), set in the first days of trading in August 2020; today the price is {{fromath:crv}} from it.
> - People lock CRV for up to four years as veCRV to vote and earn a share of the protocol's fees.
> - Curve also issues its own dollar stablecoin, crvUSD, launched in 2023.

## How it works

Instead of an order book, Curve uses pools: users deposit pairs or groups of coins and traders swap against them. Its formula keeps prices very tight when the coins in a pool are worth almost the same, so a big swap of, say, USDC to USDT costs little. People who deposit coins earn trading fees and CRV rewards. crvUSD is a stablecoin minted against crypto collateral; instead of a sudden liquidation, its "soft liquidation" system gradually converts collateral as prices fall. Curve's pools and lending markets are used by many other DeFi apps behind the scenes.

## What the CRV token is used for

CRV gains its main uses when it is locked as **veCRV** ("vote-escrowed CRV"). The longer you lock — up to four years — the more voting power you get. veCRV holders vote on upgrades and on which pools receive the most CRV rewards, receive a share of trading fees and crvUSD interest, and can boost their own rewards. Because steering rewards is valuable, other projects compete to buy or bribe veCRV votes — the so-called "Curve Wars".

## Its story so far

Curve started in January 2020, and the CRV token went live in August 2020; in the hype of its first days the price briefly jumped above $15 before falling quickly. During 2021 the Curve Wars made it one of the most important DeFi protocols, with protocols such as Convex gathering huge amounts of veCRV. In May 2023 crvUSD launched. In July 2023 a bug in the Vyper programming language let attackers drain several pools for roughly $70 million, part of which was later returned. In June 2024 large loans that the founder had taken against his CRV were liquidated, sending the price sharply lower. In 2025 the DAO approved a $60 million crvUSD credit line for Yield Basis, a new protocol by Egorov built on Curve pools.

## Alternatives

[Uniswap]({lang}/uni/) is the largest general decentralized exchange and also handles a lot of stablecoin trading. [Balancer]({lang}/bal/) offers similar multi-coin pools, while in lending and stablecoins Curve competes with [Aave]({lang}/aave/) and [Sky]({lang}/sky/) (formerly Maker), which issues USDS and DAI. Centralized exchanges remain the main place where most people simply swap stablecoins.

## Main risks

- **Big swings:** CRV has lost more than 90% from its 2020 high; DeFi tokens can fall fast in a crisis.
- **Smart-contract risk:** the 2023 exploit showed that a bug, even in a programming language, can drain funds.
- **Concentration:** a few protocols and large holders control much of the veCRV vote, and the founder's own position has already moved the price once.
- **Supply:** new CRV keeps entering the market as rewards, and many receivers sell it.
- **Regulation:** rules for DeFi exchanges and stablecoins are changing in the US, EU and elsewhere and may limit access.

## What it means if you buy regularly

Curve is a long-running, widely used protocol, but its token's chart has been mostly downhill since 2020. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads your entry over time, so you buy more when the price is low without guessing the bottom — but it does not remove the risk of losses. Many people keep DeFi tokens to a small slice of their savings. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
