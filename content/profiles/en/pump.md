**Pump.fun** is a website on the Solana blockchain where anyone can create a new memecoin in a minute for almost no money, and others can trade it straight away. It became the biggest memecoin factory in crypto and one of the highest-earning apps on Solana. PUMP is the platform's token, and the company spends a large part of its revenue buying it back. ## How it works

A new coin on Pump.fun starts on a "bonding curve": the price rises automatically as people buy and falls as they sell, so there is no need to find a market maker. When enough money has come in, the coin "graduates" and moves to a regular decentralized exchange — since March 2025 Pump.fun's own PumpSwap. The platform takes a small fee on every trade. Most of these coins are jokes that lose almost all their value within days; only a tiny share ever graduates.

## What the token is used for

PUMP does not give a share of the company or a legal right to its profits. Its value comes mainly from buybacks: from July 2025 Pump.fun used part of its fees to buy PUMP on the market, and by June 2026 it had spent more than $400 million on it. In April 2026 it burned the tokens it had bought back — about 36% of the PUMP then in circulation — and switched to a programmatic buyback-and-burn funded by half of all revenue. So the token works like a bet on how much trading the platform will attract.

## Its story so far

Pump.fun, run by founders including Alon Cohen, opened in January 2024 and quickly drove a memecoin boom on Solana. In November 2024 it switched off its livestreaming feature after harmful streams, and in December 2024 it blocked UK users after a warning from the Financial Conduct Authority. In January 2025 US traders filed a class-action lawsuit claiming the coins on the platform were unregistered securities. On 12 July 2025 the PUMP sale sold out within minutes, though it was closed to US and UK buyers. Soon after, the rival LetsBONK briefly launched more coins per day, and PUMP slipped back to its sale price. It set its record in September 2025 and by June 2026 had fallen more than 80% from it.

## Alternatives

The closest rival is LetsBONK, a launchpad linked to [Bonk]({lang}/bonk/); [Raydium]({lang}/ray/) runs its own launchpad too, and on BNB Chain Four.meme plays the same role. Pump.fun's business depends on activity on [Solana]({lang}/sol/), whose SOL token pays every trade's network fee.

## Main risks

- **Big swings:** PUMP fell more than 80% within a year of its record; memecoin trading comes and goes in waves.
- **Unlocks and concentration:** a large share of the supply went to the team and early investors and is released over time.
- **Weak rights:** buybacks can be changed or stopped by the company; holders have no legal claim on revenue.
- **Regulation and lawsuits:** the US class action and possible new rules for launchpads could hurt the business.
- **Competition:** rival launchpads can take volume quickly, as LetsBONK showed in 2025.

## What it means if you buy regularly

PUMP has a short history with sharp rises and deep falls. Buying a fixed amount regularly means you buy more when the price is low and less when it is high, without trying to guess the bottom — but it does not remove the risk of losses. Many people keep tokens like PUMP to a tiny part of their savings that they can afford to lose completely. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
