**Nexo** is a crypto platform where people can earn interest on their coins, borrow cash or stablecoins against them without selling, and trade. It was started in 2018 by Bulgarian entrepreneurs Antoni Trenchev and Kosta Kantchev, and its token, NEXO, works like a loyalty card: the more of it you hold, the better your rates. ## How it works

Nexo is custodial: you send your crypto to the company, and it keeps the keys. In return it offers savings accounts with flexible or fixed terms, credit lines that let you borrow against your bitcoin or ether instead of selling them, an exchange and, in Europe, a payment card. Nexo earns money from the interest on its loans and from trading. In April 2025 the company said it managed more than $11 billion in assets. A loan secured by crypto has a catch: if the price of the coins you pledged falls far enough, part of them is sold to repay the loan.

## What the token is used for

NEXO is an Ethereum token. Your loyalty tier — Base, Silver, Gold or Platinum — depends on how much of your Nexo portfolio is held in NEXO; Platinum needs at least 10% and a balance above $5,000. Higher tiers get higher interest on savings, cheaper credit lines and cashback, and NEXO itself can earn interest. In the past, holders also received dividends: 30% of the company's net profit. The last one, about $20.4 million, was paid on 16 June 2021, after a community vote chose daily interest on NEXO instead. The token is not a share: it gives no legal claim on Nexo's profits. Since 22 October 2026 it has also traded on Kraken.

## Its story so far

Nexo launched its token and its first crypto-backed loans in 2018. Interest in crypto lending grew fast, and NEXO climbed to about $4 in May 2021. In 2022 rival lenders Celsius and BlockFi collapsed, and NEXO fell below $0.60 by June. In the US, Nexo stopped offering its Earn Interest Product to new clients in 2022 and announced in December 2022 that it would leave the country. On 19 January 2023 it agreed to pay $45 million — $22.5 million to the SEC and $22.5 million to state regulators — without admitting or denying the findings. A week earlier, police in Bulgaria had searched its Sofia office; prosecutors closed the case on 22 December 2023, saying they found no evidence of a crime, and in January 2024 Nexo claimed more than $3 billion in damages from Bulgaria. In January 2026 California fined it $500,000 over loans made without a licence in 2018–2022. Nexo announced its return to the US in April 2025 and relaunched there on 16 February 2026 through licensed partners, including Bakkt.

## Alternatives

Crypto savings and loans are also offered by big exchanges such as Binance ([BNB]({lang}/bnb/)), [Coinbase]({lang}/coin/), Crypto.com ([Cronos]({lang}/cro/)) and [KuCoin]({lang}/kcs/). Decentralized lending apps such as [Aave]({lang}/aave/), [Compound]({lang}/comp/), [Morpho]({lang}/morpho/) and [Maple Finance]({lang}/syrup/) do the same through smart contracts, without a company holding your coins — but with their own technical risks.

## Main risks

- **Custody:** your coins are held by Nexo; if the company ran into trouble, getting them back could be slow or impossible, as Celsius and BlockFi clients learned in 2022.
- **Regulation:** crypto lending is under close watch; Nexo has already paid fines in the US, and rules differ by country.
- **Depends on one company:** NEXO's value rests mostly on Nexo's business and the benefits it chooses to offer, which it can change.
- **Big swings:** NEXO fell from about $4 in May 2021 to below $0.60 in June 2022.
- **Loans:** a sharp drop in crypto prices can trigger the sale of the coins you pledged.

## What it means if you buy regularly

NEXO is tied to one company's fortunes, so news about Nexo can move it as much as the crypto market does. Buying a fixed amount regularly means you buy more when the price is low and less when it is high, without trying to guess the bottom — but it does not remove the risk of losses. Many people keep crypto to a small part of their savings that they can afford to see fall sharply. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
