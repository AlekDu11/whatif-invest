**Axie Infinity** is a video game in which players collect, breed and battle cute monsters called Axies, and AXS is the token that runs its economy and lets holders vote on how it is managed. Made by the Vietnamese studio Sky Mavis, it became the face of "play-to-earn" gaming in 2021, when more than a million people played it every day. ## How it works

Every Axie is an NFT that the player owns and can sell. Players use teams of Axies to fight in battles and quests and earn rewards. The game runs on Ronin, a blockchain that Sky Mavis built to make game transactions cheap and fast, and AXS itself is an Ethereum-standard token that moves between Ethereum and Ronin. For years the main in-game reward was a second token, Smooth Love Potion (SLP), which players spent on breeding new Axies; in January 2026 Sky Mavis stopped issuing SLP in the main battle mode to slow down the flood of new tokens.

## What the token is used for

AXS is the governance and "ownership" token of the Axie world. Holders can stake it to earn more AXS, vote on how the community treasury is spent, and use it to breed Axies and buy upgrades. The treasury collects fees from the marketplace and breeding. In February 2026 Sky Mavis introduced bAXS, a "bonded" reward token backed one to one by AXS, airdropped first to stakers and meant to reward real players rather than people who farm and sell. New games — the land game Terrariums and the online role-playing game Atia's Legacy — are meant to create new uses for AXS.

## Its story so far

Sky Mavis released the first version of Axie Infinity in 2018. AXS launched in November 2020 for under $1, and in 2021 the game exploded, especially in the Philippines, where many people earned income from SLP during the pandemic. AXS reached about $165 in November 2021. Then the in-game economy broke: too many new tokens and too few new players pushed SLP down, and in March 2022 the Ronin bridge was hacked for about $625 million, an attack the FBI linked to North Korea's Lazarus group. By early 2026 AXS was down about 99% from its record; it briefly jumped to about $2.92 in late January 2026 on the bAXS news, then fell back.

## Main risks

- **Big swings:** AXS has lost about 99% from its peak; game tokens can fall to almost nothing.
- **Player interest:** the token depends on people wanting to play; when rewards fall, many players leave.
- **Token supply:** AXS is still being released to stakers and the ecosystem, adding selling pressure.
- **Security:** the 2022 Ronin hack showed how bridges and game chains can be attacked.
- **Concentration and regulation:** Sky Mavis and early backers hold large stakes and steer the game, and play-to-earn rewards face tax and gambling rules in some countries.

## What it means if you buy regularly

AXS is a bet on one game studio and its ability to bring players back. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads your entry price over time, but it cannot save you from a token that keeps losing value, as AXS did after 2021. Many people keep coins like this to a small part of their savings. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
