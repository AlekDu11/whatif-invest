**Sentient** is an open-source artificial intelligence project that wants to build an open alternative to the AI models of big tech companies. Its GRID network links AI models, agents, data and tools from many teams, and SENT is the token used to pay, stake and vote inside it. ## How it works

Sentient Labs is a research organization co-founded by Polygon co-founder Sandeep Nailwal together with academic AI researchers. Its main product is GRID (Global Research and Intelligence Directory), an open network where developers plug in models and AI agents that others can use; more than 110 partners had joined by early 2026. On top of it Sentient builds its own open models (the Dobby family), a multi-agent reasoning framework called ROMA and Sentient Chat, an assistant that routes questions to the right agents. The idea is that the people who build the AI keep ownership of it and get paid when it is used.

## What the token is used for

SENT is the currency of the network: it pays for AI inference, data and models on GRID. Holders can stake SENT on agents and models they trust, which signals quality and earns rewards, and staked tokens give voting power over emissions, treasury spending and upgrades. Demand for SENT therefore depends on whether real users and developers pay for Sentient's AI services.

## Its story so far

Sentient announced its $85 million seed round in July 2024. It released open models and research during 2025 and published its tokenomics on January 16, 2026: 44% for the community and airdrops, 19.55% for the ecosystem and research, 22% for the team (one-year cliff, then six years of vesting), 12.45% for investors (one-year cliff, then four years) and 2% for a public sale. Binance listed SENT for spot trading on January 22, 2026 with a "Seed Tag" warning for young, volatile tokens. The price peaked at the end of January 2026, fell by roughly three quarters by late July 2026 and partly recovered later in the year.

## Alternatives

The best-known crypto AI network is [Bittensor]({lang}/tao/), which pays miners for useful machine-learning work. [Artificial Superintelligence Alliance]({lang}/fet/) builds AI agents and infrastructure, and [Virtuals Protocol]({lang}/virtual/) focuses on tokenized AI agents. Outside crypto, Sentient competes for developers with open models such as Meta's Llama and with closed AI services from OpenAI and Google.

## Main risks

- **Big swings:** SENT lost most of its value within months of listing; new tokens are often very volatile.
- **Token unlocks:** about four fifths of the supply was not yet circulating in late 2026, and team and investor unlocks begin from early 2027.
- **Unproven demand:** it is not yet clear how many people will pay for AI services in SENT rather than in dollars.
- **Competition:** big tech companies and other open-source labs release strong AI models quickly and often for free.
- **Concentration and regulation:** the team, investors and the foundation control large shares, and rules for AI and crypto tokens are still changing.

## What it means if you buy regularly

SENT has traded for less than a year, so there is no long history to learn from, and regular buys so far have mostly been at prices below the January 2026 peak. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads out the timing risk, but upcoming unlocks can add selling pressure for years. Many people keep young tokens like this to a small part of their savings that they can afford to see fall sharply. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
