**Bitway** is a crypto project that runs "Earn" vaults: you deposit dollar stablecoins such as USDT or USDC on BNB Chain, and professional trading desks try to earn a yield on them. It also builds its own Bitcoin-compatible blockchain, and BTW is the token of that network. BTW started trading in March 2026 and is worth {{mcap:btw}} today ({{rank:btw}} among cryptocurrencies).

> **Key takeaways**
> - There are 10 billion BTW in total; most are still locked and are released on the 2nd of every month until March 2030.
> - The vault yield comes from trading strategies such as funding-rate arbitrage — holding BTW does not earn that yield by itself.

## How it works

Bitway Earn is the main product. You put USDT or USDC into a vault and get a receipt token (for example bwUSDT) that shows your share. The money is handed to partner trading desks, which mainly run "basis" and funding-rate trades — earning the gap between spot and futures prices. Profits are settled every one or two weeks. A normal withdrawal waits in a queue, usually about seven days; a "flash" withdrawal is instant but costs a penalty, and withdrawals can be paused during market stress. Separately, Bitway runs its own proof-of-stake blockchain built with the Cosmos SDK, aimed at Bitcoin-based finance ("BTCFi").

## What the token is used for

BTW pays transaction fees on Bitway's own chain. Validators stake BTW to secure that network and delegators earn rewards for supporting them. Holders vote on upgrades, fees and how the treasury is spent, and BTW is used for rewards and liquidity incentives in the ecosystem. The key point for beginners: the vaults pay out in stablecoins, so the token's price depends on demand for BTW itself, not directly on how well the vaults perform.

## Its story so far

The team, then called Side Protocol, raised a $1.5 million pre-seed round in July 2023 from investors including HashKey Capital and KR1. It renamed itself Bitway in August 2025, the same month it announced backing from YZi Labs. In December 2025 Binance Wallet ran a Pre-TGE sale (100 million BTW at $0.0005 each) and a 300 million BTW reward campaign. In January 2026 TRON DAO led a $4.444 million seed round. BTW launched on 2 March 2026 and climbed from under one cent to about $2.77 on 14 October 2026, helped by reward campaigns and short squeezes in futures. It then fell about 80% within a month, with no project-specific bad news reported.

## Alternatives

[Ethena]({lang}/ena/) earns a yield from the same kind of funding-rate trades, through its synthetic dollar. [Maple Finance]({lang}/syrup/) offers stablecoin lending yields on-chain, and [Pendle]({lang}/pendle/) lets people trade future yields. For Bitcoin-based finance, [Core]({lang}/core/) is a well-known rival chain.

## Main risks

- **Token unlocks:** roughly 1% of the supply is released every month, and the team and backer cliff ends around March 2027, when a larger amount is expected.
- **Concentration:** an on-chain review in September 2026 found about 90% of the supply in three addresses — a bridge contract, a multisig wallet and the mint wallet.
- **Custody and strategy:** vault deposits sit with trading desks and custodians; a trading loss, hack or failed partner can hit depositors, and audits are no guarantee.
- **Thin trading:** BTW trades on few exchanges with shallow order books, so prices can jump or crash fast.
- **Regulation:** yield products on stablecoins face tighter rules in many countries, which could limit who can use them.

## What it means if you buy regularly

BTW has traded for less than a year, and in that time it rose more than a hundredfold and then lost most of the gain, so there is no long record to judge it by. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads out your entry price, but it cannot protect you from a young token with most of its supply still to come. Keep in mind that using the Earn vaults and owning BTW are two different bets. Many people keep tokens like this to a very small part of their savings. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
