**JUST** is the decentralized-finance ecosystem of the TRON blockchain, the network started by Justin Sun. Its best-known parts are JustLend DAO, the largest lending market on TRON, and the USDD stablecoin. JST is the token that governs the ecosystem and, since late 2025, is bought back and destroyed with part of its profits. ## How it works

JustLend DAO works like a bank without bankers: people deposit TRX, USDT and other tokens to earn interest, and others borrow against collateral, paying interest that is set by supply and demand. It also offers sTRX, a way to stake TRX and keep a tradable token, and "energy rental", which lowers transaction fees on TRON. USDD is a dollar-pegged stablecoin backed by crypto collateral. Rules and fees across these products are changed by votes of JST holders.

## What the token is used for

JST is first of all a governance token: holders vote on interest rates, collateral and how profits are used. Its main value story since 2025 is buyback-and-burn. In October 2025 holders approved a plan under which JustLend DAO reserve income buys JST and destroys it. The first round burned about 560 million JST (about 5.7% of the original supply), the second in January 2026 about 525 million more, and further quarterly rounds followed. Fewer tokens can support the price only if demand holds up.

## Its story so far

JUST appeared in 2020, during the "DeFi summer", as TRON's answer to Ethereum's lending and stablecoin projects; JST went on sale on Binance Launchpad in May 2020. The price peaked at about $0.19 in April 2021, then fell more than 80% in the 2022 crypto crash. In May 2022 the TRON DAO Reserve launched USDD, which briefly slipped below $1 that June after the collapse of the TerraUSD stablecoin. In March 2023 the US SEC sued Justin Sun over the sale of TRX and BitTorrent tokens; in 2025 the case was put on hold. In October 2025 JUST switched to burning JST with protocol profits, and by November 2026 about a fifth of the original 9.9 billion tokens had been destroyed.

## Alternatives

On other blockchains the best-known lending protocols are [Aave]({lang}/aave/) and [Compound]({lang}/comp/). Anyone who wants exposure to the TRON network itself may look at [TRON]({lang}/trx/) directly, since TRX pays the fees on which the whole ecosystem runs.

## Main risks

- **Concentration:** a large share of JST and of JustLend deposits is linked to Justin Sun and entities close to him, so their decisions can move the price.
- **Dependence on TRON:** if activity on TRON — mostly USDT transfers — shrinks, JUST's income shrinks with it.
- **Stablecoin risk:** USDD lost its peg for a while in 2022; another break would hit the whole ecosystem.
- **Regulation:** regulators have targeted Justin Sun before, and DeFi lending rules are still unsettled in many countries.
- **Technology:** bugs in smart contracts or a sharp fall in collateral prices could cause losses.

## What it means if you buy regularly

JST has more than six years of history, with a big rise in 2021, a deep fall in 2022 and a strong climb in 2026 after the burns began. Buying a fixed amount regularly means you buy more when the price is low and less when it is high, without trying to guess the bottom — but it does not remove the risk of losses. Many people keep tokens like JST to a small part of their savings that they can afford to see fall sharply. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
