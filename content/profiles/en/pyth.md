**Pyth Network** is an "oracle": a service that brings real-world market prices — crypto, stocks, currencies, commodities — onto blockchains, so lending apps, exchanges and other smart contracts know what things are worth. The prices come straight from exchanges and trading firms rather than from scraped websites. It went live on Solana in 2021, now serves more than 100 blockchains, and its PYTH token is worth {{mcap:pyth}} in total ({{rank:pyth}} among cryptocurrencies).

## How it works

Data "publishers" send their own prices to Pyth's dedicated network many times a second. Pyth combines them into one price with a confidence range that shows how much the sources disagree. Instead of constantly writing every price to every chain, Pyth uses a "pull" model: an app fetches the latest signed price only when it needs it and pays a small fee. This keeps costs low and lets the same feeds reach many chains at once.

## What the token is used for

PYTH is mainly a governance and security token. Holders who stake it vote in the Pyth DAO on fees, rewards and new feeds. Through Oracle Integrity Staking they can also stake PYTH behind specific publishers: good data earns rewards, bad data can be penalised. Since late 2025 Pyth Pro sells premium market data to banks and trading firms by subscription, and in December 2025 the DAO started the PYTH Reserve, which each month uses a third of the treasury, funded by network revenue, to buy PYTH on the market.

## Its story so far

Pyth started on Solana in 2021, backed by a group of trading firms and exchanges, with Jump Crypto among the early contributors. The PYTH token launched in November 2023 with an airdrop to users of apps that relied on Pyth. Its price peaked in March 2024 at about $1.20, then fell for most of the next two years, with large unlocks in May 2024 and May 2025 adding supply. On 28 August 2025 the news about US GDP data sent PYTH up more than 70% in a day. Pyth Pro followed in September 2025 and the buyback reserve in December 2025.

## Alternatives

The biggest rival by far is [Chainlink]({lang}/link/), the oldest and most widely used oracle. Others include [RedStone]({lang}/red/), [API3]({lang}/api3/) and [Band]({lang}/band/). In traditional finance, Pyth Pro also competes with established data vendors such as Bloomberg and LSEG.

## Main risks

- **Token unlocks:** the last large unlock is scheduled for May 2027, and new supply has weighed on the price before.
- **Competition:** Chainlink dominates oracles, and many apps use more than one provider.
- **Data failures:** a wrong or delayed price can trigger unfair liquidations and damage trust.
- **Weak link between use and price:** the network can grow while the token falls; buybacks are small compared with the token's value.
- **Concentration and regulation:** early backers and insiders hold large amounts, and rules for crypto and market data can change.

## What it means if you buy regularly

PYTH is a young token with a short, mostly falling price history. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads your entry price, but it cannot remove the pressure from future unlocks or the risk that the token keeps losing value. Many people keep tokens like this to a small part of their savings. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
