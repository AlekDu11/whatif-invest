**WhiteBIT Coin** is the token of WhiteBIT, a European crypto exchange founded in 2018 by Ukrainian entrepreneur Volodymyr Nosov — and one of the best-performing exchange tokens since its launch in August 2022 at about $1.90. WhiteBIT says it has more than 8 million registered users.

## How it works

WBT is mainly a membership card for the exchange. Holding or locking it gives lower trading fees (up to 100% off maker fees), free withdrawals at some levels, higher referral rewards and access to new token sales. WBT is also the gas coin of Whitechain, WhiteBIT's own blockchain launched in 2023, and Whitechain fees are shared with holders every 30 days. WhiteBIT says it burns WBT weekly using 33% of trading fees and 5% of other income, aiming to burn at least half of all supply over time.

## Why it matters

There will never be more than 400 million WBT. The token peaked near $64 in December 2025 and was listed on Kraken in March 2026. One surprising fact: the company behind it, W Group, sponsors Juventus and the Ukrainian Association of Football.

## Main risks

WBT's value depends on one company: if WhiteBIT loses users, licences or trust, the token suffers. A large part of the supply sits in company-controlled funds, and a 200 million reserve is released in stages, which can add new tokens to the market. The burn rate is company policy and can change. Whitechain is run by validators controlled by WhiteBIT, so it is far more centralised than Bitcoin or Ethereum. Regular buying smooths swings but does not remove risk.
