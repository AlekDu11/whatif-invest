**WhiteBIT Coin** is the token of WhiteBIT, a European crypto exchange founded in 2018 by Ukrainian entrepreneur Volodymyr Nosov. It launched in August 2022 at about $1.90 and became one of the best-performing exchange tokens since. WBT's market value was about $6.9 billion in November 2026, ranking around 15th among cryptocurrencies.

> **Key takeaways**
> - There will never be more than 400 million WBT; about 119 million were in circulation in October 2026.
> - The exchange buys back and burns WBT with 33% of its trading-fee income plus 5% of other revenue — 131,188 WBT in October 2026 alone.
> - WBT set a record of $87.91 on 21 September 2026, after an earlier peak of $64.11 in December 2025.
> - WhiteBIT's European arm received an EU MiCA licence from Austria's regulator on 19 June 2026.

## How it works

WBT exists on Ethereum and on Whitechain, WhiteBIT's own blockchain launched in 2023, where it pays the fees ("gas"). In 2026 Whitechain began moving from a standalone network to an Ethereum layer 2 built on the OP Stack — a chain that settles on Ethereum for extra security. A public test network was running in August 2026, and the team said WBT's supply and rules would stay the same. Whitechain's validators are run by the company, so it is far more centralised than Ethereum itself.

## What the token is used for

WBT works like a membership card for the exchange. Holding or locking it gives lower trading fees (up to 100% off maker fees), free withdrawals at some levels, higher referral rewards and access to new token sales. Whitechain fees are shared with holders. The buybacks tie the token to the exchange's business: more trading means more WBT bought and burned. WhiteBIT says its long-term aim is to cut the supply towards 200 million.

## Its story so far

WBT started trading in August 2022, in a falling market, and hit its low of about $3 in February 2023. It then rose almost without pause as the exchange grew, passing $64 in December 2025. In March 2026 Kraken listed WBT, one of the first big outside exchanges to do so. In August 2026 it set new records as Whitechain announced its move to Ethereum, peaked at $87.91 in September and traded below $60 in November 2026.

## Alternatives

Other exchange tokens follow the same model: [BNB]({lang}/bnb/) (Binance), [OKB]({lang}/okb/) (OKX), [UNUS SED LEO]({lang}/leo/) (Bitfinex), [GT]({lang}/gt/) (Gate) and [Cronos]({lang}/cro/) (Crypto.com). They differ in how much of the exchange's income goes to burns and how widely the token is used outside its home exchange.

## Main risks

- **One company:** if WhiteBIT loses users, licences or trust, the token suffers directly.
- **Concentration:** a large part of the supply sits in company-controlled wallets, and the 400 million cap is far above today's circulation.
- **Burn policy can change:** the 33% buyback rule is company policy, not code.
- **Centralised chain:** Whitechain is run by the company, and its move to Ethereum adds technical risk.
- **Regulation:** exchange tokens can be treated differently from country to country; WBT is not available everywhere.

## What it means if you buy regularly

WBT's rise has been unusually smooth for crypto, but it rests on a single exchange's success, and a drop of about a third from the September 2026 record shows it can fall fast too. Buying a fixed amount regularly spreads your entry price, but it does not remove the risk of losses. Many people keep crypto to a small part of their savings that they can afford to see fall sharply. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
