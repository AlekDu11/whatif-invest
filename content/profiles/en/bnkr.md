**BankrCoin (BNKR)** is the token of Bankr, an AI agent that lets people trade crypto by simply writing a message on X or Farcaster, such as "swap $50 of ETH to USDC on Base". The token lives on Base, Coinbase's Ethereum layer 2, and is a small, high-risk bet on AI agents becoming a normal way to use crypto. ## How it works

You tag @bankrbot on X or Farcaster (or use its web terminal) and write what you want in plain words. On first use Bankr creates a crypto wallet for you, then carries out swaps, transfers between blockchains, limit orders and portfolio checks on Base, Ethereum, Polygon, Solana and other networks. Anyone can also ask Bankr to launch a new token on Base or Solana with one chat command; on Base, the person who launched it receives a share of its trading fees (60% in Bankr's own example) and Bankr keeps the rest.

## What the token is used for

BNKR is the token of this trading service. According to the project, part of the fees Bankr earns goes back into its ecosystem — buybacks, liquidity and development — and holding BNKR gives access to Bankr Club, a premium tier with lower fees and extra features. Demand therefore depends on how many people actually trade and launch tokens through Bankr, and on the wider hype around AI agents in crypto. BNKR gives no share in a company and no legal right to its income.

## Its story so far

Bankr appeared on Farcaster in late 2024 as one of the first AI agents that could trade for users inside a social feed, and BNKR started trading around the end of December 2024. During the AI-agent boom on Base the token rose more than 100-fold from its December 2024 low to its record in February 2026, when exchanges such as KuCoin ran promotions around it. Later in 2026 it fell back sharply with the rest of the AI-agent tokens.

## Alternatives

Other crypto projects built around AI agents include [Virtuals Protocol]({lang}/virtual/), a platform for creating and trading agents, [aixbt]({lang}/aixbt/), an AI market-commentary agent, the token-launch bot Clanker ([CLANKER]({lang}/clanker/)) on Farcaster and [Venice Token]({lang}/vvv/) for private AI. Outside crypto, trading apps and wallets are adding their own AI assistants.

## Main risks

- **Big swings:** BNKR is a small token; it has moved up and down by many times in a few months, and large daily moves are common.
- **Anonymous team:** the people behind Bankr are pseudonymous, which makes it harder to hold anyone responsible if something goes wrong.
- **AI and security:** an AI can misread an instruction, and tricks such as prompt injection or bad contracts can cost users money.
- **Liquidity and concentration:** trading volume is thin compared with large coins, so big holders can move the price a lot.
- **Regulation and competition:** rules for AI trading bots are unclear, and many rival agents and wallets offer similar features.

## What it means if you buy regularly

BNKR has a short and very volatile history. Buying a fixed amount regularly means you buy more when the price is low and less when it is high, but it cannot rescue a small project that loses its users. If you do buy a token like BNKR, many people keep it to a very small part of their savings that they could afford to lose entirely. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
