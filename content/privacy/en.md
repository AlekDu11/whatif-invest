*Last updated: 21 November 2026.* WhatIfStack (whatifstack.com) is a free website with market prices, calculators and articles. This page explains what data we handle.

## Without an account

- **Your portfolio, watchlists and settings** are saved only in your own browser (local storage). We do not see them.
- **Analytics:** we use Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity and Vercel Web Analytics to understand which pages are useful. They collect anonymous usage data such as pages viewed, device type and approximate country.
- **Error reports:** Sentry receives technical error messages so we can fix bugs.
- **Server logs:** our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard request logs for security.

## With an account (optional)

You can sign in with Google, Telegram or an e-mail link — there are no passwords.

- **What we store:** your e-mail address or Telegram ID, your first name as provided by Google or Telegram, and the data you choose to sync (portfolios, transactions, watchlists, bonds).
- **Why:** only to show you the same portfolio on every device.
- **Where:** in our database at Upstash (EU/US cloud). Sign-in e-mails are sent through Brevo.
- **We do not** read your Google contacts, files or anything else — sign-in asks only for your name and e-mail.

## Telegram bot and e-mail updates

- The @WhatIfStack_bot keeps your chat ID, chosen language, price alerts and any support message you send.
- If you subscribe to the weekly e-mail, we keep your address until you unsubscribe.

## What we never do

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not show your data to advertisers. Links to exchanges and brokers may be referral links: if you follow them, those companies have their own privacy policies.

## Deleting your data

Write to **support@whatifstack.com** or use 💬 Support in the Telegram bot, and we will delete your account and synced data within 30 days. Clearing your browser data removes everything stored locally.
