WhatIfStack exists to answer one question honestly: what would regular investing in crypto, stocks or gold actually have done? This page explains the rules every article, news brief, guide and asset profile on the site follows.

## Who writes

Articles are published by the WhatIfStack Research team. We use AI tools to research and draft, and every piece follows a written editorial standard: a headline that states a specific fact, a lead that answers the question, sources at the end and a section on what the topic means for people who buy regularly. We do not publish paid articles or press releases dressed up as news.

## Where the numbers come from

Prices and history come from public market data: Binance and CoinGecko for crypto, Yahoo Finance and Moscow Exchange for stocks, ETFs and metals, alternative.me for the Fear & Greed index, World Bank data for inflation and Polymarket for prediction odds. Numbers inside articles are computed from that data and update automatically, so a figure can change after publication. Every asset page shows its data source and the date of the last update. See the [methodology]({lang}/about/) for how DCA results are calculated.

## How we check facts

- Every news fact must come from a named source linked at the end of the piece. If we cannot find a reason for a price move, we say so instead of inventing one.
- Numbers are taken from our data or from the source, never estimated.
- Claims about airdrops, rewards and campaigns state what is officially confirmed and what is only expected.

## Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it on the page and, for material errors, add a short note saying what changed. You can report a mistake by sending a message to [@WhatIfStack on X](https://x.com/WhatIfStack).

## What we never do

- We never promise profit, and we never tell you to buy or sell. History is not a forecast.
- We never let a partner pay to change data, rankings or conclusions.
- We never ask for your seed phrase, passwords or private keys.

## Partner links and how we earn

Some links to exchanges, wallets and services are partner (referral) links, marked with a note next to the button. If you sign up through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. This is how the site stays free and without banner clutter. Partner status never affects what we write: activity guides describe the risks and the unconfirmed parts of every program, including the ones we earn from.

*Nothing on WhatIfStack is investment advice.*
