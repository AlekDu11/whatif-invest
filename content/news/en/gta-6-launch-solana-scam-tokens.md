Grand Theft Auto VI goes on sale today, November 19 — and crypto scammers have been preparing for months. Security firm Blockaid says it found more than 20,000 tokens with GTA in the name on Solana between July and early November, and flagged more than 8,800 of them as malicious, risky or spam.

## What happened

- **There is no official GTA coin.** Rockstar has not launched any token, airdrop or crypto payment for the game. Every "$GTAVI" or "GTA coin" is unofficial.
- **Launchpads did the heavy lifting.** About 46% of the flagged Solana tokens were created on a pump.fun-style launchpad. Blockaid also counted 593 GTA-named tokens on Base and 111 on Ethereum.
- **Fake shops drain wallets.** Pages posing as pre-order, "Ultimate Edition" or early-access sites ask visitors to connect a wallet, then empty it.
- **Coordinated posts on X.** Blockaid tracked 451 lure posts from 116 accounts with near-identical "check your eligibility" links. Activity picked up from late August and grew again from October.
