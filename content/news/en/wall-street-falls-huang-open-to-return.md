US stocks sold off across the board on Tuesday, hours before Nvidia's first quarterly report since Jensen Huang stepped down as CEO. By midday the S&P 500 was down about 1.3% and the Nasdaq about 1.4% — and then Huang said publicly that he would be willing to come back.

## What happened

- **A broad selloff.** Around noon New York time the Dow was down roughly 820 points (1.6%), all 11 S&P 500 sectors were in the red, and falling stocks outnumbered rising ones by more than 3 to 1 on the NYSE.
- **Chips and software led the drop.** Micron lost about 4%, AMD and Intel more than 3%, Salesforce and Intuit 7–10%. Nvidia has shed roughly $410 billion in market value since Huang resigned last week.
- **Huang's offer.** The former CEO said he would be "honored" to return if the board decided it served shareholders and employees. Nvidia's loss narrowed from 3.5% to about 0.5% on the news, and bitcoin and the indexes bounced more than 1% from their lows. The board has not decided; the WSJ reports it hired a recruiting firm, and Colette Kress is interim CEO.
- **What's next.** Nvidia reports after the close (4:20 p.m. ET, 21:20 UTC), with revenue expected around $108 billion. Markets price a roughly 95% chance of a December Fed hike, and Anthropic is due to debut on Nasdaq on November 19.
