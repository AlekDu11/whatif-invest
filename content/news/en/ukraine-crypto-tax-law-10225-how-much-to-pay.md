Crypto gains in Ukraine will be taxed at 23% of the profit — 18% income tax plus a 5% military levy. That is what law No. 10225-d, passed by the Verkhovna Rada on 25 November 2026, sets out; the new rules apply from 1 July 2027. You can work out your own tax with our [crypto tax calculator for Ukraine]({lang}/taxes/ua/).

## How much tax on crypto

- **Standard rate:** 23% (18% income tax + 5% military levy).
- **Tax base:** the profit — sale price minus purchase price — when you cash out to hryvnia.
- **Relief:** crypto bought before the law takes effect is taxed at a reduced 5% + 5% = 10% until 2028.
- **VAT:** crypto transactions are exempt.
- **When:** the law takes effect on 1 July 2027.

## Example: bought for 100,000, sold for 180,000 hryvnia

The profit is 80,000 hryvnia. At the standard rate the tax is 18,400 hryvnia and you keep 161,600. Try your own amounts in the [calculator]({lang}/taxes/ua/), which also lists the relief, the filing deadline and the rules for stocks.

## What it means if you buy regularly

Tax is due on the profit when you cash out, not on every purchase — so if you buy a little every month, keep your purchase history: it is what your cost will be based on. $10 a day in bitcoin over 5 years: {{dca:btc:5}} from {{dcainv:5}} invested ({{dcaroi:btc:5}}). Bitcoin now: {{price:btc}}.
