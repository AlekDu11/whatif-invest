CoinGecko, one of the oldest independent crypto price trackers, now has a controlling owner. The Solana Foundation, the Swiss nonprofit that supports the Solana blockchain, said on Tuesday it holds a majority interest in the company. The news came on the final day of Solana's Breakpoint conference in London.

## What happened

- **Majority stake, secret price.** Solana Foundation product chief Vibhu Norby confirmed the majority interest on X. Neither side disclosed the price or the size of the deal.
- **From data to trading.** CoinGecko plans to widen its coverage (including tokenized real-world assets and derivatives), build market-intelligence tools and explore features that let users trade on-chain. The foundation spoke of new trading and DeFi products built with Solana.
- **A neutrality pledge.** CoinGecko's founders said its methodology will stay public and transparent, and that the website, app and API will keep running as usual.
- **A big audience.** Founded in 2014, CoinGecko says it tracks more than 50 million assets across over 2,000 exchanges and has more than 10 million monthly users.
