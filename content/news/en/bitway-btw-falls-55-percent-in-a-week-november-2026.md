Bitway, the token behind a set of stablecoin "Earn" vaults on BNB Chain, has been the biggest loser among the top ~90 cryptocurrencies almost every day this week. Barely a month after its record, it is worth about a fifth of its peak.

## What happened

- **Thursday, November 13.** BTW fell about 19% in 24 hours to roughly $0.85 (TokenPost snapshot at 12:02 UTC), during a broad altcoin sell-off: bitcoin was down nearly 5% to about $69,300 that day.
- **Friday–Saturday, November 14–15.** Another 22% drop to about $0.66, then a fall of roughly 21% more.
- **Night to Monday, November 16.** At 04:02 UTC TokenPost had BTW down 31.5% in 24 hours at $0.483 — the worst result in its top-89 list, even though 54 of those 89 coins were up and bitcoin was flat near $65,100.
- **Monday morning.** CoinMarketCap showed BTW around $0.44–0.52, down about 28–37% in a day and about 55% over seven days. Trading volume also fell by about half, so the price is moving on thin liquidity.

## Why it moved

We found no Bitway-specific announcement — no hack, delisting or product problem — that explains the slide. What we do know:

- **The rally before it was mostly hype.** BTW went from under $0.01 at its March 2026 launch to a record $2.77 on October 14, helped by a Binance Wallet reward campaign (which ended October 2), a points-conversion event and heavy short-covering in futures. Analysts at Surf called that rise "reflexive" rather than a real repricing. Coins that rise this fast often give much of it back once attention fades.
- **New supply keeps arriving.** About 101.6 million BTW (roughly 1% of the total supply) unlocked on November 2, and similar unlocks follow on the 2nd of every month. Around 70% of all 10 billion tokens are still locked, and a bigger cliff for the team and backers comes in March 2027.
- **The market is in "extreme fear".** The Crypto Fear & Greed Index is around 10–12, bitcoin briefly fell below $62,000, and risky small-cap tokens are hit hardest in such weeks. But on the latest leg BTW fell while most of the market was rising, so this is not just the market.

Our price history for BTW only starts in August 2026, so there is no long track record to judge.
