A multisig wallet built on Safe (formerly Gnosis Safe) lost about $6.45 million on November 16. Security firm PeckShield flagged the drain, and on-chain analyst Specter traced it to permissions that had already been granted inside the wallet's Zodiac Roles module.

## What happened

- **Most of it was dollars.** Around $4.3 million left as USDC and about $1.7 million as syrupUSDC, a yield-bearing version of the stablecoin.
- **No new signatures needed.** The Roles module lets approved addresses run pre-authorised transactions without the owners signing again, and the attacker reportedly used exactly that path.
- **Into Monero.** PeckShield said about $813,000 was used to buy Monero (XMR) on Hyperliquid; Specter separately reported about $1.2 million converted via Wagyu. Monero is much harder to trace.
- **Not the first time.** Add-on modules around Safe wallets were also behind losses in May (about $3 million), June (about $1.5 million) and October (about $305,000).

## What it means for your own coins

The lesson is about add-ons, not just passwords: every module or permission you switch on is another key to the vault. If you hold crypto yourself, review which apps and modules have approval over your wallet, and remove the ones you no longer use.
