The pool of dollar stablecoins that usually fuels crypto buying is shrinking fast. The combined supply of USDT, USDC and DAI fell by about $11.5 billion over the past 30 days — the biggest drop on record, according to Coinbase Institutional data.

## What happened

- **Less dry powder.** Stablecoins are the cash traders keep on exchanges. When supply shrinks, there is less money ready to buy when prices fall, Coinbase Institutional noted.
- **The Fed is the main driver.** Coinbase's weekly analysis linked the crypto slide mainly to rate expectations after October jobs and inflation data came in hotter than forecast. Consumer prices rose 0.4% in October versus 0.3% expected.
- **A hike is now the base case.** CME FedWatch showed about an 88.5% chance of a quarter-point rate rise at the December meeting, from a current range of 3.75%–4%.
- **Losses on paper.** Bitcoin holders were sitting on about $12.7 billion of unrealized losses, per the same analysis.
