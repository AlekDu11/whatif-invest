The two largest corporate crypto treasuries are deep under water. After bitcoin's slide to about $55,000 on Saturday, Michael Saylor's Strategy and Tom Lee's BitMine together showed an unrealised loss of roughly $27.9 billion, per Bitcoin Sistemi's tally.

## What happened

- **Strategy.** The company holds 850,849 BTC bought for about $64.2 billion, an average of $75,452 per coin. At Saturday's prices that stack was worth about $47 billion — a paper loss near $17 billion, or about 26%.
- **BitMine.** It holds 6,077,745 ETH, almost 5% of all ether, at an estimated average of about $3,350. With ether near $1,550–1,650 the loss is about $11 billion; it still bought 7,653 ETH last week.
- **Shares.** Strategy (MSTR) closed Friday at $77.80, down 9.7% and at a 52-week low. Last week it bought only 485 BTC but spent $228 million buying back its STRC preferred shares.
- **No forced selling.** Strategy says all its bitcoin is unencumbered and it keeps a $35 billion reserve; Saylor wrote that seller exhaustion will come before his.
