Crypto exchange Backpack has received approval from the US Commodity Futures Trading Commission to run a regulated derivatives exchange and clearing house in the United States. That would let American users trade leveraged crypto perpetual futures — a product so far mostly available on offshore platforms. Backpack's BP token rose about 42% in 24 hours to $1.19, according to CoinMarketCap data.

## What happened

- **Two licences.** The CFTC registered Backpack's clearing arm (TLD Clearing) as a derivatives clearing organisation on November 13 and approved its exchange (TLD Markets) as a designated contract market on November 16.
- **The product.** US customers are to get round-the-clock perpetual futures with leverage, fiat or crypto as collateral, cross-margining and access to Backpack's global order book.
- **No date yet.** The company has not said when trading will start.
- **Background.** Backpack's CEO Armani Ferrante previously worked at Alameda Research, and the company earlier bought FTX's European business.

## What it means if you buy regularly

Perpetual futures are leveraged bets, not ownership of a coin: a small move against you can wipe out the whole position. A regulated venue makes the product more accessible, not less risky.
