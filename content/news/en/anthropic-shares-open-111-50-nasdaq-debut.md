Anthropic's stock started trading on Nasdaq on Thursday, November 19, under the ticker ANT. The first trade came just before noon New York time at $111.50 — about 34% above the $83 IPO price — but the early jump faded fast as the wider market turned lower.

## What happened

- **A big first print.** The opening price valued the company above $1.5 trillion. Bloomberg had indicated an open nearer $103.
- **Up, then down.** Shares climbed as high as $115.10 right after the open, fell to about $100 by 12:05 p.m. and traded near $94 roughly an hour later — still around 13–14% above the offer price.
- **Record offering.** Anthropic sold 980 million shares and raised $81.3 billion, the largest IPO ever, ahead of SpaceX's $75 billion in June. It had first planned 825 million shares at $70–78.
- **Weak backdrop.** The Dow was down about 900 points (−1.8%) and the Nasdaq about 1.5% in early afternoon trading. Listed options on ANT start on Monday.
