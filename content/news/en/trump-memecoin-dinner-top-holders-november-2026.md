Donald Trump hosts the biggest holders of his TRUMP memecoin at his golf club near Washington on Sunday, November 22. It is the third such dinner. The token gained about 9% after the event was announced, but it trades about 99% below its January 2025 peak.

## What happened

- **Who is invited:** the 185 wallets with the largest time-weighted TRUMP balances between September 30 and November 12. The top 29 get a VIP reception and the top four get gold watches. Holders who sold more than half of their coins by November 12 dropped off the list.
- **Venue:** Trump National Golf Club in the Washington, DC area. Earlier dinners took place in May 2025 and April 2026.
- **Cheaper seats:** the cost of a spot, measured in tokens needed, has reportedly fallen to around $518, from at least $55,000 for the first dinner.
- **Criticism:** watchdog Public Citizen found that 15 of the 29 VIP winners bought on exchanges that do not serve US customers, and Rep. Robert Garcia asked Trump's sons to preserve related documents.
