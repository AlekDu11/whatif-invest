US tech was heading for another weak open on Monday. About 90 minutes before the bell, Nasdaq 100 futures were down roughly 1.3%, Nvidia was off around 3% in premarket trading a day before its quarterly results, and AI-infrastructure stocks were falling 5% or more.

## What happened

- **Tech took the hit.** S&P 500 futures were down about 0.6% and Dow futures were almost flat, so the selling was concentrated in technology.
- **Nvidia's slide continues.** The stock lost nearly 13% over the past five days and has had its longest run of weekly losses since October 2012. It reports after the close on Tuesday, November 17, its first results since Jensen Huang stepped down as CEO.
- **AI infrastructure fell hardest.** Before the open, CoreWeave and Nebius were down about 5.5%, Intel, AMD, Broadcom and Micron 3.5–5%, and Dell and HP around 5%.
- **Rates and the IPO add pressure.** The 10-year Treasury yield sat near 5.15% and markets fully price a December Fed hike. Anthropic is due to start trading on November 19, and one Swissquote analyst said buyers may sell other tech shares to make room.
