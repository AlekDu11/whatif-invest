Ethereum is down today because selling keeps outweighing buying: ether fell about 4–5% in 24 hours to near $1,650 on Saturday, November 28, as money kept leaving US spot ether ETFs, more ETH moved onto exchanges and a Trump-linked project's reserve dumped about 12,000 ETH. At around $1,650, ether is roughly 66% below its August 2025 record near $4,950.

## What happened

- **The drop.** Ether traded near $1,659 at 06:43 UTC, down about 4.2%, in a 24-hour range of roughly $1,638–1,741 (CoinGecko via CryptoTicker). By 11:00 UTC it was near $1,645.
- **A big seller.** Around 01:00 UTC a World Liberty Financial reserve wallet sold about 12,121 ETH (including staked mETH) for some $20 million, about half what it paid ([our WLFI brief]({lang}/news/why-is-wlfi-crashing-november-2026/)).
- **Liquidations.** About $87 million of leveraged ETH longs and $16 million of shorts were wiped out in 24 hours (CoinGlass).
- **Mood.** The Crypto Fear & Greed Index fell to 10, "extreme fear". Bitcoin was down about 3% near $59,500.

## Why is ether falling?

There was no single ether-specific trigger; three pressures stacked up. US spot ether ETFs lost about $523 million over eight sessions to November 25 and another $69 million on November 27 (Farside, SoSoValue). CryptoQuant counts about 516,000 more ETH on exchanges over 11 days — coins usually move there to be sold. And with US inflation high, futures price further Fed rate hikes. Ether is down about 26% in November.
