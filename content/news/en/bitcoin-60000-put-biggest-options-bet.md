The mood in bitcoin options has flipped. The single largest position on Deribit, the biggest crypto options exchange, is now a put — a contract that pays off if bitcoin falls — at a $60,000 strike, with about $900 million in open interest. The $100,000 call, long the favourite bet on a rally, has dropped to second place.

## What happened

- **Puts on top.** The $60,000 put holds roughly $900 million in notional open interest versus about $867 million for the $100,000 call, CoinDesk reported.
- **Big hedges moved lower.** More than $1 billion of put blocks traded on the Paradigm platform in two days as large traders rolled their protection from $74,000 strikes down to $63,000.
- **Fear in volatility.** Volmex's 30-day implied volatility index for bitcoin hit about 57 on Sunday, its highest since June.
- **The price backdrop.** Bitcoin dipped below $62,000 on Saturday, then bounced above $64,000 on Monday. It is down about 17% in November and has posted six weekly losses in a row.

## Our numbers

A big put position is often insurance, not a forecast: funds buy it to protect holdings they keep. Bitcoin trades at {{price:btc}}, about 49% below its all-time high of $126,080 from October 2025.
