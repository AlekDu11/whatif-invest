Minutes of the Federal Reserve's October 27–28 meeting, released on November 18, show that almost all policymakers expect to raise interest rates further in the coming months. Crypto slid after the release: by early November 19 bitcoin was down about 2.7% in a day to around $62,600, and ether was near $1,770.

## What happened

- **A narrow hold.** The Fed kept its rate at 3.75–4% in a 10–2 vote; Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack wanted a quarter-point hike, and some who voted to hold called it a close call.
- **Bigger moves on the table.** Two officials said a hike larger than 0.25 points could be considered in December if inflation data disappoint. Futures now point to quarter-point hikes in December and January.
- **An AI warning.** Many officials saw the AI build-out as a material inflation risk, and staff flagged quickly growing, often opaque AI-related debt that could amplify losses if returns disappoint.
- **Fear everywhere.** The Crypto Fear & Greed Index sat at 10 out of 100, and gold dropped to about $3,736.
