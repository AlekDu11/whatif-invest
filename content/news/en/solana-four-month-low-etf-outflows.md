Solana is in one of its worst stretches of the year. SOL dropped to $67.41 over the weekend, its lowest price since late June, and traded near $71 on Monday as money kept leaving US spot Solana ETFs.

## What happened

- **Seven red weeks.** Sunday's weekly close of $72.71 was the seventh lower weekly close in a row. SOL is down about 32% in a month and roughly 40% from its $121.96 weekly close on September 27.
- **ETF money is leaving.** US spot Solana ETFs had about $32 million of net outflows in the week to November 13, the sixth losing week in a row, for about $197 million in total. That came after 14 straight weeks of inflows.
- **The whole market is weak.** Bitcoin is near $64,000, ether near $1,800, and the Crypto Fear & Greed Index sits at 12 out of 100.
- **The upgrade didn't help.** The Alpenglow network upgrade that went live on October 21 has not stopped the slide.
