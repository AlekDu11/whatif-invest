The Bank of England said on Thursday, November 26, that risks to the global financial system have grown, and pointed to the fast-growing pile of debt behind the AI boom. In its half-yearly Financial Stability Report it says AI financing now runs through stock markets, bond markets, private credit and more complex deals — and that bonds from the big cloud companies alone are set to top $305 billion this year.

## What happened

- **AI borrowing.** Bond issuance by hyperscalers (Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Oracle and peers) is on course to exceed $305 billion in 2026, about 8% of all global investment-grade issuance. Spreads widened sharply for some lower-quality AI-related borrowers.
- **Crypto.** Coverage of the report says the Bank used October's roughly $6.4 billion Binance hack as an example of weak operational controls at crypto firms, while judging that crypto's limited links to mainstream finance kept the damage contained.
- **Quantum.** The Bank warns quantum computing could break widely used cryptography and calls an orderly move to quantum-resistant systems a priority.
- **Banks.** It finished its review of UK bank capital: Tier 1 requirements for major banks fall by about 0.5 percentage point, the countercyclical buffer stays at 2%.
