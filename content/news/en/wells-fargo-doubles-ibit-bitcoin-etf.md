Wells Fargo more than doubled its holdings of BlackRock's spot bitcoin fund (IBIT) in the third quarter, according to a 13F filing published on November 16. The position was worth about $2.35 billion on September 30 — a snapshot from before bitcoin's slide this autumn.

## What happened

- **From 24.4 to 49.6 million shares.** The share count rose about 103% from the end of June, when the stake was worth $812 million.
- **Mostly clients' money.** Galaxy Research's Alex Thorn said about 85% sat in Wells Fargo's two brokerage arms, meaning these are largely customer positions rather than the bank's own bet. Galaxy puts its total exposure across spot bitcoin ETFs at about 30,550 BTC.
- **Other banks added too.** Barclays raised its IBIT stake 48% to $187 million; Citigroup also increased its bitcoin ETF holdings.
- **November looks different.** US spot bitcoin ETFs lost about $1.28 billion in the first week of November and $1.07 billion in the second, per Farside data.
