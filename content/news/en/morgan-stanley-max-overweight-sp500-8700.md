Morgan Stanley used its 2027 outlook to move global equities to "maximum overweight", betting that the AI investment cycle has further to run. The call landed on a rough day: on Monday, November 16, the S&P 500 fell 1.9% and the Nasdaq 2.5% as Nvidia led AI stocks lower.

## What happened

- **Ambitious targets.** The bank sees the S&P 500 at 8,700 by the end of 2027, about 15% above current levels, MSCI Europe at 3,030 (+15%) and Japan's TOPIX at 4,800 (+22%).
- **Where the money goes.** Europe and Japan were raised to overweight, emerging markets stay at equal-weight. To fund more stocks, it cut exposure to other bonds, commodities and cash.
- **The reasoning.** About $1.7 trillion of hyperscaler capex expected next year, global earnings growth of 13% in 2027, and a world-stock forward P/E near 15, the lowest since 2023.
- **The risks it names.** Inflation forcing more Fed tightening than the two further hikes its economists expect, and doubts about how long AI spending lasts.
