Chip research firm SemiAnalysis has sharply lowered its forecast for global spending on AI accelerators, and several big banks cut their Nvidia price targets just before the company reports results after the US close on Tuesday, November 17.

## What happened

- **A $608 billion cut for 2030.** SemiAnalysis now expects $923 billion of AI accelerator spending in 2030, down from $1.531 trillion (-40%). Its 2028 estimate fell 28% to $758 billion and 2029 fell 41% to $796 billion; 2026 stays near $392 billion.
- **The reasons.** Slower growth in new revenue at frontier AI labs in the third quarter, cooling investor appetite to fund data centres, and about 5.8 GW of US data-centre capacity being delayed.
- **Nvidia takes most of the hit.** It accounts for about two-thirds of the cut in projected wafer volumes, as AMD and big tech companies' own chips win share.
- **Banks trim targets.** HSBC cut its Nvidia target from $365 to $170 and downgraded to Hold, UBS from $300 to $240, Mizuho from $250 to $220, citing the leadership change after Jensen Huang's exit and tighter export rules.
