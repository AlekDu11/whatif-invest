The Trump administration plans to give more than two dozen American AI companies the label of "national champions", The Wall Street Journal reported, citing people familiar with the plan. Nvidia, Microsoft, Amazon, OpenAI and Anthropic are said to be on the list. The news lands in a week that will test the AI trade hard.

## What happened

- **A list of 20+ companies.** The idea is to help these firms win business abroad and compete with foreign rivals. What the label would bring in practice, and when it would be announced, is not yet clear, and the plan has not been officially confirmed.
- **Nvidia is still on it.** That comes days after Jensen Huang stepped down as CEO; former CFO Colette Kress is running the company for now.
- **AI stocks are deep in a correction.** Nvidia closed Friday at $162.85, about 33% below its October 6 peak of $243.37. The chip ETF SMH is roughly 28% off its 52-week high.
- **A big week ahead.** Nvidia reports results on Tuesday, November 17, after the US close, and Anthropic is set to price its IPO on Wednesday.
