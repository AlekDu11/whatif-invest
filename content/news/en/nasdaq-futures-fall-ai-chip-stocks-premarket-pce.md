The sell-off in AI stocks deepened before Wall Street opened on Wednesday, November 25. Nasdaq-100 futures were down about 2.5% early in the morning in New York, and Nvidia, Micron, AMD and Dell lost 5–7% in premarket trading, a day after the Philadelphia chip index fell 7.9%.

## What happened

- **Futures.** Around 10:50 UTC, Nasdaq-100 futures were down about 2–2.5%, S&P 500 futures about 1–1.5% and Dow futures about 0.4–0.8%.
- **AI hardware.** Nvidia was about 5% lower near $147, Micron about 6%, AMD about 6%, Dell about 7%, CoreWeave about 6% and Broadcom about 4%, per Investing.com and TECHi.
- **Two-day swing.** A basket of ten AI hardware stocks rose 13.6% on Monday after Jensen Huang's return as Nvidia CEO, fell 8.1% on Tuesday and was set to open below Friday's close, TECHi calculated.
- **Asia.** South Korea's Kospi fell about 6% and SK hynix about 11%.

## Why it matters

Three worries hit at once: doubts about whether huge, partly debt-funded AI data-centre spending will pay off, wider credit spreads at AI-linked companies, and a pending US trade (Section 301) finding that could bring new duties on 16 economies. Money markets also price a 96% chance of a Fed rate hike in December, and the October PCE inflation report is due at 13:30 UTC. US markets are closed on Thursday for Thanksgiving.
