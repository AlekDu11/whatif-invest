Bitcoin could not hold Monday's bounce. After rising almost 6% during the US session on November 23, when Nvidia's surge lifted the whole stock market, it slipped back below $60,000 in Asian trading on Tuesday, November 24. Over 24 hours it swung between about $59,400 and $64,500.

## What happened

- **ETF outflows continued.** US spot bitcoin ETFs lost a net $569.7 million on November 23, according to SoSoValue. Last week they lost about $1.8 billion, the fourth weekly outflow in a row.
- **Stocks and bitcoin split.** The Nasdaq jumped 6.5% and the S&P 500 4.7% on Monday, their best day since April 2025, while bitcoin kept only a small part of its gain.
- **Leverage was flushed both ways.** CoinGlass counted about $825 million of liquidations in 24 hours, $349 million of it in bitcoin, most of that from short sellers.
- **Fear stays extreme.** The Crypto Fear & Greed Index stood at 11 out of 100. The weekend low of $55,254 was the lowest in about two years.
