Anthropic, the AI company behind Claude, now trades below the $83 price of its record IPO less than a week after its Nasdaq debut. The stock closed at $81.91 on Tuesday, November 24, and fell again on Wednesday as a new sell-off in chip stocks pushed the Nasdaq-100 close to bear-market territory.

## What happened

- **Below the IPO price.** After opening at $111.50 on November 19 and closing its first day at $97.17, Anthropic slipped under $83 on November 24 and ended that day down 6.5% at $81.91 (Yahoo Finance).
- **Wednesday.** Shares lost another 5.7% before the open, to about $77, and the FT reported a drop of around 9% to just under $75 during the session. That is more than 30% below the first-day high.
- **The wider market (15:00–16:30 UTC).** The Nasdaq-100 fund QQQ was down more than 4%, leaving the index about 20% below its October record; Micron and Lam Research lost about 11%, Nvidia 7–8%. Losses narrowed in the afternoon.
- **The trigger.** Hotter October PCE inflation (3.8% a year), a likely Fed rate hike in December and proposed new US tariffs on 14 economies.
