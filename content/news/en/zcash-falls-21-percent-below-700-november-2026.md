Zcash, the privacy coin that was one of 2026's best performers, became the weakest of the 25 largest cryptocurrencies overnight. ZEC lost about a fifth of its value in 24 hours and slipped under $700 for the first time since August.

## What happened

- **Friday, November 20.** ZEC was already down about 14–15% by 13:00 UTC, the heaviest loss in the top 20, per U.Today. It traded near $843 on Friday morning; the 24-hour high was about $865.
- **Saturday, November 21.** Around 03:50 UTC ZEC was near $687, down about 21% in a day and 26% in a week (CoinGecko data via CryptoTicker). It was the lowest level since August 21.
- **Leverage.** CoinGlass counted about $14 million of ZEC positions liquidated in 24 hours, roughly 93% of them bets on a rise.

## Why it moved

We found no Zcash-specific trigger, and CryptoTicker reported none either. ZEC fell with the whole market: bitcoin dropped below $56,000, the total crypto market lost about 10% in a day, and spot bitcoin ETFs saw heavy outflows. Coins that rallied hardest this year tend to fall hardest when leveraged longs are flushed out. In the background, ZEC holders are voting until November 30 on moving 50,000 ZEC from the Zcash Community Grants treasury, and the Grayscale Zcash fund saw about $53 million of outflows in early November.
