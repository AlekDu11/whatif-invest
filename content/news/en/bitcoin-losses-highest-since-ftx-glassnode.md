Bitcoin holders are selling at a loss on a scale not seen since the FTX collapse in November 2022, on-chain analytics firm Glassnode said on November 24, calling it the first major capitulation of this bear market. Bitcoin is holding just under $60,000 ahead of US inflation data today.

## What happened

- **Losses.** Glassnode's ratio of realized losses to capital inflows nearly doubled in a week and hit its highest level since FTX; recent buyers took the heavier share.
- **ETF outflows.** US spot bitcoin ETFs lost $569.7 million on November 23 and $231.6 million on November 24 (SoSoValue), and about $4.3 billion over 20 trading days.
- **Price levels.** Bitcoin touched $55,300 on November 21. Glassnode watches $58,500 (the June closing low) and the "realized price", the average cost of all coins, near $53,200.
- **Today.** US PCE inflation for October and the second estimate of Q3 GDP come out at 13:30 UTC.
