Rain, the token of a prediction-market protocol on Arbitrum, was the best performer among the largest cryptocurrencies on Monday. It doubled in a day while most of the market fell. We found no announcement that explains the move.

## What happened

- **Monday, November 16.** By 08:00 UTC RAIN was up about 108% in 24 hours at $0.00755, per TokenPost. At 16:00 UTC it was still up about 98% at $0.00732. That made it the top gainer among the ~88 largest coins, while 58 of them were down.
- **Thin trading.** CoinDesk put RAIN's daily volume at about $26 million. That is small for a token ranked around 19th by market value, so a modest amount of buying can move the price a lot.
- **A bad week before that.** RAIN lost ground almost every day from November 8 to 10, dropping to about $0.00375. A large unlock of about 35.9 billion RAIN was scheduled for November 10, per KuCoin's weekly unlock list. Monday's jump brought the price roughly back to where it was at the start of the month.

## Why it moved

We found no Rain-specific news behind Monday's rally. CoinDesk also wrote that the token doubled "with no clear catalyst" and pointed to low volume. The most likely explanation is a rebound after the selling around the unlock, made sharper by thin liquidity.

The wider market gave no help. Bitcoin touched $62,100 on Sunday, its lowest since mid-July, and about $2 billion of positions were liquidated over the weekend. The Fear & Greed index fell to 12, "extreme fear".

Supply is the main thing to watch. About 714 billion RAIN are in circulation out of a 1.15 trillion maximum, so more than a third of the supply has yet to enter the market. Background: the token first took off in November 2025, when Nasdaq-listed Enlivex announced a $212 million RAIN treasury.
