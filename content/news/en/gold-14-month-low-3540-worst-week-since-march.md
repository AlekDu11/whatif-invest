Gold's sell-off deepened on Friday. Spot gold fell 2.3% to about $3,540 an ounce in Asian trading on November 20 — its lowest level since September 2025 — and is on course for a weekly drop of around 9%, the steepest since March. Two days ago it was still near $3,770.

## What happened

- **Futures fell harder.** US gold futures dropped about 4% to roughly $3,537.
- **Silver and platinum too.** Silver slid 6.6% to about $41.45 and platinum 3.4% to around $1,305.
- **The Fed is the driver.** Traders now price a roughly 97% chance of a 25-basis-point rate hike in December, according to CME FedWatch, after Fed minutes showed almost all officials expect more hikes. Higher rates make gold, which pays no interest, less attractive.
- **A strong dollar and forced selling.** The dollar sits near a 19-month high, and margin calls linked to the tech sell-off pushed some investors to sell what they could.
