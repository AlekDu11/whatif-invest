Nvidia is on track for its longest run of weekly declines in 18 years. The chipmaker's shares fell almost 4% in premarket trading on Friday, November 20, and a lower close for the week would make it seven weeks down in a row — a streak not seen since 2008.

## What happened

- **A weak year for the AI leader.** Through Thursday's close Nvidia was down about 21.7% in 2026, while the S&P 500 was still up about 4.8%. Its strong quarterly results this week did not stop the slide: on Thursday the stock swung from a 4.7% gain to a 3.3% loss, and California's attorney general served it with a subpoena the same day.
- **Analysts stay positive.** Of 65 brokerages covering the stock, 48 rate it a buy or better, with a median target of $233.50, per TokenPost.
- **The whole world is selling.** MSCI's All Country World index fell for a seventh session in a row, its longest losing run since September 2023. Korea's Kospi closed 5.2% lower and Japan's Nikkei 5.1%; before the US open, Nasdaq-100 futures were down about 1.7%.
- **Rates add pressure.** A Fed rate hike in December is fully priced in and the 10-year Treasury yield is near 5%.
