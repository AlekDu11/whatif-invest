The top US derivatives regulator has spelled out how decentralized trading venues could come onshore. Speaking at the Capitol Crypto Forum in Washington on November 19, CFTC Chairman Michael Selig said platforms such as Hyperliquid can offer derivatives to US retail traders if they either register as an exchange themselves or partner with one that already is.

## What happened

- **Same rules, any technology.** Selig said onchain venues can keep running on blockchains but must follow the same rules as traditional exchanges. In his words, a lawful derivative "should be regulated by the market regulator."
- **Backpack as the template.** He pointed to Backpack, which won three US licences earlier this week, as the first example of the registration route.
- **Perps are futures.** He said single-stock perpetual contracts count as futures, not security-based swaps, which keeps them under the CFTC.
- **Prediction markets.** The CFTC will work with the Justice Department against state challenges to federally registered prediction markets; it has already sued Utah, Arkansas and Louisiana.
