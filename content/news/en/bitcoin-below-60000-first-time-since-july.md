Bitcoin broke below $60,000 on Friday afternoon for the first time since early July. The drop came as US tech stocks fell hard for a second day, and it pushed the whole crypto market down about 8% in 24 hours.

## What happened

- **The level gave way around 15:45 UTC.** Bitcoin was near $63,300 on Thursday evening. On Friday it slipped under $60,000 and hit an intraday low of about $58,995 shortly after 16:00 UTC, per CoinGecko. The year's low is $58,566 from July 1.
- **Forced selling.** CoinGlass counted about $1.58 billion of liquidated positions in 24 hours, more than 90% of them bets on a rise. Over half a billion dollars were wiped out in a single hour as the price broke $60,000.
- **Ether fell harder.** ETH lost about 8% to roughly $1,640, its lowest since July. Total crypto market value dropped to about $2.07 trillion.
- **Same story as stocks.** Reuters linked the slide to doubts that huge AI spending will pay off, higher borrowing costs and US regulatory uncertainty. The Nasdaq 100 was down about 3% by late morning in New York, with chipmakers leading.
