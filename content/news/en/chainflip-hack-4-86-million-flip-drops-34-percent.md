Cross-chain swap protocol Chainflip lost about $4.86 million from its Ethereum vault on Saturday, November 21. Its FLIP token fell roughly 34%, and the team switched off deposits, withdrawals and swaps while it investigates.

## What happened

- **The theft.** At about 07:10 UTC an attacker took 520.2 ETH, 3.25 million USDC and 758,000 USDT from the Ethereum vault, per security monitors Decurity and Defimon Alerts.
- **Likely cause.** Early analysis points to a valid signature from the validators' shared signing key — a compromised key rather than a bug in the smart contract. The cause is not yet confirmed. The key needs 100 of 150 validators and rotates every three days.
- **Funds laundered fast.** The stolen money was sent to the Tornado Cash mixer in under 10 minutes, per AMLBot. Chainflip's Bitcoin and Solana vaults appeared unaffected.
- **Token hit.** FLIP's market value shrank from about $41.6 million to $27.1 million. Chainflip already lost about $736,000 in a Tron-related exploit in September.

## The bigger picture

FLIP now trades at {{price:flip}}, about 97% below its March 2024 peak of $9.48 — see the [from-ATH page]({lang}/ath/).
