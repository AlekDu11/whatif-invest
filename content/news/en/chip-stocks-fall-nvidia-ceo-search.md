The AI selloff picked up again in Asia on Monday. Taiwan's main index dropped more than 4% and TSMC lost about 4%, while Nasdaq 100 futures pointed lower, as investors weighed Nvidia's leadership vacuum one day before its quarterly results.

## What happened

- **Asian tech sold off.** Besides Taiwan, the tech-heavy indexes in Japan and South Korea also fell sharply. Nasdaq 100 futures were down about 0.5% after the cash index lost 1.2% on Friday.
- **Nvidia has no permanent CEO.** Jensen Huang resigned last week and former CFO Colette Kress is interim chief. The Financial Times reported that Huang has discussed a possible return and that Nvidia sounded out Arm CEO Rene Haas.
- **Washington weighed in.** On Sunday, US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Nvidia should look for a leader from outside the company.
- **A lot of value is gone.** Nvidia has shed about $1.9 trillion in market value since early October, per LSEG data. Results are due Tuesday, November 17.
