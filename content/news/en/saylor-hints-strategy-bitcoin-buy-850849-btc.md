Michael Saylor posted his familiar bitcoin purchase chart on Sunday with the caption "The dots aren't done", the hint that usually comes a day before Strategy announces a new buy. It would land in a rough spot: the company's 850,849 BTC cost an average of $75,452 each, while bitcoin spent the weekend around $60,000 after a drop to about $55,130.

## What happened

- **The hint.** Saylor's Sunday post shows Strategy's purchase history. It does not confirm a buy; the weekly filing usually arrives on Monday.
- **The stack.** As of November 15 Strategy held 850,849 BTC bought for about $64.2 billion. At roughly $60,400 that is worth about $51.4 billion, around 20% below cost.
- **Smaller buys lately.** In the week of November 9–15 it added just 485 BTC for $33.4 million, after 1,036 BTC the week before. It also spent $228 million buying back STRC preferred shares and sold MSTR stock to fund it.
- **Index pressure.** MSCI plans to drop Strategy from affected indexes after the November 30 close, which can force index funds to sell the shares.
