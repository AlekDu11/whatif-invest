Nvidia reports quarterly results after the US market close on Tuesday, November 17 — its first report since Jensen Huang stepped down, with CFO Colette Kress running the company as interim CEO. The stock goes into the numbers bruised: it fell 4.1% on Monday to $156.14 and lost about 12.6% over five sessions.

## What happened

- **A high bar.** Analysts expect revenue of roughly $108.8 billion, close to 90% more than a year earlier, and adjusted earnings of about $2.47 per share. Nvidia's own guidance was $108 billion, give or take 2%, with a 74% gross margin.
- **A big expected move.** Options traders are pricing a swing of about 7.8% in either direction after the report, according to UBS.
- **Asia sold chips first.** On Tuesday morning South Korea's KOSPI fell about 2%, Taiwan 1.4% and Japan's Nikkei 1%, after Washington tightened rules on Chinese access to AI computing, including remote access through overseas data centres.
- **US futures were calmer.** Nasdaq futures were up about 0.35% in Asian hours.
