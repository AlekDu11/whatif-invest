Dick's Sporting Goods was one of the best-performing big US stocks on Tuesday, November 24, while chip stocks fell. Shares rose about 15% by mid-morning New York time and about 17% by 18:00 UTC, near $157, after third-quarter results beat forecasts by a wide margin.

## What happened

- **Profit.** Adjusted earnings were $1.94 a share in the quarter to November 1, against about $1.42 expected by analysts (GAAP: $1.87).
- **Sales.** Revenue rose about 23% to more than $5.1 billion, mostly thanks to Foot Locker, which Dick's now owns.
- **Comparable sales.** Up 2.6% at Dick's own stores and 0.5% at Foot Locker — a small rise, but a sign that the struggling chain is growing again.
- **Outlook.** The company raised its full-year forecast to about $11.83 a share on more than $22 billion of sales, above Wall Street's estimates.

## Why it moved

This is an earnings move: profit beat forecasts by more than a third, the operating margin more than doubled to 4.7%, and the market had worried about Foot Locker dragging the group down. Retailers did well overall on Tuesday — Dollar Tree and Best Buy also rose — as investors moved out of AI stocks.
