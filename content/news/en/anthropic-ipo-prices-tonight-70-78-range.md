Anthropic, the AI company behind Claude, is due to set the final price of its initial public offering on the evening of November 18, with shares expected to start trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on Thursday, November 19. At the top of the $70–78 range it would raise about $64 billion, one of the largest listings ever.

## What happened

- **The terms.** 825 million shares are on offer, most of them newly issued, plus an option for underwriters to sell 123.75 million more.
- **The valuation.** With about 13.1 billion shares outstanding after the deal, the range values the company at roughly $916 billion to $1.02 trillion, and up to about $1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis. Earlier talk had been closer to $1.5–2 trillion.
- **Chip makers are buying in.** Seven compute suppliers agreed to buy $26 billion of separate non-voting shares at the IPO price, including about $10 billion each from Google and Nvidia.
- **The numbers behind it.** Revenue for the first nine months of 2026 was $33.2 billion, up from $2.5 billion a year earlier. The reported net loss was $104 billion, mostly a non-cash accounting charge.
