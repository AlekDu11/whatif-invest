Strategy, the largest corporate bitcoin holder, kept buying last week, but at a much slower pace. It added 485 BTC for about $33.4 million while bitcoin slipped below the average price the company has paid for its whole stack, and its shares fell about 8% in early Monday trading.

## What happened

- **A small buy.** Between November 9 and 15, Strategy bought 485 BTC at an average of $68,887. It now holds 850,849 BTC at an average cost of $75,452 per coin.
- **More went to buybacks than to bitcoin.** The company spent $228.1 million repurchasing its STRC preferred shares, paid for mostly by selling $244.7 million of MSTR common stock.
- **Crypto stocks slid.** MSTR traded near $102.50, Coinbase and MARA fell about 7%. MSCI is set to drop Strategy from its indexes after the close on November 30, so tracker funds have to sell.
- **Others are still buying.** Strive added 251 BTC (about $17.5 million), and Bitmine bought 7,653 ETH, bringing it to about 4.97% of all ether, close to its 5% goal.

## The numbers right now

Bitcoin trades at {{price:btc}}, about 49% below its all-time high of $126,080 (October 6, 2025). At this level Strategy's coins are worth less than it paid, a paper loss of about $9.6 billion. Ether is at {{price:eth}}, roughly 63% under its August 2025 record. See every coin's gap on our [from-ATH page]({lang}/ath/).

## What it means if you buy regularly

Strategy is, in effect, a giant DCA buyer, but one funded by new shares and debt, which magnifies every swing.
