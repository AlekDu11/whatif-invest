Bitway (BTW) is the strongest large coin of the weekend: it rose about 27% to $1.38 by 16:00 UTC on Saturday, November 28 (TokenPost), and our data showed close to +48% in 24 hours by the evening, while bitcoin and most of the market fell. The clearest trigger is a new listing: Korean exchange Bithumb added BTW to its won market on Friday.

## What happened

- **Nov 27, about 04:50 UTC.** Bithumb announced BTW for its Korean won (KRW) market; trading opened about 07:00 UTC (PANews, POPA Recon).
- **Nov 27, 16:00 UTC.** BTW was up about 13% at $1.10, the top gainer among large coins (TokenPost).
- **Nov 28, 16:00 UTC.** Another +27% to $1.38, again the top large-cap gainer, on a day when 68 of 89 big coins fell and WLFI lost more than half its value (TokenPost).
- **The week.** In our 7-day data BTW went from about $0.74 to about $1.41 — almost double.

## Why is Bitway going up?

New access for Korean traders is the only concrete catalyst we found; won-market listings on Bithumb or Upbit often bring a burst of local buying. We found no other Bitway announcement for November 28. Keep two things in mind: only about 15% of the 10 billion maximum supply is in circulation, so the value of all tokens (FDV) is several times the market cap, and BTW is still well below its October record. The wider market is weak — bitcoin is below $60,000 and the fear index is near its lows.
