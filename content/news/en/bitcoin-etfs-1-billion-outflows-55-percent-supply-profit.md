Money keeps leaving US spot bitcoin funds. On November 17 they recorded a net outflow of $239.9 million, bringing the five-day total to roughly $1 billion, while bitcoin hovered around $64,000. On-chain data shows how deep the drawdown now goes: only about 55% of all bitcoin is worth more than its owners paid.

## What happened

- **Outflows resumed.** BlackRock's IBIT accounted for most of Tuesday's selling with $209.5 million, followed by Grayscale's GBTC ($17.7 million) and ARK's ARKB ($13.1 million). The day before, the funds had taken in a small $7.9 million.
- **Still a big base.** Even after this run, total net inflows since the funds launched stand at about $54.5 billion.
- **Fewer holders in profit.** Glassnode's weekly report puts the share of supply in profit at 55.3%, down from 60.4% a week earlier; by late September, less than a quarter of supply had been underwater.
- **Mood near the bottom of the scale.** The Crypto Fear & Greed Index read 11 out of 100, close to its lowest levels.
