The US dollar is the strongest it has been in 19 months. The dollar index, which measures it against six major currencies, peaked at 103.69 in Asian trading on Tuesday, November 17 — its highest since April 2025 — as traders lock in bets that the Federal Reserve will raise rates in December.

## What happened

- **Rate bets drive it.** CME FedWatch data show more than 90% odds of a quarter-point hike at the Fed meeting that ends December 9, and close to 10% odds of a half-point move.
- **Others weaken.** The euro slipped to about $1.0985, its lowest since April 2025, and the yen trades near 160 per dollar — a level where Tokyo is believed to have stepped in earlier this year.
- **More Fed news ahead.** Fed Governor Christopher Waller speaks on Tuesday, US retail sales for October are due the same day, and minutes of the October Fed meeting come out on Wednesday.
- **Gold held up.** Spot gold was little changed at about $3,860 an ounce.
