The US Commerce Department has added seven companies, including Alibaba's Singapore subsidiary Apex Enterprise Solutions and six firms tied to Singapore-based Megaspeed International, to its Entity List. The move, announced on Monday, November 23, follows a long probe into whether Nvidia's AI chips were diverted to China against US export rules.

## What happened

- **Who is on the list.** Apex plus Megaspeed International and Swiftdata (Singapore), MSFusion, PT Megaspeed International and Speedmatrix (Malaysia) and Megaspeed (Thailand). From November 25 US suppliers need a licence to sell them technology, and requests will usually be refused.
- **The money trail.** Singapore company filings cited in reports show Apex prepaid Megaspeed up to about $474 million for computing capacity. Reuters reported Apex prepaid about $4.2 billion to Megaspeed and other firms in total.
- **Long investigation.** US officials began looking at Megaspeed in 2024 and asked Nvidia for its sales records in May 2025; Nvidia then put Megaspeed on an internal no-sell list. The Justice Department now leads the probe.
- **Denials.** Alibaba says Apex never diverted restricted chips, calls the listing a mistake and plans legal action. Megaspeed also denies wrongdoing.
