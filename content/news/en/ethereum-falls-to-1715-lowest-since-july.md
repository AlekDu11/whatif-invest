Ether slid to about $1,715 at 01:45 UTC on Sunday, November 15 — its lowest level since July — before bouncing back above $1,800 within a few hours. The coin has lost nearly a fifth of its value in seven days.

## What happened

- **A steep week.** Ether is down about 18.6% over seven days and about 25% over 30 days; it was still above $2,400 on November 5.
- **Money left the ETFs.** US spot ether funds saw net outflows of about $399 million from November 9 to 13, according to Farside Investors data.
- **Rate worries.** Futures markets priced in a high chance (around 88%) that the Fed raises rates in December, a headwind for risky assets.
- **Coins leaving Binance.** CryptoQuant data shows Binance's ether reserve near 2.9 million ETH, the lowest in almost five years — though withdrawals alone do not prove anyone is buying.
