Tech stocks were set for a strong open on Thursday, November 19, the day Anthropic's shares start trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. Nasdaq-100 futures were up about 2% early in the morning in New York, after Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share — above its $70–78 range — raising a record $81.3 billion.

## What happened

- **Futures up across the board.** Around 6 a.m. New York time, Nasdaq-100 futures were up about 2.1%, S&P 500 futures about 1.2% and Dow futures about 0.6–0.7%.
- **Chips led premarket.** Nvidia gained about 2%, AMD about 3%, Intel and Micron around 4.5–4.7%. Intel was also helped by news of its first outside customer for its 18A manufacturing process; Nvidia got a lift after President Trump said Blackwell chip sales to China could be allowed in two to three years.
- **Hints for the ANT open.** Pre-listing quotes pointed to a first trade roughly 20–29% above the $83 offer price, around $100–107 — indications only, not a guaranteed opening price.
- **Other movers.** Palo Alto Networks jumped about 10% premarket after beating estimates. US jobless claims came in at 203,000, a little above the 199,000 expected.
