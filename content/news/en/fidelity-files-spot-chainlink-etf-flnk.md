Fidelity has asked the SEC to approve a spot Chainlink fund, the Fidelity Chainlink Fund, which would trade on Nasdaq under the ticker FLNK and hold the LINK token directly. The S-1 filing, made on Wednesday, November 25, takes the asset manager beyond bitcoin, ether and solana at a time when investors are pulling record sums out of crypto ETFs.

## What happened

- **The filing.** Fidelity submitted a Form S-1, the first step to list an ETF. It names no launch date and no management fee yet, so approval and timing are open.
- **No staking.** The fund would hold LINK but not stake it, so shareholders would not receive staking rewards. Custody would sit with Anchorage Digital Bank, BitGo Bank & Trust and Coinbase Custody.
- **Part of a wider push.** Fidelity already runs spot ETFs for bitcoin (FBTC), ether (FETH) and solana (FSOL), and on November 24 its crypto trading app added AAVE, LINK and UNI.
- **Bad timing for flows.** US spot bitcoin ETFs lost a net $5.39 billion in November through the 25th, the worst month on record, and ether ETFs lost $1.30 billion (SoSoValue).
