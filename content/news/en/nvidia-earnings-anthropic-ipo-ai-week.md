The coming week is a big one for anyone with money in AI. Nvidia reports quarterly results on Tuesday, November 17, with its stock down more than 30% from its October peak, and Anthropic plans to price its long-awaited IPO a day later.

## What happened

- **Nvidia's bar is high.** Analysts expect revenue near $109 billion, up about 91% from a year earlier, and adjusted earnings of $2.47 per share. The company itself guided to about $108 billion and has beaten the top of its own forecast for 14 quarters in a row.
- **Margins are the worry.** Nvidia expects gross margin of about 74%, and warned it could slip to 71–72% next quarter because memory chips are getting more expensive.
- **Anthropic's IPO.** The Claude developer set a range of $70–78 per share for its Nasdaq listing under the ticker "ANT", with pricing planned for November 18 and trading from November 19. At the top of the range the deal would raise about $64 billion.
- **Nvidia is a buyer too.** Nvidia and Google are each set to buy about $10 billion of Anthropic stock at the IPO price.
