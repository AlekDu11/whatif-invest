Shares of the big US bitcoin miners had one of their worst days of the year on Friday, November 20. MARA Holdings fell 18.6%, and IREN, Hut 8, CleanSpark and Cipher lost 16–18% each, hit at once by a falling bitcoin and a sell-off in AI infrastructure stocks.

## What happened

- **Friday, November 20 (US session, 14:30–21:00 UTC):** MARA closed down 18.6%, IREN about 18%, Hut 8 about 17.7%, CleanSpark about 16.3% and Cipher about 16.1%. Core Scientific and Bitdeer also lost more than 13%.
- **Bitcoin broke below $60,000** during US trading for the first time since July, and touched about $55,250 on Saturday before bouncing.
- **The Nasdaq fell 5.3% to 22,618** on Friday, ending a week down about 10%. Nvidia lost 14% that day after a Reuters report of an SEC inquiry into its accounting.

## Why it moved

Two forces hit miners at once. Their revenue is paid in bitcoin, so a lower coin price cuts income directly. And many of them now rent power and data centres to AI companies: IREN's AI cloud revenue recently overtook its mining revenue. That makes them trade like AI infrastructure stocks, which were sold hard all week. We found no MARA-specific news behind Friday's drop. A proposed data-centre moratorium in Texas, where many miners operate, adds political risk, Startup Fortune notes.
