Citi has trimmed its crypto forecasts again. The bank's 12-month target for bitcoin is now $99,532, down about 12% from $113,000, and its ether target falls to $2,838 from $3,028. The cut landed on a weak day: bitcoin touched about $62,200 on Wednesday morning in New York.

## What happened

- **Why Citi turned more cautious.** Bitcoin has dropped below its 200-day moving average, overall leverage in the market has shrunk, and bitcoin now moves more in step with the S&P 500 than with gold, which leaves it more exposed to stock sell-offs. Analyst Alex Saunders moved Citi's near-term view on fund inflows to neutral.
- **ETF money is leaving again.** US spot bitcoin ETFs lost about $240 million on November 17, most of it from BlackRock's IBIT, and roughly $3.2 billion over 13 sessions since October 30.
- **Citi's target has swung a lot this year.** It was $143,000 in December 2025, $112,000 in March, $82,000 in the summer and $113,000 again in October.
- **Still above the market.** Even after the cut, the new target sits about 55% above where bitcoin traded on Wednesday.
