Arm Holdings, the British chip designer whose technology sits inside almost every smartphone and a growing number of AI servers, lost 14% in a single session on Friday as the sell-off in AI and semiconductor shares turned into a rout.

## What happened

- **Friday, November 20.** ARM fell $24.80 to close at $152.37 (US close, 21:00 UTC), per Seeking Alpha.
- **The whole sector fell.** The Technology Select Sector SPDR (XLK) lost more than 7%, and semiconductor peers dropped 12–15%. Nvidia slid 14% after reports of an SEC accounting probe.
- **A bad month.** By our data ARM is down about 20% in a week and more than 25% since the end of October.

## Why it moved

We found no Arm-specific news behind Friday's drop. Coverage points to the broad tech sell-off: worries about AI valuations after Anthropic's Nasdaq debut, the Nvidia probe, and expectations of further Fed rate hikes. Arm trades at a high valuation that depends on AI growth, so it tends to move more than the market when sentiment turns.
