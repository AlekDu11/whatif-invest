Anthropic's first full day as a public company ended well below its opening pop, and the slide continued into Friday. The stock (ticker ANT) closed Thursday at $97.17 — still about 17% above its $83 IPO price — and traded roughly 6% lower in early premarket trading on November 20, while the wider AI trade kept losing ground.

## What happened

- **A fading debut.** ANT opened at $111.50 on Thursday, dipped toward $93 during the session and settled at $97.17.
- **Big tech paid for it.** Six AI-linked stocks — Nvidia, Alphabet, Amazon, Microsoft, AMD and CoreWeave — lost about $496 billion of market value on Thursday combined, by Trefis's count. Nvidia fell 3.3% to $145.98 and AMD 7.7% to $489.19.
- **Indexes slid.** The S&P 500 dropped about 2% on Thursday, its sixth straight decline; on Friday morning Nasdaq-100 futures were down 1–1.5% and Nvidia was about 2.5% lower before the bell.
- **The worry is spending.** Anthropic's filing lists roughly $900 billion of computing commitments through 2045, which feeds the debate over whether AI investment will pay off.
