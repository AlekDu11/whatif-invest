Pump.fun's PUMP token gained roughly 13% in a day after the memecoin launchpad announced a new fee aimed at "bundled" token launches. Half of the money it brings in will be used to buy back and burn PUMP.

## What happened

- **November 25 — the announcement.** In a post on X, Pump.fun said it will charge a flat percentage fee on buys and sells made in the very first block of a new token (the "Block0" window). Trades made directly from the token creator's own wallet are exempt; other wallets, including ones the creator controls, pay.
- **The aim.** Block0 is where "bundlers" and snipers buy a big share of a new token in the same block it is created, then sell into later buyers. The fee makes that tactic more expensive. The exact rate has not been published yet; Pump.fun says it will start low and announce it before launch.
- **Where the money goes.** 50% goes to automated PUMP buybacks and burns, the other 50% to product development, community rewards and trading competitions. The fee starts on **December 4**.
- **November 26 — the price.** By about 10:00 UTC PUMP traded near $0.0027, up about 12–13% in 24 hours, with roughly $115 million of trading volume.

## Why it moved

The fee is the only PUMP-specific news we found, and it directly adds a new source of buying for the token. The wider market helped too: bitcoin climbed back above $60,000 in thin Thanksgiving trading, and Solana rose about 4%. Pump.fun has already spent about $463.5 million buying back and burning PUMP by late September, roughly 17% of the original supply.
