Public bitcoin miners are turning into AI landlords, and they are paying for it with their coins. Data released by Glassnode on November 18 shows a group of six listed miners has sold close to half of its bitcoin holdings since January 2026, while bitcoin itself has fallen to about $62,000.

## What happened

- **Who is selling.** Glassnode tracks CleanSpark, Hut 8, American Bitcoin, Cango, Riot Platforms and MARA Holdings. Together they have cut their BTC reserves by roughly 50% this year. The figure covers this group only, not every miner.
- **The two biggest sellers.** MARA sold about 26,984 BTC for $1.9 billion in the first nine months of 2026 and still held 34,012 BTC at the end of September. Riot sold 20,264 BTC for about $1.5 billion, mostly to fund data centers, and was left with 2,418 BTC.
- **Why.** Mining income has shrunk since the 2024 halving cut block rewards, and fees are low. Renting out power and space to AI customers pays more steadily: CoinShares estimates about 30% of listed miners' revenue already comes from AI and computing, possibly 70% by the end of 2026.
