Internet Computer (ICP), the token of the blockchain built by the DFINITY Foundation, bounced hard on Monday. It came close to its all-time low last week and was one of the few large coins rising while most of the market fell.

## What happened

- **Saturday, November 14.** ICP fell more than 15% in a single session, sliding toward $2.10–2.20, as the whole crypto market sold off.
- **Monday, November 16.** ICP gained about 13.7% in four hours on Binance, from $2.23 to $2.54 at about 17:00 UTC. That put it up about 16.8% in 24 hours, per TokenPost. Earlier in the day it was already among the top gainers in the top ~90 coins, up about 13%.
- **Not a short squeeze.** In the hour of the jump, liquidations on Binance, Bybit and OKX were tiny, about $10,000 of short positions. Funding rates had been neutral to negative before the move, so few traders were betting heavily on a rise.

## Why it moved

There is no single confirmed catalyst. CaptainAltcoin lists a few things that may have helped. DFINITY began giving waitlisted users access to its "Cloud Engines" service on November 14, a limited rollout aimed at business software and AI workloads. Network data shows about 4,000+ transactions per second, though transaction counts alone do not prove real usage. And after a steep sell-off there was room for a relief bounce.

The backdrop was weak. Bitcoin touched $62,100 on Sunday, its lowest since mid-July, about $2 billion of positions were liquidated over the weekend, and the Fear & Greed index fell to 12. Even after Monday's jump, ICP is still down on the week and roughly 25% over the past month.
