Investors keep pulling money out of US spot bitcoin ETFs. The funds recorded their seventh straight trading day of net outflows on Friday, November 13, bringing November's total to roughly $2.1 billion as bitcoin fell to around $64,500.

## What happened

- **Friday's outflow:** about $185 million left the funds, per Decrypt's ETF tracker.
- **Two bad weeks:** around $829 million left this week, after nearly $1.3 billion the week before.
- **Smaller pile:** total assets in the funds dropped to about $94.9 billion, below the $100 billion mark they held for much of October. Net inflows since the January 2024 launch are still about $55.3 billion.
- **Ether funds:** US ether ETFs ended a six-day outflow streak with a small $2.3 million inflow on Friday, but are still down about $702 million for the month.
