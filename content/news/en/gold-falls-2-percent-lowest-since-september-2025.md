Gold had its sharpest drop in weeks on Wednesday. Spot prices fell about 2% to roughly $3,772–3,778 an ounce in Asian and early European trading on November 18, the lowest level since September 2025, as traders bet on another US rate hike next month.

## What happened

- **Metals fell across the board.** Spot silver lost about 3% to around $46, while platinum and palladium each dropped roughly 2%.
- **A December hike is almost fully priced.** CME FedWatch showed about a 97% chance of a quarter-point Fed rate rise in December, after New York Fed President John Williams said rates likely need to go higher to contain inflation.
- **The dollar stays strong.** It hovered near a 19-month high, and Treasury yields rose, both of which usually weigh on gold.
- **Risk appetite is weak.** Analysts also pointed to the sell-off in AI stocks spreading to commodities. Minutes of the Fed's October meeting are due at 19:00 GMT.
