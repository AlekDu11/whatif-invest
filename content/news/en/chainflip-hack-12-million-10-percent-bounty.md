The Chainflip hack is bigger than first reported. The cross-chain swap protocol now says about $12 million was stolen on November 21 — not $4.86 million — because the attacker also emptied bitcoin from its vaults. Chainflip is offering a 10% "white-hat" bounty if the rest comes back by Monday evening.

## What happened

- **Full list of losses.** In a message to the attacker, Chainflip listed 122.86 BTC, 520.22 ETH, about 3.25 million USDC and 758,141 USDT. At Sunday prices that is roughly $12.1 million. The first $4.86 million estimate only covered the Ethereum vault.
- **The offer.** The attacker can keep 10% if 90% is returned by 18:38 UTC on November 23. Chainflip says it would then treat the case as a white-hat recovery and drop law-enforcement complaints.
- **How it happened.** A cryptographic signing key shared by the validators was exposed; Chainflip says it found the mechanism but has not published details. The attacker funded a wallet through Tornado Cash on November 11 and registered a validator on November 20.
- **Protocol frozen.** Chainflip is in full safe mode: deposits, withdrawals, swaps and quotes are off. There is no compensation plan for liquidity providers yet. FLIP lost about 43–45% in 24 hours, per CoinGecko data.
