Fed Chair Kevin Warsh speaks on Monday at noon New York time (17:00 UTC), his first public remarks since the late-October rate decision. Markets already fully price a rate hike in December after last week's hotter-than-expected inflation data, so any hint about how far the Fed may go could move bitcoin, stocks and gold.

## What happened

- **A hike is the base case.** CME FedWatch shows a December increase as fully priced, with about an 8% chance of a bigger half-point move. The policy range is 3.75–4.00%.
- **A busy Fed week.** Warsh opens a San Francisco Fed conference on productivity and jobs; Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack also speaks Monday. Minutes of the October meeting, decided by a 10-2 vote, arrive Wednesday, and BofA expects them to show a deeper split.
- **Yields are high.** The 10-year Treasury yield is around 5.1%, and the dollar index is above 103.
- **Risk assets are weak.** Bitcoin is on its sixth straight weekly loss and down about 17% in November; gold trades near $3,860 an ounce.

## Why it matters for your money

When cash and bonds pay more, assets that pay no interest, such as bitcoin and gold, tend to lose some appeal.
