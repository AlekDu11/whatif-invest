Nvidia sold a quarter of its Intel shares in the third quarter and bought more SpaceX, making the rocket and satellite company its largest disclosed stock investment. The filing landed on Monday, November 16, one day before Nvidia reports its own quarterly results after the US close, and Intel shares fell more than 3% before the opening bell on Tuesday.

## What happened

- **Intel trimmed by 25%.** Nvidia's 13F filing with the SEC shows 161.1 million Intel shares at September 30, down from 214.8 million at the end of June. Nvidia agreed in September 2025 to invest $5 billion in Intel at $23.28 a share.
- **SpaceX moves to the top.** The SpaceX stake rose to 130.5 million shares from 122.8 million, worth about $19.7 billion at the end of September, slightly ahead of Intel at about $19.4 billion.
- **Others cut too.** SpaceX was the only one of eight listed holdings where Nvidia added shares; it also trimmed Nokia by 56% and Synopsys by 10%. Intel and SpaceX make up more than 80% of the $48.3 billion disclosed portfolio.
- **Earnings tonight.** Nvidia reports fiscal Q3 results after the close on November 17; in August it guided to revenue of about $108 billion.
