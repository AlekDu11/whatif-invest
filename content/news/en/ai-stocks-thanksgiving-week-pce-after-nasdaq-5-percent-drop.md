The AI trade heads into a holiday-shortened week bruised. The Nasdaq Composite lost 5.28% last week to 22,617.73, its worst stretch since 2020, after a 14% slide took Nvidia to $125.53 and Anthropic's market debut failed to lift the sector. US markets close on Thursday for Thanksgiving and shut early on Friday, so fewer trading hours could mean sharper moves.

## What happened

- **Last week's damage.** The S&P 500 fell 3.70% to 6,910.39 and the Dow 1.93%. The VIX fear gauge jumped 26% to 35.89 and the 10-year Treasury yield sits near 4.91%.
- **AI software reports.** Workday and Autodesk report after the close on Tuesday, November 24 — a test of whether companies still pay for software as AI changes it. Analog Devices and Medtronic report before Tuesday's open.
- **Inflation data.** On Wednesday at 13:30 UTC come October PCE prices, the Fed's preferred inflation gauge, plus a second look at Q3 GDP and durable goods. Hot numbers would add to the rate-hike worries behind the sell-off.
- **Holiday hours.** Monday brings flash PMIs; markets are closed Thursday, and the NYSE closes at 18:00 UTC on Friday.
