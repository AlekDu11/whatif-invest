Nvidia handed back a large part of its historic rally on Tuesday, November 24. A day after the stock jumped 31% on Jensen Huang's return as CEO, it was down about 8% in afternoon trading, and other chip stocks fell even harder.

## What happened

- **Monday, November 23.** Nvidia closed at $165.05, up 31.5% — its biggest one-day gain in market value ever, roughly $950 billion, per StockAnalysis.
- **Tuesday, November 24.** Shares were down about 5% before the open and about 8% by 17:00 UTC, near $152.
- **Other chip stocks.** AMD and Micron were each down about 10%, Broadcom and Arm about 4%.
- **The market.** Around 17:00 UTC the Nasdaq was down about 2.2% and the S&P 500 about 1%, while the Dow was up about 0.4%.

## Why it moved

We found no single new piece of bad news about Nvidia itself. After a record day, traders took profits: Yahoo Finance called it a classic "turnaround Tuesday". Questions also remain open. Bank of America noted that the reinstatement does not end the scrutiny of Huang and of Nvidia's export controls, and Huang is due to testify at a public Senate Banking hearing on December 3. On top of that, a WSJ report about new US tariffs on China and other countries hit risk assets, and markets price about 95% odds of a Fed rate hike in December.
