**Strategy** — called MicroStrategy until 2025 — is the company that holds more bitcoin than any other. It was founded in 1989 by Michael Saylor as a business-software firm, and in August 2020 it began putting its cash into [Bitcoin]({lang}/btc/). Since then buying bitcoin has become its main business: as of 15 November 2026 it held 850,849 BTC, about 4% of all bitcoin that will ever exist.

> **Key takeaways**
> - Strategy holds bitcoin on its balance sheet; its [stock]({lang}/mstr/) behaves like a leveraged bet on the bitcoin price.
> - It pays for new bitcoin mostly by selling new shares, convertible bonds and preferred stock — not from profits.
> - Its average purchase price was about $75,450 per bitcoin in November 2026.
> - Many companies around the world copied the idea of a "bitcoin treasury" in 2024–2026.

## How it works

Strategy raises money from investors — by issuing new shares, convertible bonds and several kinds of preferred stock that pay a fixed dividend — and uses it to buy bitcoin, usually announcing purchases every Monday. When its shares trade at more than the value of the bitcoin it holds, selling new shares lets it buy more bitcoin per share; when that premium shrinks, raising money gets harder. Michael Saylor, now executive chairman, is the face of the strategy and one of bitcoin's loudest supporters.

## Why it matters for bitcoin

For years Strategy was one of the steadiest buyers in the market. Its purchases, and the many companies that copied it, became part of the bitcoin story — and a risk: if such companies ever had to sell, they could add to a fall.

## Main risks

- **Bitcoin price:** the company's value rises and falls with bitcoin, usually even more sharply.
- **Debt and dividends:** bonds and preferred stock must be paid; in a long bear market this could force hard choices.
- **Dilution:** issuing new shares spreads ownership more thinly among existing shareholders.
- **Premium can vanish:** the stock can trade below the value of its bitcoin.

## What it means for you

Buying Strategy shares is not the same as buying bitcoin: you also take on the company's debt, costs and decisions. Many people who want bitcoin itself buy it directly or through a spot ETF instead.
