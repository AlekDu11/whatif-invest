Saturday, November 21 was a crypto-only day — Wall Street was closed — and it had two halves. Early in the day Bitcoin fell to about **$55,130**, its lowest level since September 2024, as forced selling continued after Friday's stock rout. Then it climbed more than **9%** and ended the day near **$60,200**, up **1.8%** in 24 hours (live: **{{price:btc}}**).

> **Key takeaways**
> - Bitcoin +1.8% to about $60,206 and Ethereum +3.4% to $1,681 in 24 hours; Bitcoin is still −6.9% for the week.
> - 27 of 46 coins worth $1 billion or more rose, after only 1 of 45 on Friday.
> - About $1.8 billion of leveraged crypto bets were wiped out in 24 hours, $1.3 billion of them bets on a rise (CoinGlass via CoinDesk).
> - The Crypto Fear & Greed Index reads 12/100 — "extreme fear" — the same as a day ago and close to 13 a week ago.

## The big picture: a weekend bounce, not a new trend

Stocks, gold and oil did not trade on Saturday, so Friday's closes still stand: the S&P 500 fund SPY lost **8.6%** over the week, the Nasdaq-100 fund QQQ **10.5%**, gold **9.5%** and silver **16.3%**. Brent oil ended Friday near $92.6.

Crypto moved on its own. Total crypto market value was about **$2.09 trillion** late on Saturday (CoinGecko), with Bitcoin's share at **57.9%**. The rebound was broad but small: most large coins gained 1–3%. Analysts at Glassnode said a cluster of leveraged positions between $59,000 and $62,000 had been cleared, removing a large source of forced selling. CryptoQuant called the drop "extreme capitulation" — holders locked in more than $11 billion of losses in seven days.

The mood did not change. Fear & Greed stayed at 12, near its lowest readings of the year, and US spot Bitcoin ETFs lost about $1.1 billion over the past week.

## Biggest winners

Among coins worth $1 billion or more, in 24 hours:

- [Venice Token]({lang}/vvv/) **+18.3%** to about $24.5 — the biggest gain; we found no confirmed news behind it.
- [Uniswap]({lang}/uni/) **+6.1%** — no single cause found; DeFi tokens bounced with the market.
- [Zcash]({lang}/zec/) **+4.9%** to about $784 — the biggest move among the 20 largest assets, a rebound after it fell about 21% and dipped below $700 earlier ([our brief]({lang}/news/zcash-falls-21-percent-below-700-november-2026/)).
- [Bitway]({lang}/btw/) **+3.7%** — no clear reason.
- [Ethereum]({lang}/eth/) **+3.4%** — the best of the very large coins, after a −16% week.

## Biggest losers

- [MemeCore]({lang}/m/) **−10.9%** — the weakest $1B+ coin; we found no specific news.
- [Internet Computer]({lang}/icp/) **−6.7%** — no clear reason.
- [Polkadot]({lang}/dot/) **−3.7%** and [Aster]({lang}/aster/) **−3.2%** — missed the rebound, no single cause found.
- [Gram (prev. Toncoin)]({lang}/gram/) **−3.0%** and [Litecoin]({lang}/ltc/) **−2.4%** — small moves against the trend.
- Outside the $1B group, Chainflip's FLIP fell about a third after a hack (see below).

## The news that moved markets

1. **Bitcoin's two-year low and rebound.** The low of $55,130 came early on Saturday; by the afternoon Bitcoin was back above $60,000 ([brief]({lang}/news/bitcoin-bounces-back-above-60000-from-55130-low/)).
2. **Big holders under water.** At Saturday's low, Strategy and BitMine together showed a paper loss of about $27.9 billion on their Bitcoin and Ether ([brief]({lang}/news/strategy-bitmine-27-9-billion-paper-loss/)).
3. **Chainflip hack.** About $4.86 million was drained from the cross-chain swap protocol's Ethereum vault; swaps were paused ([brief]({lang}/news/chainflip-hack-4-86-million-flip-drops-34-percent/)).
4. **ETF money keeps leaving.** XRP funds are heading for their worst month of outflows, and Bitcoin funds lost about $1.1 billion in a week ([brief]({lang}/news/xrp-etfs-on-track-worst-month-outflows/)).
5. **Kraken flash drop.** Bitcoin briefly hit C$24,250 on Kraken's Canadian-dollar market; the exchange said its systems worked correctly and the trades stand.
