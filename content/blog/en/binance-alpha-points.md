Binance Alpha gives free tokens of early-stage projects to users with enough **Alpha Points** — and in November 2026 the bar was **250 to 253 points collected over a rolling 15 days**. Points come from the balance you keep on Binance and from buying Alpha tokens. That sounds simple, but reaching 250 usually means buying thousands of dollars a day of small, risky tokens, and the fees and price swings can easily cost more than the airdrop pays.

> **Key takeaways**
> - Balance gives 1 to 4 points a day ($100 to $100,000+), volume gives 1 point at $2 bought and +1 for every doubling ($1,024 = 10 points); points expire after 15 days.
> - On November 19, 2026 users with 250+ points could claim 280 ZEST, paying 15 points; on November 24 the threshold was 253 points, first come, first served.
> - Alpha tokens are not listed on Binance spot, cannot be withdrawn from the exchange and, in Binance's own words, can lose all their value.

## Points are a 15-day score of balance and buying

Binance counts two kinds of points every day at 23:59:59 UTC. **Balance points:** $100–999 of eligible assets on Binance and Binance Wallet (Keyless) gives 1 point a day, $1,000–9,999 gives 2, $10,000–99,999 gives 3, and $100,000 or more gives 4. **Volume points:** buying Alpha tokens for $2 gives 1 point, $4 gives 2, $8 gives 3, and each further doubling adds one more — $1,024 a day is 10 points, $32,768 a day is 15. Only purchases paid with tokens listed on Binance spot count; selling does not take points away.

Your score is the sum of the last 15 days. Each day's points drop off exactly 15 days later. Since October 22, 2025, volume points do not count on days when your balance is under $100.

## Recent thresholds show how high the bar is

A balance of $1,000–9,999 earns 2 × 15 = 30 points in a window. To reach 250, the other 220 points must come from buying — about 15 points a day, which means around **$16,000–33,000 of Alpha-token purchases every day**. Bigger balances lower that a little, not a lot. This is why Alpha airdrops reward heavy traders, and why the threshold tends to rise when more people chase it.

Each event has its own rules. On November 6, 2026, during a system upgrade, users with 200+ points shared a $350,000 pool automatically, with no points spent. The November 19 ZEST drop needed 250 points, cost 15 points, had to be confirmed within 24 hours, and lowered the threshold by 5 points every 5 minutes if tokens were left. The November 24 drop needed 253 points and was first come, first served. Since June 2025 Binance uses two phases: high scorers first, then a lower bar for everyone else.

## How people take part

1. **Check that Binance serves your country.** Binance.com does not serve the US (Binance.US is a separate company) or the UK, and Alpha may not be available in your region. If so, skip it — never use a VPN; it breaks the terms and can freeze the account.
2. **Verify your identity** and use your main account; sub-account balances are merged into it, and splitting balances to gain points is not allowed.
3. **Keep at least $100 of eligible assets** on Binance or in Binance Wallet (Keyless) so that volume points count at all.
4. **Watch only official channels** — binance.com announcements and Binance's verified X accounts — for each airdrop's threshold, time and claim window.
5. **Claim on the Alpha event page** inside Binance and confirm within the stated time. Points are spent the moment you confirm.

Fake "Alpha airdrop checker" sites and DMs that ask you to connect a wallet or share a code are scams. Our [9 airdrop safety rules]({lang}/blog/airdrop-safety-rules/) explain how to spot them.

## Most of the cost is hidden in the trading

Every purchase pays a trading fee and slippage on thin order books, and the tokens you buy for volume can drop while you hold them. People often buy and sell the same stablecoin-like Alpha token to keep that loss small, but it is never zero, and repeated $16,000 rounds add up. The airdrop itself is often worth tens of dollars and is paid in a brand-new token. Binance can also change thresholds and point rules at any time without notice.

## What it means if you buy regularly

Alpha points reward daily trading volume, which is the opposite of calm regular buying. If you already keep a balance on Binance, the 1–4 balance points come for free, but they alone never reach 250. Chasing the rest turns a long-term plan into a daily trading habit with real costs.
