The largest Italian companies by market cap are **UniCredit, Intesa Sanpaolo and Enel**, followed by Eni, Ferrari, Generali, Poste Italiane, Monte dei Paschi, Leonardo and Prysmian. **$10 a day in each of these top 10 companies for five years turned ~$182,600 into ~$444,200 (+143%)** in US dollars — more than three times the result of the same plan in the S&P 500 ({{dcaroi:spy:5}}), driven by the banks.

> **Key takeaways**
> - The top 10 Italian companies by market value (Euronext Milan, November 2026): UniCredit, Intesa Sanpaolo, Enel, Eni, Ferrari, Generali, Poste Italiane, Monte dei Paschi di Siena, Leonardo, Prysmian.
> - Two banks lead: UniCredit (~€112 billion) and Intesa Sanpaolo (~€99 billion) are each worth more than Ferrari and Eni.
> - $10 a day in each for 5 years: **+143%**; across all 24 Italian stocks we track with 5 years of data: **+92.6%**, and **15 of 24** beat the S&P 500. Over the last year the top 10 made **+2.0%**.
> - Seven banks together made **+176%**, defence and cables (Leonardo, Prysmian) **+179%** — while the famous brands (Ferrari, Stellantis, Moncler, Campari, Telecom Italia) made only **+3%**.

## The top 10 largest Italian companies by market cap

The ranking of the biggest companies listed on Euronext Milan (Borsa Italiana) by market value (share price × shares), from Yahoo Finance data in November 2026, among the Italian stocks we track. Live prices are on each company's page.

1. **[UniCredit]({lang}/ucg/)** — Italy's largest bank by market value, also big in Germany, Austria and Central Europe. {{price:ucg}}
2. **[Intesa Sanpaolo]({lang}/isp/)** — Italy's largest bank by assets and domestic customers. {{price:isp}}
3. **[Enel]({lang}/enel/)** — electricity and renewables in Italy, Spain (Endesa) and Latin America. {{price:enel}}
4. **[Eni]({lang}/eni/)** — oil and gas, refining and the Plenitude energy retail business.
5. **[Ferrari]({lang}/race/)** — sports cars and Formula 1, also listed in New York.
6. **[Generali]({lang}/g/)** — one of Europe's largest insurers.
7. **[Poste Italiane]({lang}/pst/)** — post, savings, insurance and payments.
8. **[Monte dei Paschi di Siena]({lang}/bmps/)** — the world's oldest bank still operating; it took control of Mediobanca in 2025.
9. **[Leonardo]({lang}/ldo-it/)** — helicopters, defence electronics and aerospace.
10. **[Prysmian]({lang}/pry/)** — the world's largest maker of power and telecom cables.

Next come Tenaris, Mediobanca, Banco BPM, Snam, Terna, Banca Mediolanum, FinecoBank, Telecom Italia, Moncler and Stellantis. Some large Italian groups are not on this list: Ferrero, Armani and Barilla are private, Exor (the Agnelli family holding) is listed in Amsterdam, and a few Milan-listed companies such as Unipol, BPER and Recordati are not yet tracked on our site.

## How to buy Italian stocks

- **Euronext Milan** (formerly Borsa Italiana's MTA) trades 9:00–17:30 Milan time on weekdays, with opening and closing auctions; prices are in euros.
- The main index is the **FTSE MIB** (40 large companies); an FTSE MIB ETF buys them in one go, and European or eurozone funds hold the big names too.
- Most brokers that offer European markets include Milan. Ferrari (RACE), Eni (E), Stellantis (STLA) and Tenaris (TS) are also listed in New York. Check what your broker offers and its currency fees.
- See every Italian stock we track, sorted by market cap: [Italy stocks]({lang}/stocks-italy/).

## What it means if you buy regularly

Regular buying in the biggest Italian companies more than doubled the money over five years — but almost all of that came from banks and defence, in a period of rising interest rates and military spending that may not repeat. Buying the famous brands every day barely broke even, and Stellantis lost most of the money. A broad FTSE MIB or European fund spreads that sector risk instead of betting on one theme. Past results, not a forecast.
