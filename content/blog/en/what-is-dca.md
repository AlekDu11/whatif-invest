**DCA (dollar-cost averaging)** means buying an asset for the same amount at regular intervals — daily, weekly or monthly — whatever the price.

## How it works

Say you buy $100 every month. When the price is high you get fewer units; when it is low you get more. Over time your average purchase price ends up below the market's average price.

## Pros of DCA

1. No need to time the market.
2. Less stress in crashes — you are buying cheaper.
3. Easy to automate on an exchange or with a broker.

## Cons of DCA

1. In a market that only goes up, a lump sum at the start often wins.
2. Fees on frequent small buys can eat into returns — choose low-fee platforms.

## How to start

Pick an asset, an amount and a frequency, and check the historical result in the [calculator]({lang}/compare/). Then set up automatic buys on an exchange or with a broker.
