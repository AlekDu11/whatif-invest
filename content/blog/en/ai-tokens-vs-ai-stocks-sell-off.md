In the worst week for AI stocks since 2008, AI crypto tokens barely moved. From 16 to 20 November Nvidia lost about **23%**, Micron about **24%** and the chip ETF SMH about **18%**, while Bittensor (TAO) slipped only about **1.4%** and Render about **2.9%** over the seven days to 22 November. The reason is not that AI tokens became safe: most of them had already done their falling, weeks earlier and much deeper.

> **Key takeaways**
> - Week to 20 November on our data: Nvidia −23%, Micron −24%, AMD −21%, CoreWeave −26%, SMH −18%. Seven days to 22 November: TAO −1.4%, Render −2.9%, NEAR −6.4%, Bitcoin −6.5%.
> - Not every AI token held up: FET fell about 19% and Virtuals Protocol about 16% in the same week.
> - Since the end of October TAO is still down about 20%, NEAR and FET about 33%, and TAO sits about 72% below its best monthly close, against about 45% for Nvidia.

## What happened to AI stocks this week

The Nasdaq Composite fell about 10% from Monday to Friday and the Philadelphia chip index about 17%, according to the Neo Reports weekly review. Nvidia beat its forecasts on Wednesday, but the stock was hit by worries about its leadership change and, on Friday, by a Reuters report of an SEC inquiry into possible accounting issues. It fell about 14% that day alone. Anthropic's record IPO on Thursday drew money towards one new name while the rest of the sector sold off.

Behind it sit bigger questions: the four largest cloud companies spent about $467 billion on capex (spending on data centres and chips) in the first nine months of the year, much of it financed with debt, and markets now price a Federal Reserve rate **hike** in December.

## Why AI tokens did not follow Nvidia down

**They had already fallen.** Bittensor dropped from about $285 in mid-October to around $185 by mid-November, and by 8 November it had closed six weeks in a row lower, according to a CoinStats news summary. Much of the selling that hit Nvidia this week had hit TAO a month earlier.

**They are not a claim on chip profits.** A share of Nvidia is a piece of a company whose earnings depend on data-centre spending. A token like TAO or RENDER is used to pay for, or reward, work on a crypto network: training AI models on Bittensor, renting GPU time on Render. Its price moves mostly with crypto demand and speculation, not with Nvidia's quarterly report.

**Crypto set the tone.** Bitcoin fell about 6.5% in the same seven days and dropped below $60,000 during the week. AI tokens tracked that broad crypto mood more closely than they tracked chip stocks.

We found no single token-specific reason for TAO's resilience. One possible support is a filing reported by CoinStats: in October Grayscale amended its plan to turn its Bittensor Trust into a spot ETF, with no approval date yet.

Two lessons from this week. First, one calm week says little about risk: TAO's record high is {{ath:tao}} ({{athd:tao}}), and Render's is {{ath:render}}, far above today's prices. Second, "AI exposure" means very different things. A chip ETF follows company profits; an AI token follows a crypto network and crypto sentiment. Holding both is not the same as spreading risk, because both can fall at once, as they did in October.

Each $10 now buys more TAO or more Nvidia than it did a month ago, which lowers your average price. That only helps if prices recover, and nobody knows when or whether they will.

## Two views on what comes next

**Why some see AI tokens as more resilient now.** Weak holders sold in October, the tokens are 70–97% below their peaks, and Bittensor networks still report paying customers for GPU rentals. Prices that have already fallen a long way sometimes react less to bad news.

**Why others see more risk.** Many AI tokens have little revenue behind them, new tokens keep diluting supply, and crypto as a whole is falling with Bitcoin. If the AI stock sell-off turns into a wider risk-off move, tokens that held up for one week can still fall hard.

## FAQ

**Are AI tokens a hedge against AI stocks?** This week they fell less, but over the last month and the last year they fell more. One week is not evidence of a hedge.

**What is Bittensor (TAO)?** A crypto network where participants train and serve AI models and are paid in TAO. Read more on its [asset page]({lang}/tao/).
