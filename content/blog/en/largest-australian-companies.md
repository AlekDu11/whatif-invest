The largest Australian companies by market cap are **Commonwealth Bank (CommBank), BHP and Rio Tinto**, followed by Westpac, ANZ, National Australia Bank, CSL, Wesfarmers, Macquarie and Woodside. **$10 a day in each of these top 10 companies for five years turned $182,600 into about $243,200 (+33%)** in US dollars — behind the same plan in the S&P 500 ({{dcaroi:spy:5}}).

> **Key takeaways**
> - The top 10 Australian companies by market value on the ASX (November 2026): Commonwealth Bank, BHP, Rio Tinto, Westpac, ANZ, NAB, CSL, Wesfarmers, Macquarie, Woodside.
> - CommBank (~A$283 billion) and BHP (~A$271 billion) are close to each other and far ahead of the rest; the big four banks together make up about **43%** of the top 10's value.
> - $10 a day in each for 5 years: **+33%**; across all 24 large Australian stocks we track: **+29.6%**, and **6 of 24** beat the S&P 500. Over the last year the top 10 were flat (**−0.1%**).
> - The big four banks made **+44.5%** together, the four miners (BHP, Rio Tinto, Fortescue, Northern Star) **+35.6%**, and seven well-known consumer and tech names **+21.3%**.

## The top 10 largest Australian companies by market cap

The ranking of the biggest companies listed on the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX) by market value (share price × shares), from Yahoo Finance data in November 2026. Live prices are on each company's page.

1. **[Commonwealth Bank]({lang}/cba/)** — Australia's largest bank and the most valuable company on the ASX. {{price:cba}}
2. **[BHP]({lang}/bhp/)** — one of the world's biggest miners: iron ore, copper and coal; since 2022 its main listing is in Australia only. {{price:bhp}}
3. **[Rio Tinto]({lang}/rio-au/)** — the other giant iron-ore miner, also big in aluminium and copper; listed in both Sydney and London. {{price:rio-au}}
4. **[Westpac]({lang}/wbc/)** — Australia's oldest bank, one of the "big four".
5. **[ANZ]({lang}/anz/)** — big-four bank with a large business in New Zealand and Asia.
6. **[National Australia Bank]({lang}/nab/)** — the big-four bank most focused on business lending.
7. **[CSL]({lang}/csl/)** — blood-plasma medicines and flu vaccines (CSL Behring, CSL Seqirus).
8. **[Wesfarmers]({lang}/wes/)** — owner of Bunnings, Kmart and Officeworks.
9. **[Macquarie]({lang}/mqg/)** — investment bank and one of the world's biggest infrastructure investors.
10. **[Woodside]({lang}/wds/)** — Australia's largest oil and gas producer, a big LNG exporter.

Next come Telstra, Goodman Group, Transurban, Fortescue, QBE Insurance, Aristocrat Leisure, Northern Star and Coles. Woolworths is also one of the largest employers and retailers, but our data has no fresh market cap for it, so it is left out of the ranking.

## How to buy Australian stocks

- The **ASX** trades 10:00–16:00 Sydney time on weekdays; prices are in Australian dollars.
- The main index is the **S&P/ASX 200**; an ASX 200 ETF buys the 200 largest companies in one go.
- BHP and Rio Tinto also trade in New York as ADRs (BHP, RIO), so they can be bought in dollars. Check what your broker offers and its currency fees.
- See every Australian stock we track, sorted by market cap: [Australia stocks]({lang}/stocks-australia/).

## What it means if you buy regularly

Regular buying in the biggest Australian companies grew the money over five years but lagged a plain S&P 500 plan, and the result depended heavily on two sectors: banks and miners make up most of the list, so iron-ore prices and Australian interest rates move it. A broad ASX 200 or global fund spreads that risk. Past results, not a forecast.
