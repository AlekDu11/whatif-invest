Binance's HODLer Airdrops pay free tokens of new projects to anyone who keeps **BNB in Simple Earn** during a snapshot window — no trading, no tasks. Since the program started in June 2024 it has run more than 60 drops (Gensyn, in May 2026, was the 64th), and a typical one hands out about **1% of the new token's supply**. The catch: you never know in advance when the snapshot is taken, and the value of each drop is small next to the price swings of BNB itself.

> **Key takeaways**
> - Binance takes several balance snapshots every hour and averages them; the window is chosen at random and is announced only after it has passed.
> - Recent drops such as Fabric Protocol (ROBO, March 2026) and Gensyn (May 2026) each gave out 100 million tokens, 1% of a 10 billion supply, for a 2–3 day window.
> - A single account can count only up to a cap, for ROBO 4% of the whole pool; BNB used as loan collateral or left in the Spot wallet does not count.

## The program rewards doing nothing, on purpose

Binance announced HODLer Airdrops on June 19, 2024 as a reward "exclusively for BNB holders". The rules are simple: subscribe BNB to Simple Earn (Flexible or Locked) or to On-Chain Yields, and keep it there. When a project is chosen, Binance looks back at a past window — for ROBO it was subscriptions between March 4 and 6, 2026 — and splits the reward pool by each user's average BNB balance. Tokens land in the Spot wallet, usually within hours of the announcement and before trading starts.

Because the window is in the past, there is no point rushing in after news of a drop. The only way to be counted is to already hold BNB in Earn.

## How people take part

1. **Check that Binance serves your country.** Binance.com does not serve the US (Binance.US is a separate company) or the UK, and each airdrop lists its own eligible regions. Do not get around it with a VPN: it breaks the terms and the account can be frozen.
2. **Complete identity verification (KYC).** It is required for every drop.
3. **Buy or move BNB into Simple Earn Flexible** — you can redeem at any time — or Locked if you accept a fixed term. Since April 2025, Simple Earn BNB is enrolled in Launchpool and HODLer Airdrops automatically.
4. **Keep at least 0.01 BNB there,** the minimum guides list for a snapshot. More BNB means a bigger share, up to the per-user cap.
5. **Wait.** Rewards appear in the Spot wallet. Watch only binance.com announcements and Binance's verified X account; fake "HODLer claim" sites ask you to connect a wallet, which the real program never does. See our [9 airdrop safety rules]({lang}/blog/airdrop-safety-rules/).

## The real risks: BNB, the exchange and token prices

The airdrop is free, but holding BNB is not risk-free. BNB trades at {{price:bnb}} and can fall faster than any airdrop pays. Simple Earn is custodial — Binance holds the coins. On October 29, 2026, attackers drained Binance hot wallets of about $6.3–6.4 billion by Binance's and analysts' estimates; Binance paused withdrawals, said it would cover the losses from its SAFU fund and own money, and began reopening withdrawals in phases on November 1. And new tokens often drop after listing: sell-offs by airdrop recipients are common, so the value you see on day one may not last.

## What it means if you buy regularly

HODLer Airdrops fit a regular-buying habit better than most activities: if BNB is already part of your plan, parking it in Simple Earn Flexible adds small extras without extra work. That only makes sense if you want BNB anyway — buying a volatile exchange token just for airdrops swaps a small bonus for a big risk.
