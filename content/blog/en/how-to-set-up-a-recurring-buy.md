**How do you set up a recurring buy?** In your exchange app you open the buy screen, switch from a one-time buy to **Recurring** (also called **Auto-Buy** or **Auto-Invest**), choose the coin, the amount, how often (daily, weekly or monthly) and how you pay. After that the exchange buys for you on schedule until you stop it.

> **Key takeaways**
> - A **recurring buy** is the automatic version of DCA (dollar-cost averaging — buying a small fixed amount on a schedule instead of guessing the best day). You set it once; it runs by itself.
> - The **payment method decides the fee.** Paying by bank card is usually the most expensive; paying from money already on the exchange (a balance in your currency or a stablecoin like USDT) is usually cheaper. Check the fee on the confirmation screen.
> - You can **pause or cancel** a recurring buy at any time in the app. If there is not enough money for one buy, that buy simply fails and the plan tries again next time.

## Before you start

You need an exchange account that is verified (ID check done) and protected with 2FA — see [How to register on a crypto exchange]({lang}/blog/how-to-register-on-a-crypto-exchange/). Decide one thing first: **an amount you can keep paying for years without stress**, even if the price falls by half.

## Step 1 — Find the recurring buy screen

Every app names it a little differently, but it is always next to the normal **Buy** button:

- **Binance:** open **Trade → Convert** (or **Buy Crypto**) and pick the **Recurring** tab. Binance says its Convert Recurring plans run hourly, daily, weekly, every two weeks or monthly, with zero Convert fees (the rate can differ a little from the spot market price). There is also a separate **Auto-Invest** product under Earn.
- **Crypto.com:** tap **Buy**, choose the coin, then pick **Recurring Buy** from the menu in the top-right corner.
- **Revolut:** open **Crypto → Trade**, choose the coin, tap **Buy**, then switch the order type to **Recurring buy**.

If you cannot find it, type "recurring" into the app's search or help centre — only inside the official app.

## Step 2 — Choose the coin

Start with one large, well-known coin such as Bitcoin (see the [Bitcoin page]({lang}/btc/)). Many beginners automate only one or two coins. Never put a recurring buy on a coin you found in an ad or a Telegram tip.

## Step 3 — Enter the amount and the frequency

1. Type the amount per buy, for example 10 in your currency. Some apps accept very small amounts — Crypto.com's minimum is US$1.
2. Choose how often: **daily, weekly or monthly**. Weekly or monthly is easier to follow and often cheaper (fewer fees).
3. Pick the start date and, if asked, the time.

Tip: many people set the buy for the day after their salary arrives.

## Step 4 — Choose how to pay (this is where the fee hides)

The app usually offers:

- **Bank card** — easiest, but usually the highest fee.
- **Balance in your currency** (money you sent by bank transfer to the exchange) — often cheaper.
- **Stablecoin balance (USDT, USDC)** — common on Binance, where the plan takes money from your Spot account.

Before you confirm, look at the line that shows **fee** and **you will receive**. If the fee is more than about 1–2% of each buy, try another payment method or a larger, less frequent buy (monthly instead of daily).

## Step 5 — Review and confirm

Check the coin, the amount, the frequency, the payment method and the first date. Press **Confirm**. The plan now appears in a list such as **Recurring**, **Orders** or **Active plans**.

## Step 6 — Check, pause or cancel

1. After the first buy, open your wallet or portfolio and check the coin arrived.
2. To stop, open the plan and press **Pause** (you can restart it) or **Cancel/Delete**.
3. Keep enough money on the card or balance. If a buy fails because the balance is too low, the app skips that buy and tries again at the next date (Revolut and Binance both work this way; Binance cancels a plan that keeps failing for 6 months).

Track what you buy in [your portfolio]({lang}/me/) — add each purchase, or the total once a month.

> **⚠️ Watch out**
> - **Card fees eat small buys.** A fee of a few percent on every $10 buy adds up over years. Compare payment methods on the confirmation screen.
> - **"Auto-trading bots" are not recurring buys.** A recurring buy only buys on a schedule. Anyone selling a "bot that doubles your money" or asking you to give them your account is a scammer.
> - **Forgetting the plan.** Write it down and check once a month. Make sure the amount still fits your budget, and never borrow money to keep it running.
