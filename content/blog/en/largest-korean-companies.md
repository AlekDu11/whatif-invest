## The top 10 largest Korean companies by market cap

The ranking of the biggest South Korean companies listed on the Korea Exchange (KOSPI) by market value (share price × shares), from Yahoo Finance data in November 2026. Live prices are on each company's page.

1. **[Samsung Electronics]({lang}/samsung-electronics/)** — memory chips, smartphones, TVs and appliances; the core of the Samsung group. {{price:samsung-electronics}}
2. **[SK hynix]({lang}/sk-hynix/)** — memory chips, including the high-bandwidth memory (HBM) used in AI servers. {{price:sk-hynix}}
3. **[LG Energy Solution]({lang}/lg-energy-solution/)** — batteries for electric cars and energy storage. {{price:lg-energy-solution}}
4. **[Hyundai Motor]({lang}/hyundai-motor/)** — cars under the Hyundai and Genesis brands; controls Kia.
5. **[Samsung Biologics]({lang}/samsung-biologics/)** — contract manufacturing of biotech drugs.
6. **[KB Financial]({lang}/kb-financial/)** — Korea's largest banking group (KB Kookmin Bank).
7. **[Celltrion]({lang}/celltrion/)** — biosimilars (copies of biotech drugs).
8. **[Samsung Life]({lang}/samsung-life/)** — Korea's largest life insurer.
9. **[Samsung C&T]({lang}/samsung-c-t/)** — construction, trading and the de facto holding company of the Samsung group.
10. **[Shinhan Financial]({lang}/shinhan-financial/)** — banking group (Shinhan Bank, Shinhan Card).

Next come Kia, Samsung SDI, Hana Financial, Hanwha Aerospace, Hyundai Mobis, Doosan Enerbility, HD Hyundai Heavy Industries, Naver, LG Electronics and POSCO. Big family groups (chaebol) — Samsung, SK, Hyundai, LG — own stakes in many of these companies, so one group often appears several times.

## How to buy Korean stocks

- The **Korea Exchange** (KOSPI main board, KOSDAQ for smaller and tech companies) trades 9:00–15:30 Korea time on weekdays; prices are in won.
- The main index is the **KOSPI**; the KOSPI 200 holds the 200 biggest companies. A KOSPI 200 ETF buys them in one go.
- Outside Korea, few brokers offer KRX shares directly. Some companies have shares abroad (KB Financial, Shinhan and POSCO are listed in New York, Samsung has GDRs in London), and US-listed South Korea ETFs hold the big names. Check what your broker offers and its currency fees.

## What it means if you buy regularly

Regular buying in the biggest Korean companies worked over five years — but mostly thanks to two chipmakers. The spread was huge, from SK hynix's {{dcaroi:sk-hynix:5}} to Kakao's {{dcaroi:kakao:5}}, and anyone who started a year ago is down. Korea's market leans heavily on memory chips, which move in booms and busts. A plan on a broad KOSPI fund, or alongside a global fund, depends less on the next chip cycle.
