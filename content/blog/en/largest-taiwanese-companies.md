The largest companies in Taiwan by market cap are **TSMC, MediaTek and Hon Hai (Foxconn)**, followed by Delta Electronics, ASE Technology, Fubon Financial, Cathay Financial, Chunghwa Telecom, CTBC Financial and UMC. **$10 a day in each of these top 10 companies for five years turned $182,600 into about $441,100 (+142%)** in US dollars — more than three times the result of the same plan in the S&P 500 ({{dcaroi:spy:5}}).

> **Key takeaways**
> - The top 10 Taiwanese companies by market value (Taiwan Stock Exchange, November 2026, among the stocks we track): TSMC, MediaTek, Hon Hai, Delta Electronics, ASE Technology, Fubon Financial, Cathay Financial, Chunghwa Telecom, CTBC Financial, UMC.
> - TSMC alone (~NT$52.6 trillion, about $1.6 trillion) is worth more than 2.5 times the next nine combined (~NT$20.3 trillion).
> - $10 a day in each for 5 years: **+142%**; across all 15 Taiwanese stocks we track: **+107%**, and **11 of 15** beat the S&P 500. Over the last year the top 10 made **+8.3%**.
> - The four chipmakers (TSMC, MediaTek, ASE, UMC) together made **+201%**; the "old economy" trio Formosa Plastics, China Steel and Chunghwa Telecom made **+3.9%**.

## The top 10 largest companies in Taiwan by market cap

The ranking of the biggest companies listed on the Taiwan Stock Exchange by market value (share price × shares), from Yahoo Finance data in November 2026. Live prices are on each company's page.

1. **[TSMC]({lang}/tsmc/)** — the world's largest contract chipmaker; it makes the processors for Apple, Nvidia, AMD and many others. {{price:tsmc}}
2. **[MediaTek]({lang}/mediatek/)** — designs chips for smartphones, TVs, Wi-Fi and, increasingly, AI data centres. {{price:mediatek}}
3. **[Hon Hai Precision (Foxconn)]({lang}/hon-hai/)** — the world's largest electronics manufacturer: iPhones, servers for AI, and the owner of Japan's Sharp. {{price:hon-hai}}
4. **[Delta Electronics]({lang}/delta-electronics/)** — power supplies and cooling for servers, electric vehicles and factories.
5. **[ASE Technology]({lang}/ase-technology/)** — the largest company that packages and tests chips after they are made.
6. **[Fubon Financial]({lang}/fubon-financial/)** — insurance and banking (Fubon Life, Taipei Fubon Bank).
7. **[Cathay Financial]({lang}/cathay-financial/)** — Taiwan's largest life insurer (Cathay Life) and Cathay United Bank.
8. **[Chunghwa Telecom]({lang}/chunghwa-telecom/)** — the biggest phone and internet company in Taiwan.
9. **[CTBC Financial]({lang}/ctbc-financial/)** — one of the largest private banks in Taiwan.
10. **[UMC]({lang}/umc/)** — the second big Taiwanese contract chipmaker, focused on older, cheaper chip technologies.

Next come Quanta Computer, Mega Financial, Largan Precision and Formosa Plastics. Some fast-growing AI hardware makers listed in Taipei are not tracked on our site yet, so the full exchange ranking below the top few can differ slightly.

## Biggest Taiwanese companies by revenue, profit and staff

- **Market cap** — TSMC, MediaTek, Hon Hai (the ranking above).
- **Revenue** — Hon Hai far ahead (~NT$10.3 trillion, about $312 billion), then TSMC (~NT$4.9 trillion) and Quanta (~NT$3.6 trillion) (last 12 months, Yahoo Finance, November 2026).
- **Net profit** — TSMC in a league of its own (~NT$2.5 trillion, about $77 billion), then Fubon, Cathay and Hon Hai (~NT$230–270 billion each).
- **Employees** — ASE (~119,000) and TSMC (~77,000) among the Taiwan-listed firms with reported figures; Hon Hai employs far more worldwide, mostly in its factories in mainland China.

## How to buy Taiwanese stocks

- The **Taiwan Stock Exchange (TWSE)** trades 9:00–13:30 Taipei time on weekdays; prices are in New Taiwan dollars. Smaller companies trade on the Taipei Exchange (TPEx).
- The main index is **TAIEX**; TSMC makes up a large share of it, so a TAIEX or Taiwan fund is also a big bet on TSMC.
- Foreign individuals usually buy through an international broker that offers Taipei, or through New York listings: TSMC (TSM), UMC (UMC), ASE (ASX) and Chunghwa Telecom (CHT) all have US ADRs. Check what your broker offers and its currency fees.
- See every Taiwanese stock we track, sorted by market cap: [Taiwan stocks]({lang}/stocks-taiwan/).

## What it means if you buy regularly

Regular buying in Taiwan's biggest companies paid off very well over the last five years — but it was almost entirely a bet on the global chip and AI-server cycle, and on TSMC above all. Taiwan's market moves sharply with chip demand and with political tension around the island. Combining a Taiwan position with a broad world or S&P 500 fund reduces that dependence. Past results, not a forecast.
