Monday, November 23 was the rebound everyone was waiting for. Nvidia's board brought back Jensen Huang as CEO, the stock jumped about **31%**, and the Nasdaq rose **6.5%** — its best day since April 2025 — while the S&P 500 gained **4.7%** and broke a seven-day losing streak. Crypto joined in more quietly: Bitcoin ended near **$61,560**, up **3.1%** in 24 hours (live: **{{price:btc}}**).

> **Key takeaways**
> - S&P 500 +4.73% to 7,237, Nasdaq +6.51% to 24,090, Dow +3.01% (+1,448 points) — Nvidia +31.5% to about $165.
> - The VIX fear index fell almost 26% to 26.6; gold futures rose 3.4% to about $3,636 and US crude fell 3.8% to about $80.
> - Bitcoin +3.1% and Ethereum +2.6% to about $1,728; 37 of 44 coins worth $1 billion or more rose.
> - The Crypto Fear & Greed Index is still at 11/100 — "extreme fear" — the same as a day ago and 10 a week ago.

## The big picture: stocks lead, crypto follows slowly

After the worst week since April 2025, stocks bounced hard. The S&P 500 had been more than 10% below its record; on Monday it added **326.8 points** to **7,237.17**. The Nasdaq-100 fund QQQ rose about **7%**. Small companies joined in too: the Russell 2000 gained **3.4%**.

Gold had its best day in weeks, **+3.4%**, after a 4.6% fall on Friday, and silver rose about 3%. Oil went the other way: US crude futures lost **3.8%** to about $80.

Crypto rose, but less than tech stocks. Total crypto market value was about **$2.09 trillion** (CoinGecko) and Bitcoin's share was **58.9%**. The mood barely changed: Fear & Greed stayed at **11**. Among the 20 largest coins the biggest mover was [Canton]({lang}/cc/), **+4.3%**, just ahead of [Hyperliquid]({lang}/hype/) **+4.1%** and [Solana]({lang}/sol/) **+4.0%**.

## Biggest losers

- [CrowdStrike]({lang}/crwd/) **−8.9%** — investors did not like the price of its $43.9 billion all-stock deal for [Zscaler]({lang}/zs/), which rose **17.7%** ([brief]({lang}/news/crowdstrike-buys-zscaler-43-9-billion/)).
- [Anthropic]({lang}/anthropic/) **−1.4%** — one of the few AI names that fell, in only its third trading day.

- [Mantle]({lang}/mnt/) **−0.9%**, [World Liberty Financial]({lang}/wlfi/) **−0.7%** and [MemeCore]({lang}/m/) **−0.7%** — small dips while almost everything else rose.

## The news that moved markets

1. **Huang is back at Nvidia.** Eleven days after he stepped down, the board voted unanimously to reinstate him as CEO, saying input from senior US government officials left it confident his return poses no national security risk. The internal investigation continues. President Trump had urged the rehire overnight ([premarket brief]({lang}/news/nvidia-rebounds-premarket-trump-rehire-huang/)).
2. **CrowdStrike to buy Zscaler.** An all-stock deal worth about $43.9 billion, a 34% premium at Friday's prices — one of the largest cybersecurity deals ever.
3. **Bitcoin ETFs: fourth week of outflows.** US spot funds lost $1.79 billion last week, the third-largest weekly outflow on record; November's total is $4.14 billion so far ([brief]({lang}/news/bitcoin-etfs-1-79-billion-weekly-outflow-fourth-week/)).
4. **Asia steadied markets.** Korea's KOSPI hit a 10-month low before recovering, while Taiwan tightened short-selling rules and China eased margin rules ([brief]({lang}/news/us-futures-bounce-asia-ai-rout-kospi-10-month-low/)).
5. **Another bridge exploit.** Fusionist's ACE bridge lost more than $5 million in two waves — the third bridge incident in a week ([brief]({lang}/news/fusionist-ace-bridge-exploit-3-7-million/)). The UK also said exchanges may not trade against their own customers from 2027 ([brief]({lang}/news/uk-bans-crypto-exchanges-market-making-own-platforms-2027/)).
