Tuesday, November 24 undid part of Monday's party. Chip stocks fell hard — Nvidia about **−5.9%**, Analog Devices **−17%** — and the Nasdaq lost **1.48%**, yet the Dow rose **0.85%** and closed back above **50,000** as money moved into healthcare, retail and other "value" stocks. Crypto slipped again: Bitcoin was near **$60,060** in our data, down **2.2%** in 24 hours (live: **{{price:btc}}**).

> **Key takeaways**
> - Dow +0.85% to 50,018.70; S&P 500 −0.50% to 7,201.09; Nasdaq −1.48% to 23,733.54; the chip index fell about 7.9%.
> - US consumer confidence jumped to 103.3 from 91.9 — far above the 92.2 economists expected.
> - Not one of the 45 coins worth $1 billion or more rose; Zcash fell 12.5%, Bitcoin 2.2%, Ethereum 2.9%.
> - The Crypto Fear & Greed Index sits at 11/100, "extreme fear" — the same as a day ago and 12 a week ago.

## The big picture: a rotation, not a crash

Monday's rally was led by chips; on Tuesday those same stocks led the way down. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index lost about **7.9%** (Bloomberg, via Startup Fortune), while the Dow — full of banks, healthcare and consumer names — gained **423 points**. The S&P 500 slipped **0.5%**, its eighth fall in nine sessions. The 10-year US Treasury yield stayed near **4.90%**.

Gold fell about **2.5%**, to near **$3,555** an ounce, and silver lost about **5.6%**. US crude edged down about **1.6%**, to near $81.

Crypto drifted lower all day. Total crypto market value was about **$2.09 trillion** (CoinGecko) and Bitcoin's share fell to **57.9%** from 58.9% a day earlier — so smaller coins fell more than Bitcoin. Among the 20 largest coins the biggest mover was [Zcash]({lang}/zec/), **−12.5%**.

## Biggest winners

Large stocks (closing moves) and the coins that held up best:

- [Dick's Sporting Goods]({lang}/dks/) about **+17%** — quarterly profit of $1.94 a share beat the $1.42 estimate, and Foot Locker's sales grew again ([our brief]({lang}/news/dicks-jumps-15-percent-foot-locker-q3-november-2026/)).
- [Dollar Tree]({lang}/dltr/) **+11.2%** — quarterly results beat expectations (Kohl's, a smaller retailer, jumped 19.5% for the same reason).
- [Sanofi]({lang}/sny/) **+10.9%**, [Moderna]({lang}/mrna/) **+7.4%** and [Amgen]({lang}/amgn/) **+5.5%** — drug makers led the move into defensive stocks; we found no single company-specific trigger.
- [Take-Two]({lang}/ttwo/) **+5.2%** — no specific news found.
- In crypto, nothing rose among $1B+ coins: [Monero]({lang}/xmr/) was flat and [TRON]({lang}/trx/) lost only **1.2%**.

## Biggest losers

- [Analog Devices]({lang}/adi/) **−17.3%** to $313.16 — it reported record quarterly results that morning, but the stock still fell with the chip sector and dragged [Texas Instruments]({lang}/txn/) about 9% lower.
- [Hewlett Packard Enterprise]({lang}/hpe/) **−17.1%** and [Dell]({lang}/dell/) **−12.0%** — AI server makers were hit hardest ([HPE brief]({lang}/news/hpe-jumps-17-percent-then-falls-after-earnings-november-2026/)).
- [Nvidia]({lang}/nvda/) about **−5.9%** — profit-taking a day after its record 31% jump; [AMD]({lang}/amd/) and [Micron]({lang}/mu/) lost about 9–10% ([our brief]({lang}/news/nvidia-gives-back-8-percent-after-record-jump-november-2026/)).
- [Zcash]({lang}/zec/) **−12.5%**, [Rain]({lang}/rain/) **−9.9%**, [Quant]({lang}/qnt/) **−8.8%** and [Hyperliquid]({lang}/hype/) **−6.9%** — the weakest large coins; we found no coin-specific news, they fell with the market.

## The news that moved markets

1. **Chip stocks gave back gains.** After Monday's 31% jump on Jensen Huang's return, Nvidia lost roughly $240 billion of market value as investors took profits and doubts about AI spending returned. Anthropic shares fell below their $83 IPO price, CNBC reported.
2. **Americans feel better.** The Conference Board's consumer confidence index rose 11.4 points to 103.3, well above forecasts — one reason shop and consumer stocks rose.
3. **New tariffs are being prepared.** The US is getting ready to raise tariffs on China, the EU, India and 14 other economies over industrial subsidies, the WSJ reported; nothing is official yet ([brief]({lang}/news/us-prepares-new-tariffs-china-eu-14-countries/)).
4. **Bitcoin ETFs keep bleeding.** US spot funds lost another $569.7 million on Monday, per SoSoValue, and Bitcoin slipped back under $60,000 in Asian trading ([brief]({lang}/news/bitcoin-slips-below-60000-etfs-570-million-outflow/)). Strategy still bought 457 BTC last week ([brief]({lang}/news/strategy-buys-457-bitcoin-851305-btc/)).
5. **Washington on crypto and chips.** Prosecutors moved to seize $1.17 billion in USDC from the Binance hack ([brief]({lang}/news/doj-seeks-1-17-billion-usdc-binance-hack/)), and the US blacklisted an Alibaba unit over suspected Nvidia chip diversion ([brief]({lang}/news/us-blacklists-alibaba-unit-nvidia-chip-diversion/)).
