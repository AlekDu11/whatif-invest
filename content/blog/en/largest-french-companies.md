The largest French companies by market cap are **L'Oréal, LVMH and TotalEnergies**, followed by Schneider Electric, Hermès, Safran, Air Liquide, Sanofi, BNP Paribas and AXA. **$10 a day in each of these top 10 companies for five years turned ~$182,600 into ~$243,100 (+33%)** in US dollars — less than the same plan in the S&P 500 ({{dcaroi:spy:5}}), mostly because the luxury giants fell.

> **Key takeaways**
> - The top 10 French companies by market value (Euronext Paris, November 2026): L'Oréal, LVMH, TotalEnergies, Schneider Electric, Hermès, Safran, Air Liquide, Sanofi, BNP Paribas, AXA.
> - L'Oréal (~€232 billion) has overtaken LVMH (~€187 billion) as France's most valuable company after LVMH's shares slid for three years.
> - $10 a day in each for 5 years: **+33%**; across all 35 French stocks we track: **+27.7%**, and **12 of 35** beat the S&P 500. Over the last year the top 10 made **−2.0%**.
> - The famous brands lagged: five luxury stocks (LVMH, Hermès, Kering, Pernod Ricard, EssilorLuxottica) made **−25.6%** together, while the banks and insurers (BNP Paribas, Société Générale, Crédit Agricole, AXA) made **+80.9%**.

## How to buy French stocks

- **Euronext Paris** trades 9:00–17:30 Paris time on weekdays, with opening and closing auctions; prices are in euros.
- The main index is the **CAC 40** (40 large companies); a CAC 40 ETF buys them in one go, and European or global funds hold the big names too.
- Most brokers that offer European markets include Paris. TotalEnergies is also listed in New York (TTE) and Sanofi has US ADRs (SNY). Check what your broker offers and its currency fees.
- See every French stock we track, sorted by market cap: [France stocks]({lang}/stocks-france/).

## What it means if you buy regularly

Regular buying in the biggest French companies made money over five years, but less than a plain S&P 500 plan — and the result depended on which sectors you held. Buying the famous luxury names every day lost about a quarter of the money, while banks and defence nearly doubled it. A broad CAC 40 or European fund spreads that risk instead of betting on one sector. Past results, not a forecast.
