The largest Swiss companies by market cap are **Roche, Novartis and Nestlé**, followed by ABB, UBS, Richemont, Zurich Insurance, Lonza, Swiss Re and Holcim. **$10 a day in each of the top 10 Swiss companies we track for five years turned ~$182,600 into ~$275,700 (+51%)** in US dollars — more than the same plan in the S&P 500 ({{dcaroi:spy:5}}), helped by the strong Swiss franc.

> **Key takeaways**
> - The biggest Swiss companies by market value (SIX Swiss Exchange, November 2026): Roche, Novartis, Nestlé, ABB, UBS, Richemont, Zurich Insurance, Lonza, Swiss Re, Holcim, Givaudan.
> - $10 a day for 5 years in the top 10 we track: **+51%**; across all 19 Swiss stocks we track: **+35.5%**, and **7 of 19** beat the S&P 500. Over the last year the top 10 made **+4.7%**.
> - The quiet winners were insurers and banks: Zurich, Swiss Re and Swiss Life together made **+72.5%**, while Nestlé, the most famous Swiss brand, made only {{dcaroi:nesn:5}}.

## The top 10 largest Swiss companies by market cap

The ranking of the biggest companies listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange by market value (share price × shares), from Yahoo Finance data in November 2026. Live prices are on each company's page.

1. **Roche** — pharmaceuticals and diagnostics (Basel). One of the three Swiss giants; we do not track its shares yet, so it is not in the tables below.
2. **[Novartis]({lang}/novn/)** — prescription medicines (heart, cancer, immunology). {{price:novn}}
3. **[Nestlé]({lang}/nesn/)** — the world's largest food company (Nescafé, KitKat, Purina, Maggi). {{price:nesn}}
4. **[ABB]({lang}/abbn/)** — electrification and industrial automation, including equipment for data centres. {{price:abbn}}
5. **[UBS]({lang}/ubsg/)** — the largest Swiss bank and the world's biggest wealth manager; it took over Credit Suisse in 2023.
6. **[Richemont]({lang}/cfr/)** — luxury jewellery and watches (Cartier, Van Cleef & Arpels, IWC).
7. **[Zurich Insurance]({lang}/zurn/)** — property, casualty and life insurance worldwide.
8. **[Lonza]({lang}/lonn/)** — makes medicines for other drug companies.
9. **[Swiss Re]({lang}/sren/)** — reinsurance: it insures the insurers.
10. **[Holcim]({lang}/holn/)** — cement and building materials.

Next come Givaudan (flavours and fragrances), Swisscom, Alcon, Sika, Kuehne+Nagel, Swiss Life, Geberit, Straumann, Partners Group and Logitech. Some famous Swiss names are private — Rolex, for example — so they are not in any stock ranking. Insurer [Chubb]({lang}/cb/) is based in Zurich but trades in New York.

## How to buy Swiss stocks

- **SIX Swiss Exchange** in Zurich trades 9:00–17:30 Swiss time on weekdays, with a closing auction at the end; prices are in Swiss francs.
- The main index is the **SMI** — the 20 largest Swiss companies. An SMI or Switzerland ETF buys them in one go, and European or global funds hold the big names too.
- Most brokers with European markets include SIX. Novartis, UBS, ABB and Logitech are also traded in the US. Check what your broker offers and its currency fees.
- See every Swiss stock we track, sorted by market cap: [Switzerland stocks]({lang}/stocks-switzerland/).

## What it means if you buy regularly

Regular buying in the biggest Swiss companies beat a plain S&P 500 plan over the last five years — unusual for a European market. But the gain came from banks, insurers and industrials, not from the household names: daily buys in Nestlé were roughly flat. A broad SMI fund spreads that risk, and the franc can help or hurt a foreign investor. Past results, not a forecast.
