Gate's Launchpool lets you lock coins on the exchange and receive a new token every hour in return — and in its 384th round, from October 26 to November 16, 2026, the reward was **tokenized gold: 36 XAUT, with the USDT pool paying about 7.69% a year** on November 16. New rounds open every week with different reward tokens. The headline APRs move every hour, though, and the biggest ones are paid in tokens that can lose value fast.

*Updated November 25, 2026: round 384 with the XAUT gold reward ended on November 16 as scheduled. Launchpool itself keeps running with new rounds, so check today's reward tokens and APRs before staking — the gold pool is not open now.*

> **Key takeaways**
> - Round 384 split 36 XAUT (Tether Gold, one token per troy ounce) into three pools: 20 for USDT stakers, 8 for GT and 8 for XAUT.
> - On November 16, 2026 the APRs were 7.69% for USDT, 5.13% for XAUT and 2.10% for GT; parallel rounds paid 133.79% and 112.67% — in their own new tokens.
> - The USDT pool limit grows 1:1 with your net deposits, rewards arrive hourly and fully unlocked, and Gate does not serve the US, UK, Germany, France, Spain and about 25 other places.

## A Launchpool is a share of a fixed reward, not a fixed rate

Every round has a fixed number of reward tokens, split by the share of the pool each user stakes. Gate takes several snapshots of your stake every hour and pays the reward to your spot account. The "APR" on the page is just today's pool size divided by today's reward: when more people join, it falls; when they leave, it rises. Treat it as a weather report, not a promise.

The reward token matters more than the number. In the same week as the gold round, Gate ran pools for two newer tokens, KOR and VLX. Their own-token pools showed 133.79% and 112.67% APR, while staking USDT for KOR paid 0.86% and staking BTC for VLX 2.67%. A triple-digit yield paid in a token that falls 70% after listing can be worth less than a single-digit yield in gold.

## How people take part

1. **Check that Gate serves your country.** Its restricted list includes the US, Canada, the UK, Germany, France, Spain, the Netherlands, Austria, Japan, South Korea, India, Indonesia, Thailand, the UAE, Argentina and others, and Gate may add more without notice. If you are on it, skip this — never use a VPN.
2. **Verify your identity (KYC)** — Gate requires it to stake.
3. **Open Earn → Launchpool,** pick a pool and stake. For the USDT pool, how much you can stake depends on your net deposits.
4. **Keep the amount stable.** Rewards follow hourly snapshots; adding or removing often changes your share.
5. **Check "Airdrop Records"** for the hourly payouts. When the round ends, the staked coins go back to Simple Earn or spot, depending on your settings.

Only use gate.com itself or the app; "Gate Launchpool bonus" links in DMs are fake. Our [9 airdrop safety rules]({lang}/blog/airdrop-safety-rules/) cover the rest.

## What can go wrong

The coins sit on the exchange the whole time, so you carry exchange risk: if Gate pauses withdrawals or fails, the yield is irrelevant. Reward tokens can crash. Tokenized gold like XAUT tracks the gold price, which falls too, and redeeming it for physical metal has its own rules and fees. And staking a volatile coin for a few percent does not protect you from its price swings.

## What it means if you buy regularly

If you already hold USDT between purchases, a pool that pays in gold is one of the calmer ways to put idle cash to work for a few weeks — as long as the amount is one you could afford to have locked on an exchange.
