Hyperliquid ran the airdrop every points farmer now measures against: on November 29, 2024, **310 million HYPE — 31% of the total supply — went to users who had earned points**. Two years later, people keep asking about "season 2". The honest answer: Hyperliquid's own documentation still lists no points phase after November 2024 and no second airdrop. What does exist are smaller points programs run by apps built on Hyperliquid.

> **Key takeaways**
> - Season 1 paid 1,000,000 points a week from November 2023 to May 2024; the L1 phase paid 700,000 a week from May to November 2024.
> - Referrers earned 1 point for every 4 points their invited users earned.
> - As of autumn 2026 the official docs announce no new phase; any "Hyperliquid season 2 claim" site is fake.

## How the first season worked

The program started on November 1, 2023 and gave out a fixed 1,000,000 points every week for six months. A second, "L1" phase ran from May 29, 2024 to November 2024 at 700,000 points a week. Points went to people who traded, provided liquidity and brought in other users; then they were converted into HYPE at launch, fully unlocked. Because the weekly pool was fixed, every new farmer diluted everyone else — the earlier you came, the more each point was worth.

## What earns rewards on Hyperliquid now

There is no announced official season, so nothing you do today is guaranteed to count. What people do is:

1. **Trade spot and perpetual futures on the main exchange**, which is how points were earned the first time.
2. **Deposit into HLP**, the protocol's market-making vault. It earns a share of trading profits but can also lose when traders win; it is not risk-free.
3. **Use ecosystem apps with their own points.** Projects built on Hyperliquid run separate programs — for example prediction-market apps on its HIP-4 markets. Most have not confirmed a token either.
4. **Stake HYPE** for a share of network rewards — a yield, not an airdrop.

## The risks are bigger than the reward

Hyperliquid's main product is leveraged perpetual futures. With 10x leverage, a 10% move against you wipes out the position. "Volume farming" — opening and closing trades just to generate points — pays fees on every round trip, and security writers covering the programs say short-term wash trading and multi-wallet farming are likely to be filtered out. HYPE itself is volatile: today it trades at {{price:hype}}, and core-contributor token unlocks began on November 29, 2025, adding new supply.

## What it means if you buy regularly

Leverage and regular investing pull in opposite directions: DCA works because you never have to be right about timing, while a leveraged trade fails if you are wrong for a few hours. If you want exposure to Hyperliquid's growth, the plain way is to hold a small amount of HYPE inside your regular plan, without leverage, and treat any future reward as a bonus.
