The largest Canadian companies by market cap are **Royal Bank of Canada, TD Bank and Shopify**, followed by Bank of Montreal, Scotiabank, Enbridge, CIBC, Canadian Natural Resources, Brookfield and Agnico Eagle. **$10 a day in each of these top 10 companies for five years turned ~$182,600 into ~$328,200 (+80%)** in US dollars — well ahead of the same plan in the S&P 500 ({{dcaroi:spy:5}}).

> **Key takeaways**
> - The top 10 Canadian companies by market value (Toronto Stock Exchange, November 2026): Royal Bank of Canada, TD, Shopify, Bank of Montreal, Scotiabank, Enbridge, CIBC, Canadian Natural Resources, Brookfield Corporation, Agnico Eagle Mines.
> - Five of the ten are banks. Royal Bank of Canada alone is worth about C$373 billion — more than Enbridge and Canadian Natural Resources combined.
> - $10 a day in each for 5 years: **+80%**; across all 29 Canadian stocks we track: **+57%**, and **17 of 29** beat the S&P 500. Over the last year the top 10 made only **+2.4%**.
> - Gold miners (Agnico Eagle, Barrick, Wheaton Precious Metals) made **+126%** together, the big five banks **+81%** — while telecoms BCE and Telus lost **−21%**.

8. **[Canadian Natural Resources]({lang}/cnq/)** — Canada's largest oil and gas producer, including the oil sands.
9. **[Brookfield Corporation]({lang}/bn-ca/)** — one of the world's largest owners of real estate, infrastructure and renewable power.

Some well-known Canadian businesses are not on the list: Lululemon is listed only in the US, Bombardier, Dollarama and Franco-Nevada are not yet tracked on our site, and many Canadian giants are pension funds (CPP Investments, Caisse de dépôt) that are not listed at all.

## How to buy Canadian stocks

- The **Toronto Stock Exchange (TSX)** trades 9:30–16:00 Eastern time on weekdays; prices are in Canadian dollars.
- The main index is the **S&P/TSX Composite**; the **S&P/TSX 60** covers the 60 largest companies. A TSX 60 or Canada ETF buys them all in one go.
- Most of the top 10 — the five banks, Shopify, Enbridge, Canadian Natural, Brookfield and Agnico Eagle — are also listed in New York, so any broker with US stocks can buy them in US dollars. Check what your broker offers and its currency fees.
- See every Canadian stock we track, sorted by market cap: [Canada stocks]({lang}/stocks-canada/).

## What it means if you buy regularly

Regular buying in the biggest Canadian companies grew the money by about 80% over five years — but a big part came from gold miners in a record gold market and from banks in a period of higher interest rates, which may not repeat. Buying the telecoms every day lost money even though they were known as safe dividend stocks. A broad TSX 60 fund spreads that sector risk; Canada is still heavy in banks, energy and mining, so many investors pair it with global funds. Past results, not a forecast.
