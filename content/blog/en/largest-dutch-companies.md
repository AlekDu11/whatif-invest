The largest Dutch companies by market cap are **ASML, ING and Prosus**, followed by Heineken, ABN AMRO, ASM International, Ahold Delhaize, Universal Music Group, Adyen and Philips. **$10 a day in each of these top 10 companies for five years turned $182,600 into about $264,700 (+45%)** in US dollars — roughly level with the same plan in the S&P 500 ({{dcaroi:spy:5}}).

> **Key takeaways**
> - The top 10 Dutch companies by market value (Euronext Amsterdam, November 2026): ASML, ING, Prosus, Heineken, ABN AMRO, ASM International, Ahold Delhaize, Universal Music Group, Adyen, Philips.
> - ASML alone (~€474 billion in November 2026) was worth more than the next nine combined (~€364 billion).
> - $10 a day in each for 5 years: **+45%**; across all 36 Dutch stocks with five years of data: **+37.4%**, and **11 of 36** beat the S&P 500. Over the last year the top 10 made **−5.1%**.
> - The banks and insurers did best: ING, ABN AMRO, NN Group, Aegon and ASR together made **+116%**; seven famous household brands (Heineken, Philips, KPN, Ahold Delhaize, Akzo Nobel, TomTom, PostNL) made **+6.4%**.

## The top 10 largest Dutch companies by market cap

The ranking of the biggest companies listed in Amsterdam by market value (share price × shares), from Yahoo Finance data in November 2026. Live prices are on each company's page.

1. **[ASML]({lang}/asml/)** — the only maker of the EUV machines used to print the most advanced computer chips. {{price:asml}}
2. **[ING Group]({lang}/inga/)** — the largest Dutch bank, active across Europe. {{price:inga}}
3. **[Prosus]({lang}/prx/)** — a tech investor whose biggest holding is a stake in China's Tencent. {{price:prx}}
4. **[Heineken]({lang}/heia/)** — the world's second-largest brewer (Heineken, Amstel, Desperados).
5. **[ABN AMRO]({lang}/abn/)** — a major Dutch bank, still partly owned by the Dutch state.
6. **[ASM International]({lang}/asm/)** — equipment that deposits ultra-thin layers on chips.
7. **[Ahold Delhaize]({lang}/ad/)** — supermarkets: Albert Heijn in the Netherlands, Food Lion and Stop & Shop in the US.
8. **[Universal Music Group]({lang}/umg/)** — the world's largest record company (Taylor Swift, Drake, The Beatles catalogue).
9. **[Adyen]({lang}/adyen/)** — payment processing for companies like Uber, Spotify and Microsoft.
10. **[Philips]({lang}/phia/)** — medical equipment and personal health products.

Next come Exor, NN Group, Wolters Kluwer, KPN and ASR Nederland. Shell and Unilever started as Dutch companies but now have their head office and main listing in London, so they count as British in most rankings; Stellantis, Ferrari and Airbus are Dutch-registered but listed and run elsewhere.

## How to buy Dutch stocks

- **Euronext Amsterdam** trades 9:00–17:30 Amsterdam time on weekdays, with opening and closing auctions; prices are in euros.
- The main index is the **AEX** (the largest and most traded Amsterdam stocks); an AEX ETF buys them in one go.
- ASML is also listed on Nasdaq in New York (ASML), so it can be bought in dollars. Check what your broker offers and its currency fees.
- See every Dutch stock we track, sorted by market cap: [Netherlands stocks]({lang}/stocks-netherlands/).

## What it means if you buy regularly

Regular buying in the biggest Dutch companies matched a plain S&P 500 plan over five years — but the result hung on one company and one sector. ASML is so large that it moves the whole list, and the banks did the heavy lifting while the famous brands barely beat the money put in. A broad AEX or European fund spreads that risk instead of betting on chips or banks. Past results, not a forecast.
