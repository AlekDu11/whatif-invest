## Why DCA works psychologically

- You never have to guess the top or the bottom.
- In a crash you automatically buy more coins for the same money.
- $10 a day is small enough to forget about, so the habit survives for years.

> An investor's worst enemy is not volatility but emotional decisions. Recurring buys take emotion out of the process.
