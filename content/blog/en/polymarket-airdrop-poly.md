Polymarket, the largest prediction market, has said there will be a POLY token and an airdrop — but **more than a year later it has published no launch date, no snapshot date and no eligibility rules**. Anyone using Polymarket "for the airdrop" is betting on a promise whose size and terms are unknown. Here is what is actually confirmed, what is only rumoured and how people take part without fooling themselves.

*Updated November 18, 2026: still no token, date or rules. On October 29, 2026 Polymarket briefly switched its international exchange to cancel-only after malicious code loaded through a third-party script drained some users' accounts (about $2.6–2.7 million by on-chain estimates); Polymarket says affected users will be made whole.*

> **Key takeaways**
> - Polymarket's CMO Matthew Modabber confirmed a token and an airdrop on October 24, 2025, and said the US relaunch comes first.
> - Its parent company Blockratize filed "POLY" and "$POLY" trademarks with the US patent office on February 4, 2026.
> - As of autumn 2026, Polymarket still has no native token; the 5–10% "airdrop share" quoted online is not an official figure.

## What is confirmed

Three things are on the record. First, the promise: on a podcast in October 2025, Polymarket's marketing chief said there would be a token and an airdrop, and that the team would not rush it before its US app was running. Second, the paperwork: the POLY trademark applications in February 2026, a usual step before a branded token. Third, the product keeps growing — in 2026 Polymarket added pUSD, a dollar token backed 1:1 by USDC, used as collateral for bets. That is not the airdrop token.

## What is not confirmed

Everything that matters for a participant. There is no official date, no announced snapshot, no rules on who qualifies and no official share of supply for users. Speculative dates, including mid-2026 targets, have already passed. Sites that show an "estimated allocation" calculator are guessing.

## Why people compare it with Hyperliquid

Hyperliquid's points program ended with the HYPE launch on November 29, 2024, when 310 million tokens — 31% of the supply — went to users who had earned points. That is the record airdrop farmers remember, and Polymarket's own team has pointed to the comparison. But Hyperliquid published its points rules in advance; Polymarket has not. Read our [Hyperliquid points guide]({lang}/blog/hyperliquid-points-season-2/) for how that one worked.

## How people take part (and what it really costs)

Retroactive airdrops usually reward real, repeated use rather than one big burst. People who position for one typically:

1. **Check that Polymarket is legal where they live.** As of late October 2026, Polymarket itself blocks or limits to "close-only" about 40 countries, including the US, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Poland, Belgium, Australia, Brazil, Singapore and Thailand, and governments in Ukraine, Spain, Portugal, Turkey, India, Indonesia, the Philippines, Argentina, South Korea and others block the site themselves. Using a VPN to get around this breaks the terms and the law and can get an account frozen — do not. Our partner button checks your country and will not send you there if it is on these lists.
2. **Deposit a small amount** they are ready to lose, for example $10–$50.
3. **Make real predictions in several categories** (crypto, politics, sports, economy) over weeks and months, rather than one large trade.
4. **Hold positions to resolution** sometimes, and occasionally provide liquidity, as the platform's own users describe.
5. **Watch only official channels** for the announcement: polymarket.com and its verified X account. Fake "POLY claim" sites will appear the day the news lands — see our [9 airdrop safety rules]({lang}/blog/airdrop-safety-rules/).

The real cost is the bets themselves. A prediction market is not a savings account: if you are wrong, the stake is gone, airdrop or not.

## What it means if you buy regularly

An airdrop is a lottery ticket with unknown odds; a monthly investment plan is a habit with a long, measurable record. If you try Polymarket, size it as entertainment money — not money taken from your regular buys. For scale: $10 a day in Bitcoin over the last year turned {{dcainv:1}} into {{dca:btc:1}} ({{dcaroi:btc:1}}). Whatever POLY turns out to be worth, it should not come at the cost of the plan that actually compounds.
