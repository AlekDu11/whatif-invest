Venice Token (VVV), the token behind Erik Voorhees' private AI chat and API platform Venice, was the biggest gainer among the 200 largest cryptocurrencies on Sunday morning. It rose about 24% in 24 hours to near $25, while bitcoin bounced about 6%.

What happened

  • Sunday, November 22, 06:00 UTC: VVV traded near $24.90, up about 24% in a day, according to CoinCodex. It was the top gainer in the top 200; GEODNET was second with about +22%.
  • The rise started on Saturday. CoinMarketCap data showed VVV near $23.35 (+12.6%) on Saturday afternoon, after a dip to about $20.50 on Friday.
  • New places to trade it. Poloniex and HTX opened VVV deposits on November 21, and the Australian exchange CoinJar added buying and selling on November 20.
  • The whole market bounced. Bitcoin recovered from about $55,250 on Saturday, and roughly 91% of coins were up over 24 hours. About $227 million of short bets were liquidated within four hours, per the Panic Drop daily recap.

Why it moved

We found no single announcement behind Sunday's jump. Several things point the same way. New listings bring new buyers. Venice cut new token issuance from 14 million VVV a year at launch to 2 million from October, and part of its API revenue goes to buying back and burning tokens (about $628,000 of buybacks in mid-November, per CoinStats). A wallet linked to BitMEX co-founder Arthur Hayes, a vocal VVV backer, received about $306,000 of VVV from market maker Flowdesk on November 20; it is not confirmed that this was a purchase. Note the risk too: the ten largest addresses hold most of the circulating supply, so prices can swing fast in both directions.

The numbers right now

VVV trades at $25.96 +6.13%, +2,459% over 12 months. Its record high is $34.53 (September 2026), so it is still below the top. See how far every coin is from its peak on our from-ATH page and other AI projects on the AI page.

What it means if you buy regularly

A 24% day is exactly the kind of move that tempts people to jump in late. Someone who put $10 a day into VVV over the last year has invested $3,650 and now holds $16,950 (+364%). The same plan in bitcoin is worth $3,065. That is history, not a forecast: a small token with a concentrated supply can fall as fast as it rises. Compare amounts and assets in the DCA calculator.
