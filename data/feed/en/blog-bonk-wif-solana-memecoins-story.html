How did BONK and WIF take off? BONK was handed out for free to the Solana community on 25 December 2022, right after the FTX crash, and jumped many times over within days. WIF (dogwifhat), a photo of a dog in a pink hat, launched in November 2023 and passed a $2 billion market value in March 2024, after Binance listed it. Together they made Solana the home of memecoins.

Key takeaways

  • BONK gave away 50% of its 100 trillion coins in an airdrop (free tokens sent to many wallets). By 5 January 2023 its market value had reached about $205 million — and two days later it was about $82 million, CoinGecko counted.
  • WIF had no product at all. After Binance listed it on 5 March 2024, its market value passed $2 billion and its 24-hour trading came close to $1 billion, CoinDesk reported.
  • Today BONK trades at $0.00000221 +0.91% and WIF at $0.1387 +5.08%. WIF's record was $4.83 (March 2024); it is −97.1% from it.

This story goes deeper than the 2023–2024 memecoin wave. Words like "airdrop", "burn" or "listing" are explained in the memecoin glossary.

Chapter 1 — November 2022: Solana after FTX

In November 2022 the big exchange FTX collapsed. FTX and its founder had been loud supporters of the Solana network, so SOL's price crashed and many people expected Solana to fade. By the start of January 2023 SOL was trading at about $10.

Chapter 2 — 25 December 2022: a free dog for everyone

An anonymous team created BONK, a dog coin with 100 trillion tokens, and gave half of them away. According to CoinGecko, the split was:

  • 20% to holders of Solana NFTs (digital collectibles),
  • 15% to traders on the Solana exchange OpenBook,
  • 10% to artists and collectors,
  • 5% to Solana developers.

People did not pay anything to get BONK — it simply appeared in their wallets. That is why the community called it "the people's coin".

Chapter 3 — January 2023: up 3,649% — and down again

Then the price exploded. CoinGecko measured that BONK reached a 300% gain in just 8 days; Shiba Inu had needed 141 days for that, Dogecoin 1,253. On 5 January 2023 BONK hit its first record, up about 3,649%, with a market value of about $205 million.

The fall came just as fast: on 7 January the market value was about $82 million — more than half gone in two days. The team burned (destroyed forever) 5 trillion BONK, 5% of the supply, to calm holders. Interesting detail: SOL itself rose about 63% between 2 and 10 January 2023, as people bought SOL to trade BONK.

Chapter 4 — December 2023: the big exchanges arrive

For most of 2023 BONK was a small Solana coin. In December 2023 that changed: Coinbase added it (with an "experimental" label) and Binance listed it with a Seed Tag (Binance's warning label for new, riskier tokens) and opened trading on 16 December 2023. Millions of exchange users could now buy it with two taps.

Chapter 5 — November 2023 to March 2024: a dog in a hat

dogwifhat (WIF) launched on Solana in November 2023. It is a photo of a Shiba Inu dog wearing a pink knitted hat — nothing more. Still, Solana memecoins were the hottest trade of early 2024.

On 5 March 2024 Binance listed WIF. The next day it rose about 48% in 24 hours to above $2.11, and its market value passed $2 billion, CoinDesk reported. Binance alone saw $219 million of WIF trades on the first day. The Block counted a gain of about 1,245% since the start of 2024. At the end of March WIF set its record — $4.83 (March 2024).

Then fans collected about $700,000 to show the dog on the giant Las Vegas Sphere screen. The ad did not appear, DL News reported later in 2024 — a "community promise" with no contract behind it.

Chapter 6 — after the peak

Both coins fell a long way from their records, rose again in later rallies and fell again. Right now BONK's 12-month change is −76.4% and WIF's is −62.1%. Their success also inspired tools that let anyone create a coin in a minute — see the story of pump.fun — so thousands of copies appeared every day, and most went to almost zero.

What beginners can learn from it

  1. A free coin is not free money. BONK holders who sold some early locked in gains; those who waited saw half the value disappear in two days.
  2. Listings move prices — for a short time. Big jumps came right after Binance and Coinbase listings. People who bought on the listing headline often bought near a local top.
  3. No product means price = attention. WIF has no app or business. When attention moves on, so does the price.
  4. Survivors hide the graveyard. For every BONK or WIF there were thousands of dog coins that went to zero.

⚠️ Watch out

  • Fake "BONK airdrop" or "WIF claim" sites. They ask you to connect your wallet and then drain it. Real airdrops do not need you to sign strange transactions.
  • Copycat tokens. "BONK 2.0", "Baby WIF"… Check the real contract address on CoinGecko or a big exchange — see How to check a memecoin.
  • Buying after a 1,000% move. Past jumps say nothing about tomorrow; memecoins can lose half their value in days.

What it means if you buy regularly

Buying a memecoin in small parts only spreads the moment you buy — the risk stays. $10 a day in BONK for 2 years ($7,300 in total) became $2,441 (−66.6%); the same in WIF became $3,415 (−53.2%), and in Solana itself $4,625 (−36.6%).
