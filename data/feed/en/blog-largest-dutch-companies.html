How to buy Dutch stocks

  • Euronext Amsterdam trades 9:00–17:30 Amsterdam time on weekdays, with opening and closing auctions; prices are in euros.
  • The main index is the AEX (the largest and most traded Amsterdam stocks); an AEX ETF buys them in one go.
  • ASML is also listed on Nasdaq in New York (ASML), so it can be bought in dollars. Check what your broker offers and its currency fees.

What it means if you buy regularly

Regular buying in the biggest Dutch companies matched a plain S&P 500 plan over five years — but the result hung on one company and one sector. ASML is so large that it moves the whole list, and the banks did the heavy lifting while the famous brands barely beat the money put in. A broad AEX or European fund spreads that risk instead of betting on chips or banks. Past results, not a forecast.
