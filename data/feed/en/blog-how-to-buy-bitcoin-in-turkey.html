The legal way to buy Bitcoin in Turkey is to open an account on a platform from the Capital Markets Board (SPK) list of crypto asset service providers and send Turkish lira from a bank account in your own name. With a havale or FAST transfer you can start within minutes and buy a small slice of Bitcoin for a few hundred lira. Key takeaways

  • Law No. 7518 took effect on July 2, 2024. It put crypto platforms under SPK supervision, and foreign platforms targeting Turkish residents had to stop by October 2, 2024.
  • MASAK rules from June 2025 cap withdrawals at $3,000 a day for stablecoins and $6,000 a day for other crypto, with a wait of at least 72 hours on a customer's first withdrawal.
  • There is no crypto-specific tax in 2026: a 0.03% transaction tax and a 10% withholding tax were dropped from a bill in parliament on March 27, 2026.

The rules changed in 2024: what the SPK list means

Under Law No. 7518, crypto asset service providers came under the SPK. Existing platforms applied for licences, and the SPK publishes them on a temporary "currently operating" list. One detail matters: the SPK states that being on the list does not mean a platform has been authorised. So these platforms are "on the SPK list"; no trading platform has received a full operating licence yet.

Names on the list include BtcTurk, Paribu, Binance Turkey (binance.tr), OKX TR, Bybit TR, Gate TR, Bitlo, CoinTR and Garanti BBVA's platform. Firms being wound down appear on a separate liquidation list. Before opening an account, check the platform's full company name (ending in A.Ş.) against both lists.

Paying for goods and services with crypto has been banned by a central bank (TCMB) regulation since April 30, 2021. Buying and holding Bitcoin is allowed; paying for a coffee with it is not.

How people in Turkey pay

SPK rules require money to move in and out of platforms only through banks, with no cash. In practice:

  1. Havale. Free and 24/7 at the platform's partner banks (for BtcTurk: Akbank, İş Bankası, VakıfBank, Yapı Kredi and Ziraat).
  2. FAST / EFT. From other banks, instant up to the FAST limit and by EFT above it.
  3. Your own account only. Money from someone else's account is returned or frozen. Enter the reference code the platform asks for.

Transfers to your own wallet or abroad come with waiting times and daily limits. That's normal; it's a MASAK rule.

Scams to watch out for

Turkey lived through the Thodex collapse in 2021: more than 400,000 users were affected and its founder was sentenced to 11,196 years in prison. The most common traps today are fake sites copying well-known brands and leveraged forex or crypto sites promising "guaranteed returns". In July 2025 alone, the SPK blocked access to 46 sites. The rules are simple: check the platform's name on the SPK list, send money only to the platform's A.Ş. company name, and never trade through a "consultant" on WhatsApp or Telegram.

Taxes: the situation in 2026

  • No crypto-specific tax. A bill brought to parliament in March 2026 included a 0.03% transaction tax and a quarterly 10% withholding tax on gains. Those articles were withdrawn in the General Assembly on March 27, 2026.
  • General income tax rules still apply. Frequent, regular trading can count as commercial income, while the taxation of an individual's capital gains is still debated. Inherited crypto is subject to inheritance tax.
  • More data sharing is coming. Under the OECD's crypto reporting framework (CARF), platform data will be shared between tax authorities.

A crypto tax could come back. Record the date, amount and rate of every purchase now.

Where to keep your Bitcoin

If you buy small amounts every month, keeping them on an SPK-listed platform is practical. As the amount grows, many people move coins to a wallet where only they hold the keys. Write the recovery words (12–24 words) on paper, never photograph them and never enter them on any website.

What it means if you buy regularly

You don't have to catch the right moment. Many people buy a fixed amount every week or month, an approach called DCA. Someone who put $10 a day into Bitcoin for 5 years invested $18,260 in total, and it's worth $25,666 today (+40.6%). But Bitcoin has fallen more than 70% from its peak in the past. FAQ

Is buying Bitcoin legal in Turkey?

Yes. Buying and holding it through platforms on the SPK list is legal. What's banned is paying for goods or services with crypto.

Where can I buy Bitcoin in Turkey?

On a platform from the SPK's currently-operating list, with a lira havale, EFT or FAST transfer from a bank account in your name.

Is there a crypto tax in Turkey in 2026?

No crypto-specific tax is in force. The March 2026 plan for a transaction tax and withholding tax was withdrawn in parliament.

What is the crypto withdrawal limit in Turkey?

Under MASAK rules, $3,000 a day for stablecoins and $6,000 a day for other crypto, with at least a 72-hour wait on the first withdrawal.
