NEAR Protocol was one of the weakest large coins on Friday, November 20. The token lost roughly 10–12% in 24 hours and slipped to about $3.02, its market cap rank falling to 23rd — outside the top 20.

What happened

  • Thursday, November 19. NEAR peaked near $3.47 and closed the day at about $3.20, down 3%.
  • Friday, 00:00–04:00 UTC. The slide sped up: NEAR touched about $3.02 in the first four hours of the day and closed that window near $3.05.
  • Heavy trading. Daily volume was about $550 million, high for a coin of its size, which points to selling across the market rather than a quiet drift.
  • Weak momentum. The daily RSI sat around 37, close to oversold territory but not there yet.

Why it moved

We found no NEAR-specific news behind the drop — no hack, token unlock or delisting reported by the sources we checked. The move came with the wider market: bitcoin closed Thursday below its weekend low and fell to about $61,200 early Friday, more than $390 million of positions were liquidated in a day (mostly longs), and the crypto Fear & Greed index dropped to 10, "extreme fear". Higher-risk altcoins such as NEAR usually fall harder than bitcoin in days like this.
