US spot bitcoin ETFs had net outflows of about $1.79 billion last week, the third-largest weekly total since they launched in January 2024 and the fourth negative week in a row. BlackRock's IBIT alone accounted for $1.37 billion, about three quarters of the total.

What happened

  • Record days for IBIT. The fund lost $541.1 million on Thursday and $544.7 million on Friday, its two largest daily outflows ever, per Cointelegraph.
  • November is close to a record. Outflows reached $4.14 billion month to date, about 8% short of June's record $4.51 billion. Total bitcoin ETF assets fell to $71.35 billion from about $110 billion in mid-September, per The Block.
  • Ether funds too. Ether ETFs lost $314 million, their eighth negative week in a row.
  • Analysts are split. Laevitas's Rick Maeda sees mostly risk reduction; LVRG Research said the four-week streak looks more structural. Many point to Wednesday's PCE inflation data and an expected Fed rate hike in December.

The numbers right now

Bitcoin trades at $60,634.00 −1.75% (−32.9% over 12 months) after touching about $55,100 over the weekend, its lowest since September 2024; the record is $126,080.00 (October 2025). IBIT is at $34.62 +4.85%. ETF flows show what big buyers are doing, but they follow the price as often as they lead it.
