FET, the token of the Artificial Superintelligence Alliance (Fetch.ai, SingularityNET and Ocean), rose about 12% in 24 hours on Friday, November 27 — one of the strongest moves among the top 150 coins. We found no project announcement behind it: the jump came on higher trading volume as AI stocks and the wider crypto market bounced.

What happened

  • Friday, Nov 27 (about 06:00–14:00 UTC). FET climbed from about $0.113 to near $0.13, per CoinGecko and Binance data cited by Coin-Turk and The Coin Republic; market value rose to about $310–320 million.
  • More trading. 24-hour volume was about 26–33% higher than the day before, and futures open interest rose about 14% (CoinGlass via The Coin Republic).
  • Other AI tokens lagged. Bittensor (TAO) gained about 2.6% and Render about 4% over the same window (TokenPost).
  • After a deep slide. FET had fallen more than 60% from its early-October high near $0.24–0.25 to a low of about $0.10.

Why did FET go up?

None of the reports names a FET-specific trigger, and TokenPost notes that one rebound does not confirm a trend change. The bounce lines up with Friday's relief rally in AI and chip stocks and bitcoin's climb back above $60,000. Two scheduled events are worth knowing: about 2.4 million FET unlock on November 28 for the SingularityNET token migration (about 0.1% of supply), and, according to Crypto News Flash, Korean exchange Bithumb plans to stop FET trading on December 7.

The numbers right now

FET trades at $0.1282 +12.8% (−49.9% over 12 months), far below its record of $3.45 (March 2024). See our from-ATH page and the AI page.
