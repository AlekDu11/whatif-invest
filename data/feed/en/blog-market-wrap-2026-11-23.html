Monday, November 23 was the rebound everyone was waiting for. Nvidia's board brought back Jensen Huang as CEO, the stock jumped about 31%, and the Nasdaq rose 6.5% — its best day since April 2025 — while the S&P 500 gained 4.7% and broke a seven-day losing streak. Crypto joined in more quietly: Bitcoin ended near $61,560, up 3.1% in 24 hours (live: $59,625.80 −2.85%).

Key takeaways

  • S&P 500 +4.73% to 7,237, Nasdaq +6.51% to 24,090, Dow +3.01% (+1,448 points) — Nvidia +31.5% to about $165.
  • The VIX fear index fell almost 26% to 26.6; gold futures rose 3.4% to about $3,636 and US crude fell 3.8% to about $80.
  • Bitcoin +3.1% and Ethereum +2.6% to about $1,728; 37 of 44 coins worth $1 billion or more rose.
  • The Crypto Fear & Greed Index is still at 11/100 — "extreme fear" — the same as a day ago and 10 a week ago.

The big picture: stocks lead, crypto follows slowly

After the worst week since April 2025, stocks bounced hard. The S&P 500 had been more than 10% below its record; on Monday it added 326.8 points to 7,237.17. The Nasdaq-100 fund QQQ rose about 7%. Small companies joined in too: the Russell 2000 gained 3.4%.

Gold had its best day in weeks, +3.4%, after a 4.6% fall on Friday, and silver rose about 3%. Oil went the other way: US crude futures lost 3.8% to about $80.

Crypto rose, but less than tech stocks. Total crypto market value was about $2.09 trillion (CoinGecko) and Bitcoin's share was 58.9%. The mood barely changed: Fear & Greed stayed at 11. Among the 20 largest coins the biggest mover was Canton, +4.3%, just ahead of Hyperliquid +4.1% and Solana +4.0%.

Biggest winners

Large stocks (closing moves) and coins worth $1 billion or more (24 hours):

  • Nvidia +31.5% to about $165, after Friday's −14% — the board unanimously reinstated Huang (our brief).
  • Micron +19.0% — it makes the memory that sits next to Nvidia's chips; it had lost 23.6% last week (brief).
  • KLA +15.0% and TSMC +14.0% — the whole chip chain bounced with Nvidia.
  • CoreWeave +22.3% and IREN about +29% — AI cloud renters rose with Nvidia (brief).
  • Quant +7.7%, Internet Computer +5.4% and OKB +5.1% — the best large coins; no specific news found for any of them.

Biggest losers

  • CrowdStrike −8.9% — investors did not like the price of its $43.9 billion all-stock deal for Zscaler, which rose 17.7% (brief).
  • Anthropic −1.4% — one of the few AI names that fell, in only its third trading day.
  • Rain −3.7% — the weakest $1B+ coin again; it keeps giving back its jump of November 16. No clear cause.
  • Mantle −0.9%, World Liberty Financial −0.7% and MemeCore −0.7% — small dips while almost everything else rose.

The news that moved markets

  1. Huang is back at Nvidia. Eleven days after he stepped down, the board voted unanimously to reinstate him as CEO, saying input from senior US government officials left it confident his return poses no national security risk. The internal investigation continues. President Trump had urged the rehire overnight (premarket brief).
  2. CrowdStrike to buy Zscaler. An all-stock deal worth about $43.9 billion, a 34% premium at Friday's prices — one of the largest cybersecurity deals ever.
  3. Bitcoin ETFs: fourth week of outflows. US spot funds lost $1.79 billion last week, the third-largest weekly outflow on record; November's total is $4.14 billion so far (brief).
  4. Asia steadied markets. Korea's KOSPI hit a 10-month low before recovering, while Taiwan tightened short-selling rules and China eased margin rules (brief).
  5. Another bridge exploit. Fusionist's ACE bridge lost more than $5 million in two waves — the third bridge incident in a week (brief). The UK also said exchanges may not trade against their own customers from 2027 (brief).

What to watch this week

  • Wednesday, Nov 25: US PCE inflation for October, the second estimate of Q3 GDP, durable goods and jobless claims.

What it means if you buy regularly

A day like Monday can make up for much of a bad week — but nobody knew it was coming on Friday, which is why steady buyers do not try to time it. Someone who put $10 a day into Bitcoin over the last year ($3,650 in total) now has $3,014 (−17.4%). The same habit in Ethereum stands at −21.5%, in the S&P 500 at +0.53%, in gold at −16.2% and in Nvidia at −15.4%.
