Money finally came back into US spot Solana ETFs. The funds recorded about $11.6 million of net inflows on November 17, their first day of new money in 24 days, and SOL rose about 3% to around $75 on Wednesday.

What happened

  • Two funds did the work. Bitwise's BSOL took in about $10.8 million and VanEck's VSOL about $0.9 million. Some trackers date the flow to November 16 or show flat flows, as issuers report on different days.
  • Small next to the total. Since their launch in late 2025, the Solana funds have gathered about $1.42 billion in net inflows; one $11.6 million day is under 1% of that.
  • The previous inflow was on October 24. In between, SOL fell to a four-month low of $67.41 over the weekend.
  • Fear still rules. Bitcoin is near $64,600 and the Crypto Fear & Greed Index stood at 11 on Wednesday, its sixth day in a row below 25.

The numbers right now

SOL trades at $72.81, roughly 75% below its all-time high of $293.31 from January 19, 2025. Compare how far every top coin is from its peak on our from-ATH page.

What it means if you buy regularly

One day of inflows is not a trend change. A $10-a-day SOL plan over 5 years has put in $18,260 and is now worth $25,090 (+37.4%); the same plan in bitcoin stands at $26,166 (+43.3%).
