One of the Federal Reserve's most influential governors has put a bigger rate hike on the table. In a CNBC interview on Wednesday, Christopher Waller said the Fed may have to raise rates by half a percentage point in December, double what markets expect, and bitcoin slid below $63,000 in the hours that followed.

What happened

  • A tougher tone. Waller said he is very worried that inflation is heading the wrong way and spreading, and that the Fed may need to "shock people". He suggested undoing all three of the 2025 "insurance" cuts and possibly going further.
  • The 50-point condition. If the committee does not clearly signal more hikes, he said, it should move by 50 basis points in December to get it done.
  • Markets still expect 25. CME FedWatch showed odds above 95% for a quarter-point hike at the December 8–9 meeting.
  • Crypto took the hit. Bitcoin fell about 3% on the day to under $63,000 after trading between $64,000 and $66,000 since November 15; ether dropped about 2% to near $1,740. The Fear & Greed Index sat at 11, "extreme fear". The S&P 500 was down about 0.3%.

What it means if you buy regularly

Higher rates make cash and bonds more attractive and tend to weigh on bitcoin and growth stocks. Bitcoin is at $62,043.90, far below its $126,080 peak (see our from-ATH page).
