  1. Commonwealth Bank — Australia's largest bank and the most valuable company on the ASX. $112.59 −0.41%

How to buy Australian stocks

  • The ASX trades 10:00–16:00 Sydney time on weekdays; prices are in Australian dollars.
  • The main index is the S&P/ASX 200; an ASX 200 ETF buys the 200 largest companies in one go.
  • BHP and Rio Tinto also trade in New York as ADRs (BHP, RIO), so they can be bought in dollars. Check what your broker offers and its currency fees.
  • See every Australian stock we track, sorted by market cap: Australia stocks.

What it means if you buy regularly

Regular buying in the biggest Australian companies grew the money over five years but lagged a plain S&P 500 plan, and the result depended heavily on two sectors: banks and miners make up most of the list, so iron-ore prices and Australian interest rates move it. A broad ASX 200 or global fund spreads that risk. Past results, not a forecast.
