What was the 2023–2024 memecoin wave? After the crypto crash of 2022, three new memecoins — PEPE on Ethereum, BONK and dogwifhat (WIF) on Solana — went from zero to billions of dollars in market value within months. They made a few early buyers rich, pulled millions of newcomers in, and then showed how fast memecoins can fall.

Key takeaways

  • PEPE launched in April 2023 and passed a $1.6 billion market value in about three weeks, then lost much of it within days. Today it trades at $0.0000036 +3.75%.
  • BONK was given away for free on 25 December 2022 to the Solana community, which was hurt by the FTX collapse. It became Solana's first big memecoin.
  • WIF, a picture of a dog in a knitted hat, launched in November 2023 and rose above $4 in March 2024. A crowdfunding to put the dog on the Las Vegas Sphere collected about $700,000 — and the ad never happened.

This lesson continues the story of Shiba Inu. New words like "airdrop" or "market value" are explained in the memecoin glossary.

Chapter 1 — April 2023: PEPE, the frog with no promises

Pepe the Frog is an old internet cartoon. In mid-April 2023 an anonymous team launched a PEPE token on Ethereum with a very honest website: it said the coin had no value, no team roadmap and was "for entertainment purposes only".

People bought it anyway. Within about three weeks its market value (price × number of coins) passed $1.6 billion, Fortune reported on 9 May 2023. Big exchanges such as Binance listed it, and stories of tiny bets turning into millions spread on social media. Then the price fell sharply in the following days, and many people who bought on the hype were left with losses.

PEPE did not disappear: it came back in the 2024 rally and set its record of $0.00002803 (December 2024). Today it is −87.2% from that record, with a market value of $1.5 B.

Chapter 2 — December 2022: BONK, a Christmas gift for Solana

In November 2022 the exchange FTX collapsed. FTX had been a big supporter of the Solana network, so SOL's price crashed and its community was depressed.

On 25 December 2022 an anonymous group launched BONK, a Shiba Inu-style dog coin, and gave half of all tokens away for free through an airdrop (free tokens sent to many wallets) — to Solana developers, artists and NFT holders. In the first days of January 2023 BONK jumped many times over, and TechCrunch reported that SOL's own price rose with it as people bought SOL to trade BONK.

BONK became the "community coin" of Solana. In December 2023 large exchanges listed it, and it rose again in the 2024 memecoin rally. Its 12-month change today is −75.9%.

Chapter 3 — November 2023: WIF, a dog in a hat

dogwifhat (WIF) is literally a photo of a Shiba Inu dog wearing a pink knitted hat. It launched on Solana in November 2023 with no product at all.

In early 2024 memecoins on Solana became the hottest trade in crypto. WIF climbed above $4 in late March 2024 and for a while was the third-largest memecoin after Dogecoin and Shiba Inu, passing PEPE.

Fans then crowdfunded about $700,000 to show the dog on the giant Las Vegas Sphere screen. In 2024 DL News reported that the ad had not run and the organisers were sitting on the money. A good reminder: in memecoins, a "community" promise usually has no contract behind it. Today WIF trades at $0.1272 +3.33%, −97.4% from its record.

Chapter 4 — 2024: the wave becomes a flood

The success of BONK and WIF led to tools that let anyone create a token in a minute (the next lesson is about one of them, pump.fun). Thousands of copies appeared every day. A few made early buyers rich; most fell close to zero within hours or days. Many buyers who arrived late, after the big headlines, bought near the top.

What beginners can learn from it

  1. The winners you hear about are the survivors. PEPE, BONK and WIF are remembered because they made it. Thousands of frog and dog coins from the same months are worth almost nothing.
  2. Headlines arrive after the biggest jump. By the time a memecoin is on the news, early holders are often selling to new buyers.
  3. A free airdrop is not free money forever. People who received BONK and sold some early locked in gains; others watched it fall and rise and fall again.
  4. Promises are not contracts. The Sphere ad shows that money collected by a "community" can simply not be spent as promised.

⚠️ Watch out

  • Copycat tokens. "PEPE 2.0", "Baby BONK", "WIF on Base"… Always check the real contract address before buying (see How to check a memecoin).
  • Fake airdrop sites. "Claim your free BONK" pages ask you to connect your wallet and then empty it.
  • Buying because a coin "already went up 1000%". Past jumps say nothing about tomorrow, and memecoins can fall 50% in a day.

What it means if you buy regularly

Buying a small amount on a schedule means you never put everything in on the most exciting day. $10 a day in PEPE for 2 years ($7,300 in total) became $5,153 (−29.4%); the same in BONK became $2,442. Past results, not a forecast — with memecoins keep it to a tiny share of your money. Follow today's memecoins on our memes page and the prices on the PEPE, BONK and WIF pages.
