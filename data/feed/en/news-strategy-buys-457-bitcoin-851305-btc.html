Strategy confirmed the purchase Michael Saylor hinted at on Sunday: the company bought 457 bitcoin for about $29.6 million last week, paying an average of $64,898 per coin, according to its filing with the SEC. It now holds 851,305 BTC, bought at an average of $75,446 each — well above where bitcoin traded on Tuesday morning, around $60,000–61,000.

What happened

  • A small buy. 457 BTC is one of Strategy's smallest weekly purchases this autumn, after 485 BTC the week before. It was paid for by selling common stock.
  • Buybacks too. The company also bought back $211.8 million of its STRC preferred shares.
  • A rough week for the shares. MSTR fell 30.6% last week as bitcoin dropped toward $55,000 before bouncing. MSCI plans to drop Strategy from affected indexes after the November 30 close.
  • Weak demand elsewhere. US spot bitcoin ETFs lost about $1.8 billion last week, their fourth straight week of outflows.

What it means if you buy regularly

Strategy keeps buying through the drop, but its average cost is now above the market price, so the stack is under water on paper.
