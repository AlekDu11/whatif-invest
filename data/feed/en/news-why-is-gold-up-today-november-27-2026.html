Gold is up today because buyers stepped back in after a steep slide: COMEX gold futures rose about 2.7% to roughly $3,662 an ounce early on Friday, November 27, and silver jumped almost 8% to about $43.7. Analysts point to bargain buying after metals hit roughly 14-month lows, a calmer mood in tech stocks and thin trading after the US Thanksgiving holiday. No single piece of news triggered the move.

What happened

  • Overnight jump (UTC). By 03:25 COMEX gold traded near $3,632 (+2.7% vs Wednesday's close, per Upstox) and by 04:41 at about $3,662, close to the day's high of $3,664.6 (Yahoo Finance data, via CryptoTicker). Silver reached about $43.4–43.7, up 8%.
  • India followed. MCX gold futures hit ₹1,25,151 per 10 grams (+2.35%) and silver ₹1,53,954 per kg (+5.5%) by mid-morning in Mumbai.
  • Thin market. US stock markets were shut on Thursday and close early on Friday, so fewer trades can move prices further than usual.
  • The dollar barely moved. The dollar index slipped only 0.04% to 103.73, so a weaker dollar does not explain the jump.

Why is gold rising after its fall?

Gold had a brutal autumn: it closed at a record of about $5,318 in late January 2026 and fell to an intraday low near $3,425 on November 25. Kedia Advisory's Amit Gupta told Business Today the rebound came as tech and AI stocks steadied, which eased forced selling of metals to raise cash. Upstox also cites value buying near the lows. The headwinds remain: strong US data keeps a December Fed rate hike in play, and higher rates make gold, which pays no interest, less attractive. UBS still sees central-bank and Chinese buying as long-term support.

The numbers right now

Gold trades at $3,639.60 +2.94% (−14.5% over 12 months) and silver at $43.17 +7.78% (−23.5%). Gold's record on our data is $4,745.10 (January 2026); see how far each asset is from its peak on our from-ATH page.

What it means if you buy regularly

One good day does not undo a 30% fall, and a rebound in thin holiday trade can fade just as fast. Someone who put $10 a day into gold for 5 years has invested $18,260, now worth $27,363 (+49.9%); the same plan in silver is $27,036 and in bitcoin $25,154. That is history, not a forecast.
