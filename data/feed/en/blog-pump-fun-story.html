What is pump.fun? pump.fun is a website on the Solana blockchain, launched in January 2024, where anyone can create a memecoin in a minute without paying a creation fee. It became the biggest memecoin factory in crypto — and its own data shows that in most weeks fewer than 2 out of every 100 new tokens ever reached a real exchange.

Key takeaways

  • On pump.fun a new token first trades on a bonding curve (a built-in price formula: the more people buy, the higher the price). Only when it reaches about $100,000 in market value does it "graduate" to a normal DEX (decentralised exchange).
  • The graduation rate peaked at about 2.1% in June 2024 and was below 1% for four weeks in a row from 17 February 2025 (Dune data, reported by Cointelegraph).
  • The platform itself earned a lot: its fees passed $800 million in total by August 2025, and in July 2025 it raised $600 million selling its own PUMP token in 12 minutes.

How pump.fun works, in plain words

  1. Someone creates a token. They choose a name, a ticker and a picture. Creating it costs no fee.
  2. It trades on the bonding curve. The first buyers pay the lowest price. Each new buy moves the price up, each sale moves it down. Words like this are explained in our memecoin glossary.
  3. Graduation. When enough tokens are sold (about 800 million of the supply) and the market value reaches about $100,000, pump.fun moves the token to a DEX with real liquidity (money in the trading pool) — first Raydium, later its own PumpSwap.
  4. Or nothing. Most tokens never get there. A few people buy, the creator or early buyers sell, and the chart goes flat within hours.

The story in dates and numbers

January 2024 — launch. pump.fun started on Solana right as the BONK and WIF wave (see the previous lesson) made people want "the next one". Creating a coin became as easy as posting a photo.

May 2024 — the first big incident. A former employee used his access to withdraw about $1.9 million from the platform's contracts. Trading was stopped and the contracts were redeployed.

June 2024 — the best graduation rate. About 2.1% of new tokens graduated — roughly 1 in 50.

November 2024 — a flood of tokens. In the week starting 11 November 2024, about 323,000 tokens were created; 1.67% of them graduated, about 5,400 tokens.

January 2025 — the peak. On 23 January 2025 a record 71,735 tokens were launched in a single day. Daily platform revenue peaked at about $15.4 million around 25 January. That month 24,008 tokens graduated.

January 2025 — lawsuits. A class action in the US claimed the platform sold unregistered securities; an amended complaint in July 2025 added racketeering (RICO) claims. These are claims, not court findings.

February 2025 — the drop. After the memecoin frenzy cooled, launches fell to 25,385 on 26 February 2025, daily revenue to about $1.1 million, and the graduation rate stayed under 1% for four straight weeks.

July 2025 — its own token. On 12 July 2025 pump.fun sold 15% of its PUMP token supply at $0.004 per token and raised $600 million in about 12 minutes. PUMP is at $0.002614 +7.00% right now (−11.3% over 12 months).

August 2025 — $800 million. Total fees the platform collected passed $800 million.

Who made money?

Look at the two sides. The platform earned a fee on almost every trade, whether the token went up or crashed. Many buyers paid fees too, bought after the creator or bots, and then held tokens nobody wanted. When 98 out of 100 tokens never even reach a DEX, the odds for a random buyer are poor.

What beginners can learn from it

  1. Easy to create means easy to copy. Anyone can make a token in a minute — including scammers. A new token is not "early", it is usually just new.
  2. The first seconds belong to bots. Insiders and fast programs buy first and sell into the crowd. You are almost never first.
  3. Check before you buy. Use the 6 checks from the lesson How to check a memecoin before buying and the safety light on our memecoins page, which also shows the launch pulse (how many tokens appear per hour).
  4. The shovel seller wins. In a gold rush, the people selling shovels often earn most. Here, the shovel is the platform fee.

⚠️ Watch out

  • "Next 100x" groups. Telegram and X groups pushing a fresh pump.fun token often bought it first and sell to you.
  • Fake copies. Tokens with the same name and picture as a popular coin appear within minutes. Always check the contract address.
  • Gambling mood. Fast charts and small amounts make it feel like a game. Set a limit you can lose completely and stop there.

What it means if you buy regularly

Buying a little every month works for assets that are expected to still exist in five years. Brand-new launchpad tokens mostly are not. If you want regular buys, beginners usually start with large, long-running assets — compare them with our calculator — and keep memecoins, if any, as a tiny share.
