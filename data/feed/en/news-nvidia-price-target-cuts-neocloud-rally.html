Nvidia's record quarter brought buyers back to AI stocks on Wednesday, but Wall Street turned more cautious on the stock at the same time. Shares traded about 3–4% above Tuesday's close before the open, while four brokers cut their price targets even after the beat.

What happened

  • A relief bounce. Nvidia closed at $155.46 on Tuesday, ending a four-day losing run, and changed hands near $161.90 after hours (+4.1%) once it reported $115.8 billion in revenue and guided to $130 billion.
  • Neoclouds lead the rebound. AI cloud renters CoreWeave and Nebius jumped before the open. Both had lost more than half their value in three months; Nvidia owns over 10% of CoreWeave. AMD and Broadcom also gained.
  • Conditional support. Nvidia disclosed $32 billion of commitments to neocloud companies through 2032, but it can scale them back if those firms fail to raise money or deliver data centers on time.
  • Lower targets anyway. Piper Sandler cut its target from $300 to $230, Wells Fargo from $300 to $250, UBS from $240 to $220 and Evercore ISI from $186 to $184. The average target is about $230.
