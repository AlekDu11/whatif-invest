How do you create a Binance Wallet? Open the Binance app, switch to the Wallet tab at the top, tap Create Keyless Wallet and back it up with a recovery password. It takes a few minutes, needs no seed phrase, and gives you a self-custody wallet (a wallet where you, not the exchange, control the coins) for buying on-chain tokens such as memecoins.

Key takeaways

  • Binance Wallet lives inside the Binance app. You need a verified Binance account, but the coins in the wallet are self-custody — you are responsible for the backup.
  • It is a "keyless" (MPC) wallet: the key is split into 3 shares — on your phone, in your cloud backup and with Binance. Any 2 of them open the wallet.
  • Never skip the backup. If you lose your phone and forget the recovery password, Binance cannot restore the wallet.

Before you start: most new memecoins lose almost all their value. Only use money you can afford to lose completely — see what memecoins are.

What you need

  • A phone with the official Binance app (download it only from the App Store, Google Play or binance.com — fake apps are common).
  • A Binance account with identity verification (KYC — showing your ID) done. If you do not have one yet, you can sign up through our link: Binance Wallet.
  • A little BNB or SOL in your Binance account to buy with and to pay network fees.

Step 1 — Open the wallet

  1. Log in to the Binance app.
  2. At the top of the screen, switch from Exchange to Wallet.
  3. Tap Create Keyless Wallet. (If you already have another wallet, the other option is Import Wallet — beginners do not need it.)

Creation takes seconds. The app does not show you 12 or 24 seed words: instead, it uses MPC (multi-party computation — the key is split into pieces that are never in one place).

Step 2 — Back it up (do not skip this)

The app offers three ways to save your backup share. Pick one:

  • iCloud or Google Drive. You set a recovery password that only you know. The share is encrypted with it and saved to your cloud.
  • QR code. You set a recovery password and save a QR code; the share is stored in a decentralised cloud.
  • Quick Backup. You confirm with Face ID or fingerprint; no password, but the share is held by a third-party provider.

Write the recovery password on paper and keep it somewhere safe. Then check: tap Settings (top left on Home or Assets) → Wallet Management. You should see the Backed Up tag.

Step 3 — Move coins from Binance into the wallet

  1. In the wallet, tap Receive → Withdraw from Binance Exchange.
  2. Choose the coin (for example BNB) and the network (BNB Smart Chain for BNB, Solana for SOL). The network must match.
  3. Enter the amount. The app shows the fee and the final amount before you confirm.
  4. Choose whether the money comes from your Spot or Funding account, tap Withdraw, check the address and network, and Confirm.

Start with a small test amount. A confirmed transfer cannot be cancelled. Always leave a little BNB or SOL in the wallet: every action on the blockchain costs a network fee (gas), even a failed one.

Step 4 — Find the right token by its contract address

Many memecoins have copies with the same name and logo. The only reliable ID is the contract address (a long string of letters and numbers that is unique to each token).

  1. Get the address from a trusted source: the project's verified account, a big data site or a token page on our memecoins page.
  2. In the wallet's Markets or Trade screen, paste the address into search instead of typing the name.
  3. Compare the first and last 4–6 characters with your source.

Step 5 — Check the token before buying

On our memecoins page, each token page shows Binance's security check and the key numbers. Look at:

  • Liquidity — how much money is in the trading pool. Little liquidity = you may not be able to sell.
  • Holders and top-10 share — if a few wallets hold most of the supply, they can crash the price.
  • Age — a token created an hour ago is a lottery ticket.
  • Warnings — if any risk or security warning appears, stop.

Binance itself says it does not check the tokens listed on Meme Rush (its feed of brand-new memecoins), so the checking is on you. Our scams and rug pulls lesson lists the warning signs.

Step 6 — Swap

  1. Open Trade → Swap. Choose what you pay with (BNB) and the token you want, on the same network.
  2. Enter a small amount. Read the rate, the slippage (the biggest price change you accept before the trade fails) and the network fee.
  3. Some tokens need an Approve step first; it costs a small fee.
  4. Tap Swap and confirm with your passkey or 2FA.

To sell, do the same in reverse. If you want to see the very newest tokens, the Markets tab has Meme Rush: brand-new tokens trade there on a "bonding curve" and cannot be moved out of the wallet until they "migrate" to a normal DEX (decentralised exchange). This is the riskiest corner of the market.

⚠️ Watch out

  • No backup, or a password saved in screenshots. Lose the phone and the password and the coins are gone for good.
  • Wrong network or wrong address. Binance's FAQ says tokens sent to a wrong address cannot be refunded. Always send a test amount.
  • Fake tokens and "support" messages. Search by contract address only. Binance staff will never ask for your recovery password or to "verify" your wallet on a website.
