How to buy Canadian stocks

  • The Toronto Stock Exchange (TSX) trades 9:30–16:00 Eastern time on weekdays; prices are in Canadian dollars.
  • The main index is the S&P/TSX Composite; the S&P/TSX 60 covers the 60 largest companies. A TSX 60 or Canada ETF buys them all in one go.
  • Most of the top 10 — the five banks, Shopify, Enbridge, Canadian Natural, Brookfield and Agnico Eagle — are also listed in New York, so any broker with US stocks can buy them in US dollars. Check what your broker offers and its currency fees.
  • See every Canadian stock we track, sorted by market cap: Canada stocks.

What it means if you buy regularly

Regular buying in the biggest Canadian companies grew the money by about 80% over five years — but a big part came from gold miners in a record gold market and from banks in a period of higher interest rates, which may not repeat. Buying the telecoms every day lost money even though they were known as safe dividend stocks. A broad TSX 60 fund spreads that sector risk; Canada is still heavy in banks, energy and mining, so many investors pair it with global funds. Past results, not a forecast.
