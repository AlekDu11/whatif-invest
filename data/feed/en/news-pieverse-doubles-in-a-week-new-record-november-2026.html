Pieverse, the BNB Chain project that builds payment rails and messenger-based assistants for AI agents, set another record on Sunday, November 22. The token has roughly doubled in a week and now has a market value of about $1 billion, while bitcoin is still trading far below its own peak.

What happened

  • New all-time high on November 22. CoinGecko data showed an intraday high of about $3.75 in the early hours (UTC); CoinPaprika had PIEVERSE at about $4.22 at 08:50 UTC. Prices differ between data providers and exchanges.
  • A steep climb. Per CoinPaprika the token was up about 40% in 24 hours and about 112% in seven days; CoinGecko showed +84% in a week and about +246% in 30 days.
  • Wide daily swings. On CoinGecko the 24-hour range ran from about $2.84 to $3.75, a gap of roughly 32%.
  • Previous record broken. Our last update on November 18 covered a jump to about $3.17; that level has now been left behind.

Why it moved

Pieverse has been pushing its AI agent products. It launched "Confidential Agents", a security layer that protects conversations, files and credentials inside its Purr-Fect Claw agent service (early access, invited users only), and announced a partnership with the BNB Chain launch platform Four.Meme, without a format or timeline. The wider market also helped: bitcoin bounced from about $55,100 to above $60,000 over the weekend as short sellers were squeezed.

Two risks stand out. About 72% of the 1 billion PIEVERSE tokens are not yet circulating, and a large investor-and-team unlock was scheduled for November 14. As of early November, 25 large wallets held about 97% of the supply, according to Interactivecrypto. Few holders and thin trading can move the price sharply in both directions.

The numbers right now

PIEVERSE trades at $3.88 +28.2%. Its record is $3.92 (November 2026), so it is trading close to the top. Over 12 months the token has changed by +809%. Compare with other coins on our from-ATH page.

What it means if you buy regularly

Buying after a 100% week means buying near the most expensive point of the year, and young, concentrated tokens often give back a large part of such gains. Spreading purchases over time softens the timing risk but does not remove it. Someone who put $10 a day into PIEVERSE over the past year has invested $3,650 and now holds $20,105 (+451%). The same plan in bitcoin is worth $2,986. That is history, not a forecast. Try other amounts in the DCA calculator.
