How to buy German stocks

  • Xetra in Frankfurt trades 9:00–17:30 Central European Time on weekdays; prices are in euros.
  • The main index is the DAX with Germany's 40 largest companies (expanded from 30 in September 2021). A DAX ETF or fund buys all of them at once.
  • Outside Europe, SAP and Deutsche Bank are also listed in New York, and the US-listed iShares MSCI Germany ETF (EWG) holds the big names. Check what your broker offers and its currency fees.
  • More on the market itself, sectors and the DAX: our guide to German stocks.

What it means if you buy regularly

Regular buying in the biggest German companies worked over five years — but the spread was huge, from Siemens Energy's +366% to Volkswagen's −25.1%. Picking the famous brands would have lost money, and anyone who started a year ago is roughly flat. A plan on a DAX fund or many stocks depends far less on picking the winning sector.
