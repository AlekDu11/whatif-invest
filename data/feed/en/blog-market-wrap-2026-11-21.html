Saturday, November 21 was a crypto-only day — Wall Street was closed — and it had two halves. Early in the day Bitcoin fell to about $55,130, its lowest level since September 2024, as forced selling continued after Friday's stock rout. Then it climbed more than 9% and ended the day near $60,200, up 1.8% in 24 hours (live: $59,230.00 +5.08%).

Key takeaways

  • Bitcoin +1.8% to about $60,206 and Ethereum +3.4% to $1,681 in 24 hours; Bitcoin is still −6.9% for the week.
  • 27 of 46 coins worth $1 billion or more rose, after only 1 of 45 on Friday.
  • About $1.8 billion of leveraged crypto bets were wiped out in 24 hours, $1.3 billion of them bets on a rise (CoinGlass via CoinDesk).
  • The Crypto Fear & Greed Index reads 12/100 — "extreme fear" — the same as a day ago and close to 13 a week ago.

The big picture: a weekend bounce, not a new trend

Stocks, gold and oil did not trade on Saturday, so Friday's closes still stand: the S&P 500 fund SPY lost 8.6% over the week, the Nasdaq-100 fund QQQ 10.5%, gold 9.5% and silver 16.3%. Brent oil ended Friday near $92.6.

Crypto moved on its own. Total crypto market value was about $2.09 trillion late on Saturday (CoinGecko), with Bitcoin's share at 57.9%. The rebound was broad but small: most large coins gained 1–3%. Analysts at Glassnode said a cluster of leveraged positions between $59,000 and $62,000 had been cleared, removing a large source of forced selling. CryptoQuant called the drop "extreme capitulation" — holders locked in more than $11 billion of losses in seven days.

The mood did not change. Fear & Greed stayed at 12, near its lowest readings of the year, and US spot Bitcoin ETFs lost about $1.1 billion over the past week.

Biggest winners

Among coins worth $1 billion or more, in 24 hours:

  • Venice Token +18.3% to about $24.5 — the biggest gain; we found no confirmed news behind it.
  • Uniswap +6.1% — no single cause found; DeFi tokens bounced with the market.
  • Zcash +4.9% to about $784 — the biggest move among the 20 largest assets, a rebound after it fell about 21% and dipped below $700 earlier (our brief).
  • Bitway +3.7% — no clear reason.
  • Ethereum +3.4% — the best of the very large coins, after a −16% week.

Biggest losers

  • MemeCore −10.9% — the weakest $1B+ coin; we found no specific news.
  • Internet Computer −6.7% — no clear reason.
  • Polkadot −3.7% and Aster −3.2% — missed the rebound, no single cause found.
  • Gram (prev. Toncoin) −3.0% and Litecoin −2.4% — small moves against the trend.
  • Outside the $1B group, Chainflip's FLIP fell about a third after a hack (see below).

The news that moved markets

  1. Bitcoin's two-year low and rebound. The low of $55,130 came early on Saturday; by the afternoon Bitcoin was back above $60,000 (brief).
  2. Big holders under water. At Saturday's low, Strategy and BitMine together showed a paper loss of about $27.9 billion on their Bitcoin and Ether (brief).
  3. Chainflip hack. About $4.86 million was drained from the cross-chain swap protocol's Ethereum vault; swaps were paused (brief).
  4. ETF money keeps leaving. XRP funds are heading for their worst month of outflows, and Bitcoin funds lost about $1.1 billion in a week (brief).
  5. Kraken flash drop. Bitcoin briefly hit C$24,250 on Kraken's Canadian-dollar market; the exchange said its systems worked correctly and the trades stand.

What to watch this week

  • Monday, Nov 23: stock markets reopen in the US (Japan is closed for a holiday); November flash PMI surveys worldwide.
  • Tuesday, Nov 24: US consumer confidence and Germany's Ifo survey.
  • Wednesday, Nov 25: US PCE inflation for October, the second estimate of Q3 GDP, durable goods, jobless claims and the Fed's Beige Book.
  • Thursday, Nov 26: US markets closed for Thanksgiving; ECB minutes. Friday, Nov 27: US stocks close early.

What it means if you buy regularly

A bounce of 9% in a few hours shows how fast crypto can move both ways — it tells us little about next week. Someone who put $10 a day into Bitcoin over the last year ($3,650 in total) now has $2,991 (−18.1%). The same habit in Ethereum stands at −22.6%, in the S&P 500 at −3.69% and in gold at −18.6%.
