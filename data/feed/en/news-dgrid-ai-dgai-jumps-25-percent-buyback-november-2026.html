DGrid AI (DGAI), the token of a decentralized network for running AI models, was one of the biggest gainers among the 200 largest cryptocurrencies on Sunday, up about 25% in 24 hours. The jump came two days after the project said it will use revenue to buy back and burn its own tokens, and as the whole crypto market bounced.

What happened

  • November 20, ~19:46 UTC. DGrid announced a buyback-and-burn program: profit from every renewal of its paid Premium plan goes to buying DGAI on the market, at least half of the renewal revenue, and the bought tokens are sent to a burn address. The team promised to publish amounts and transaction hashes each period.
  • November 21–22. DGAI climbed about 25% in a day to around $0.89–0.93, with a 24-hour range of roughly $0.69 to $0.98. Its market value is about $133 million (rank ~194).
  • Market backdrop. Bitcoin rebounded about 6% from a two-year low and 91% of coins rose over 24 hours, per CoinCodex.

Why it moved

The buyback news is the only project-specific trigger we found. The program has no fixed size — it depends on how many users pay for Premium — so its real effect on supply is still unknown. Only 150 million of 1 billion DGAI (15%) are in circulation, so future unlocks can add selling pressure.

The numbers right now

DGAI trades at $0.9911 +20.8%. Its record is $1.14 (September 2026); its low was about $0.36 in August 2026, right after launch. See the from-ATH page and other AI tokens.

What it means if you buy regularly

DGAI only started trading in August 2026, too short a history for a fair «$10 a day» test. For comparison, $10 a day into bitcoin over the last year: $3,650 invested, now $3,099 (−15.1%). That is history, not a forecast. Young tokens can swing 25% either way in a day — try amounts in the calculator.
