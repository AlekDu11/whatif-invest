Another DeFi vault was emptied on Thursday, November 19. K3 Capital's sBOLD savings vault on Ethereum — a yield product built on Liquity's BOLD stablecoin — lost about $3.4 million in an attack that took roughly eight minutes and 97 transactions.

What happened

  • A pricing flaw. The attacker repeatedly deposited BOLD and slipped extra WETH into the vault, inflating the value of each sBOLD share, then withdrew other depositors' money at that inflated price.
  • Bots took a cut. MEV bots copied the attacker's transactions and captured about $1.9 million, paying most of it to block builders as fees. The attacker reportedly kept around $520,000.
  • A known risk. Audit firm ChainSecurity had flagged a similar risk in a May 2025 review, according to the report.
  • No recovery yet. The team says it is tracking the funds, assessing losses for depositors and fixing the code.

What it means for ordinary investors

Small yield vaults add a layer of smart-contract risk on top of the crypto itself — and 2026 has already been a heavy year for hacks.
