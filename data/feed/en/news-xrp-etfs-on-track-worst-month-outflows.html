US spot XRP exchange-traded funds are heading for their weakest month since launch. Investors have pulled a net $30.76 million from them so far in November, and with more than a week left, the funds are about $1 million away from their record month of outflows.

What happened

  • Steady exits. Per SoSoValue data cited by U.Today, XRP ETFs have not had a single day of net inflows this month.
  • Close to a record. The only previous month with net outflows was March, at about $31.2 million.
  • Same picture in bitcoin. Spot bitcoin ETFs lost about $3.5 billion from the start of November to November 19, including $582 million on Thursday alone, per Farside Investors.
  • Price slide. XRP fell about 10% in 24 hours to near $0.87 early on November 21 as bitcoin dropped below $56,000.

What our data shows

XRP trades at $0.8969. Its all-time high was $3.65 in July 2025, so it is about 76% below the top; bitcoin is about 54% below its own record. Fund outflows show that larger investors are cutting risk, but they are a snapshot of sentiment, not a forecast. See all assets on our from-ATH page.
