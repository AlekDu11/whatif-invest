The crypto sell-off deepened overnight. Bitcoin dropped below $56,000 early on Saturday, November 21, the lowest level since September 2024, after Friday's rout on Wall Street spilled into digital assets.

What happened

  • New low. Bitcoin fell about 9.9% in 24 hours to roughly $55,790 at 05:56 UTC, per Investing.com, breaking below this year's summer low. On Thursday evening it was still above $63,000.
  • Forced selling. About $2.4 billion of leveraged crypto positions were liquidated in 24 hours, more than $1 billion of them in bitcoin and $545 million in ether, per CoinGlass. The largest single liquidation was a $51.7 million bitcoin long.
  • Altcoins fell harder. Ether lost about 10% to near $1,564; XRP, Solana and BNB each fell roughly 10%.
  • ETF money keeps leaving. US spot bitcoin ETFs saw $582 million of net outflows on November 19, $541 million of it from BlackRock's IBIT, per Farside data.

Analysts pointed to the same mix as on Wall Street: the Nasdaq's 5.3% drop on AI worries, higher real Treasury yields and bets on further Fed rate hikes.
