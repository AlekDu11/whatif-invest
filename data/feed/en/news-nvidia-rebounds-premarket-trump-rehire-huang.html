Nvidia shares jumped about 9% in premarket trading on Monday, November 23, after a 14% crash on Friday. The bounce came after President Trump wrote on Truth Social that he hopes Nvidia's board rehires Jensen Huang, who stepped down as CEO on November 12.

What happened

  • The post. Trump posted late on Sunday, around 02:54 UTC on Monday, saying Huang's return would be the best thing for the company and for the US. Prediction market Kalshi put the odds of a return at about 16%, up from under 10% a week earlier, per Yahoo Finance.
  • The move. Nvidia traded near $137 at 09:30 UTC, about 9% above Friday's close of $125.53. TECHi noted that roughly 44% of the gain had already happened in after-hours trading on Friday, before the post.
  • The whole AI trade bounced. Ten AI hardware stocks rose 7.4% on average before the open, including CoreWeave, Dell and TSMC; Nasdaq-100 futures were up almost 4%.
  • The probes have not gone away. Reuters reported on Friday that SEC staff are looking at Nvidia's accounting, and California's attorney general said his inquiry over chip sales to China could lead to a criminal case. The board said on November 17 it did not plan to reinstate Huang.

Our angle: a post is not a resolution

Nvidia trades at $155.38 −5.86% (−12.0% over 12 months), well below its record of $243.37 (October 2026). Key dates this week: Treasury Secretary Bessent's Wednesday deadline for answers on China chip sales, the PCE inflation report the same day and Nvidia's delayed quarterly filing, due by December 4.
