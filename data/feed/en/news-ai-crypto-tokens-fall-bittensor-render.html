Crypto's AI corner is having a bad week. Tokens tied to artificial intelligence fell sharply over seven days, with Virtuals Protocol down about 18% and Render and Bittensor each down about 12%, as doubts about the AI boom spread from stocks into crypto.

What happened

  • The losers. Over the week Virtuals Protocol dropped about 18% to roughly $0.43, Render 12.4% to $1.37, Bittensor (TAO) 12.3% to about $190 and NEAR 11% to $3.19, per CoinGecko data. Internet Computer lost 11.4% in a single day.
  • A shaky backdrop. Bitcoin was around $63,900 on November 17, about 15% below its level of November 9, and crypto has been moving with tech stocks.
  • Nvidia's leadership change. Jensen Huang stepped down as Nvidia's CEO on November 12, with CFO Colette Kress as interim chief — a jolt for the whole AI trade.
  • Money for Anthropic? Some crypto analysts think investors are selling coins to buy into Anthropic's Nasdaq listing, which could raise up to $64.4 billion. That is a theory, not a proven cause.
  • A few exceptions. Smaller AI tokens such as Pearl and Venice rose over the same week.
