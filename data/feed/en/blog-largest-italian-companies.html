The top 10 largest Italian companies by market cap

The ranking of the biggest companies listed on Euronext Milan (Borsa Italiana) by market value (share price × shares), from Yahoo Finance data in November 2026, among the Italian stocks we track. Live prices are on each company's page.

  1. UniCredit — Italy's largest bank by market value, also big in Germany, Austria and Central Europe. $83.42 +3.05%

How to buy Italian stocks

  • Euronext Milan (formerly Borsa Italiana's MTA) trades 9:00–17:30 Milan time on weekdays, with opening and closing auctions; prices are in euros.
  • The main index is the FTSE MIB (40 large companies); an FTSE MIB ETF buys them in one go, and European or eurozone funds hold the big names too.
  • Most brokers that offer European markets include Milan. Ferrari (RACE), Eni (E), Stellantis (STLA) and Tenaris (TS) are also listed in New York. Check what your broker offers and its currency fees.
  • See every Italian stock we track, sorted by market cap: Italy stocks.

What it means if you buy regularly

Regular buying in the biggest Italian companies more than doubled the money over five years — but almost all of that came from banks and defence, in a period of rising interest rates and military spending that may not repeat. Buying the famous brands every day barely broke even, and Stellantis lost most of the money. A broad FTSE MIB or European fund spreads that sector risk instead of betting on one theme.
